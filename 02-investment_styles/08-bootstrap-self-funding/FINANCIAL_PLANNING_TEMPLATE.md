## 12. Growth Investment Guidelines

### How Much Can You Spend on CAC?

**Rule of thumb for SaaS:**
```
Maximum CAC = (LTV / 5)

If LTV is $10,000 and margin is 70%:
Maximum CAC = $10,000 / 5 = $2,000 per customer
At 40% growth, spend up to this CAC
```

**Budget allocation by stage:**
| Stage | Product | CAC | Operations | Founder Salary |
|-------|---------|-----|-------------|----------------|
| Pre-revenue | 60% | 0% | 40% | $0 |
| Early revenue | 40% | 20% | 30% | $2K |
| Growth | 30% | 40% | 20% | $5K |
| Profitability | 20% | 30% | 20% | $10K+ |
