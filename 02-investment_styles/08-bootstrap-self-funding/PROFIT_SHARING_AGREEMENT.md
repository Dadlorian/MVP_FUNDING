## 3. Definition of "Profit"

### Net Profit Calculation

Net profit is calculated as:

```
Gross Revenue
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Operating Expenses (salaries, tools, etc.)
- Taxes and withholdings
- Debt service (loans, lines of credit)
- Emergency fund contributions (if any)
= NET PROFIT AVAILABLE FOR DISTRIBUTION
```

### Items NOT Included in Net Profit

The following are NOT considered when calculating profit:

- [ ] Founder salaries or cash compensation
- [ ] Equity issuances or vesting
- [ ] Reinvestment in product development (unless explicitly approved)
- [ ] One-time expenses (legal, accounting, etc.) - OR [ ] included with approval
- [ ] Revenue used for emergency fund (first [PLACEHOLDER] months only)

## Appendix A: Profit Calculation Example

### Example: Month X Profit Calculation

```
Total Revenue:                         $50,000
- Cost of Goods Sold:                 -$10,000
= Gross Profit:                        $40,000

Operating Expenses:
  Hosting:                            -$2,000
  Salaries (founders):               -$10,000
  Tools/Software:                     -$1,500
  Marketing:                          -$5,000
  Legal/Accounting:                   -$500
  Other:                              -$3,000
  Total Operating:                   -$22,000

Gross Profit minus Operating:          $18,000
- Taxes/Withholding:                  -$4,000
= NET PROFIT (Distributable):          $14,000

Profit Distribution:
  Participant A (50%):                $7,000
  Participant B (40%):                $5,600
  Company Reserves (10%):             $1,400
```
