## 2. What Is Sweat Equity?

**Definition:** Equity (ownership stake) granted in exchange for work/services, NOT cash investment.

**Key principle:** Participant is investing TIME and LABOR instead of money.

**Tax implications:** Sweat equity may be subject to income tax on vesting (80(b) election available).

## 5. Cliff Provisions

### What Is a Cliff?

A **cliff** is a vesting milestone where a larger chunk vests all at once. Most common: 1-year cliff (25%).

**Purpose:**
- Ensures commitment (participant must stay 1 year or forfeit all)
- Protects company from early departures

## 11. Tax Implications

### Sweat Equity Tax Treatment

**General Rule:** Sweat equity is taxable as ordinary income when vesting occurs.

**Exception: 83(b) Election**
- Participant elects to be taxed on grant date (not vesting dates)
- Must file within 30 days of grant
- Advantage: Long-term capital gains on appreciation
- Disadvantage: Taxed upfront even if company fails

### Should You File 83(b)?

**File 83(b) if:**
- [ ] Participant believes company will appreciate significantly
- [ ] Participant can afford upfront tax
- [ ] Company plans to go public or be acquired
- [ ] Strike price is very low relative to value

**Don't file 83(b) if:**
- [ ] Participant can't pay tax upfront
- [ ] Company has high failure risk
- [ ] Participant expects to leave soon
