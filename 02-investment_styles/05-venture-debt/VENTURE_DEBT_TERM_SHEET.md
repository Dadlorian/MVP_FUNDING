## 6. FINANCIAL COVENANTS

*These covenants are measured quarterly unless otherwise stated*

### 6.1 Minimum Revenue Covenant
**Requirement**: Quarterly revenue shall be no less than:
- Q[QUARTER_1]: $[Q1_REVENUE_MINIMUM]
- Q[QUARTER_2]: $[Q2_REVENUE_MINIMUM]
- Q[QUARTER_3]: $[Q3_REVENUE_MINIMUM]
- Q[QUARTER_4]: $[Q4_REVENUE_MINIMUM]
- Subsequent: [GROWTH_PERCENTAGE]% growth QoQ

**Definition**: Revenue means [DEFINITION_OF_REVENUE]
- Gross revenue from sales of products/services
- OR: Net of discounts/returns (depending on industry)
- Excludes: non-recurring items, intercompany transactions

**Covenant Holiday**: First 12 months, minimum revenue requirements waived (lender may extend if company demonstrates strong progress)

**Failure to Meet**:
- Does NOT trigger immediate default if other covenants satisfied
- May trigger covenant review with lender
- Lender may require enhanced reporting
- After [CURE_PERIOD_MONTHS] months, becomes hard default

---

### 6.2 Maximum Burn Rate Covenant
**Requirement**: Monthly cash burn shall not exceed:
- Year 1: $[YEAR_1_MAX_BURN]/month
- Year 2: $[YEAR_2_MAX_BURN]/month
- Year 3: $[YEAR_3_MAX_BURN]/month

**Definition**: Cash burn = [DEFINITION_OF_BURN]
- Operating expenses + capital expenditures
- Less: Non-recurring/one-time items
- Calculated as 12-month moving average or rolling quarter

**Exceptions**:
- Acquisition or major investment approved by lender
- Temporary spike if immediately followed by revenue increase

**Measurement**: Based on audited or reviewed financials, or lender's estimation

---

### 6.3 Minimum Cash Balance Covenant
**Requirement**: Borrower shall maintain minimum cash/liquid securities of:
- $[MINIMUM_CASH_BALANCE] at all times (or [DAYS_OF_OPERATING_EXPENSE] days of operating expense)

**Includes**: Cash in all depository accounts
**Excludes**: Restricted cash, escrows, customer deposits held in trust

**Purpose**: Ensures coverage of [COVERAGE_MONTHS] months of operating expenses + debt service

**Covenant Relief**: Amount reduced to $[REDUCED_MINIMUM] if:
- Raises Series B funding of $[SERIES_B_THRESHOLD]+, OR
- Achieves $[REVENUE_MILESTONE]/quarter revenue

---

### 6.4 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
**Requirement**:
- Trailing twelve months EBITDA ÷ Trailing twelve months debt service ≥ [DSCR_RATIO]x

**Measured**: Quarterly, on TTM basis

**Definition**:
- **EBITDA**: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization
  - Add back: Non-recurring items, stock compensation, one-time losses
  - Excluded: Acquisition-related charges (if lender approves)
- **Debt Service**: Total debt payments (principal + interest) on all company obligations

**Timing**: Only becomes applicable when company reaches $[EBITDA_POSITIVE_THRESHOLD] in annual revenues

---

### 6.5 Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio
**Requirement**: Outstanding loan balance ÷ Company Valuation < [LTV_RATIO]%

**Valuation basis**:
- Most recent funding round valuation, OR
- Annual 409A valuation, OR
- Lender's estimate (used if more conservative)

**Purpose**: Prevents overleveraging as company grows

## 9. EVENTS OF DEFAULT

Loan becomes immediately due and payable if:

### 9.1 Payment Default
- Any payment of principal or interest not made when due (plus [CURE_DAYS] day cure period)
- Any other financial obligation to lender not paid when due

### 9.2 Covenant Breach
- Material breach of any financial or negative covenant not cured within [CURE_PERIOD_DAYS] days of notice
- Breach of quarterly financial covenants if not cured within [FINANCIAL_CURE_DAYS] days
- Multiple covenant breaches (even if individually cured) within 12-month period

### 9.3 Misrepresentation
- Any representation or warranty proves materially false or misleading at any time
- Discovery of material adverse change in financial condition or business

### 9.4 Bankruptcy
- Borrower or guarantor files bankruptcy, receivership, or insolvency proceedings
- Borrower or guarantor becomes subject to involuntary bankruptcy proceedings
- Borrower makes assignment for benefit of creditors

### 9.5 Judgment
- Any judgment of $[JUDGMENT_THRESHOLD] or greater entered against Borrower not discharged within [DISCHARGE_DAYS] days
- Any material lien (other than permitted liens) attaches to Borrower's assets

### 9.6 Cross-Default
- Borrower defaults on any other debt obligation > $[CROSS_DEFAULT_THRESHOLD]
- Any guarantor defaults on obligations > $[GUARANTOR_DEFAULT_THRESHOLD]

### 9.7 Material Adverse Change
- Any event materially adversely affecting Borrower's business, assets, financial condition, or ability to repay
- Loss of key customers representing > [KEY_CUSTOMER_THRESHOLD]% of revenue
- Catastrophic loss, cyber attack, or regulatory action

### 9.8 Fraud or Misrepresentation
- Discovery that any material representation was fraudulent or materially misleading
- Undisclosed liabilities exceeding $[LIABILITY_THRESHOLD]

### 9.9 Control Change
- Change of control (as defined above) occurs without lender consent

## 10. REMEDIES UPON DEFAULT

Upon default, lender may:

### 10.1 Acceleration
- Declare entire unpaid balance immediately due and payable
- Accelerate all future interest (may use higher default interest rate)
- Pursue all available remedies simultaneously

### 10.2 Default Interest
- Increase interest rate to [DEFAULT_INTEREST_RATE]% (typically +[DEFAULT_SPREAD]% above contract rate)
- Continue charging default interest until default cured or loan repaid

### 10.3 Enforcement Against Borrower
- Exercise all remedies under loan documents
- Freeze company bank accounts and restrict transactions
- Take possession of any collateral or pledged assets
- Pursue collection actions
- File UCC financing statements
- Foreclose on any pledged assets

### 10.4 Enforcement Against Guarantors
- Pursue personal guarantees against founders
- Pursue founders' personal bank accounts and assets
- File suit against guarantors
- Pursue guarantors' credit
- No release of guarantors until loan satisfied

### 10.5 Warrant Exercise
- Automatically exercise or accelerate vesting of all warrants
- Exercise warrants on cashless basis
- Convert warrants to equity immediately

### 10.6 Costs and Expenses
- Borrower reimburses all of lender's costs including:
  - Attorneys' fees and legal expenses
  - Collection costs
  - Audit fees and accounting costs
  - UCC filing and court costs
  - Other reasonable costs
