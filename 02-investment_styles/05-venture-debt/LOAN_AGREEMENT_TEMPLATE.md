**DSCR = TTM EBITDA ÷ TTM Debt Service**

**(d) Failure Consequences**

- May trigger acceleration of Loan

## ARTICLE 4: DEFAULTS AND REMEDIES

### 4.1 Events of Default

Any one of the following constitutes an Event of Default ("Default"):

#### 4.1.1 Payment Default
- Any payment of principal or interest not made when due
- Payment default continues [CURE_DAYS] days after due date
- Any other monetary obligation not paid within [PAYMENT_CURE_DAYS] days of due date

#### 4.1.2 Covenant Defaults

**(a) Financial Covenant Breaches**
- Failure to meet minimum revenue covenant, uncured [FINANCIAL_COVENANT_CURE_DAYS] days
- Failure to meet maximum burn rate covenant
- Failure to maintain minimum cash balance
- Failure to maintain DSCR or LTV covenants

**(b) Reporting Covenant Breaches**
- Failure to deliver financial statements or board materials when due
- Failure to provide covenant compliance certification
- Failure to notify Lender of covenant breach timely

**(c) Operational Covenant Breaches**
- Incurrence of unauthorized debt or guarantees
- Acquisition, merger, or change of control without consent
- Related-party transaction without consent
- Material violation of Section 2.2 covenants

#### 4.1.3 Misrepresentation Default
- Discovery that any representation or warranty is materially false or misleading
- Omission of material information in any submission to Lender
- Misrepresentation regarding cap table, material contracts, or IP

#### 4.1.4 Cross-Default
- Default under any other debt obligation > $[CROSS_DEFAULT_THRESHOLD]
- Default on equipment financing, credit card, or other loan
- Default by Guarantor on personal obligations > $[GUARANTOR_DEFAULT_THRESHOLD]

#### 4.1.5 Bankruptcy Default
- Borrower files for bankruptcy, receivership, or insolvency
- Borrower becomes subject to involuntary bankruptcy proceedings
- Borrower makes assignment for benefit of creditors
- Trustee appointed over Borrower's assets
- Borrower unable to pay obligations as they come due
- Borrower ceases business operations

#### 4.1.6 Judgment Default
- Money judgment of $[JUDGMENT_THRESHOLD] or more entered against Borrower
- Judgment not discharged, bonded, or stayed within [JUDGMENT_CURE_DAYS] days
- Judgment creditor levies on Borrower's assets
- Lien attaches to Borrower's material assets

#### 4.1.7 Material Adverse Change
- Event materially adversely affecting Borrower's business, assets, or ability to repay
- Loss of material customer (>25% of revenue) without replacement
- Departure of CEO/key personnel without approved replacement
- Catastrophic loss or business interruption
- Cyber attack, data breach, or ransomware causing significant damage
- Regulatory action or permit revocation affecting business
- Discovery of material IP infringement or IP liability

#### 4.1.8 Control Change Default
- Change of control occurs without Lender's consent (Section 2.2.6)
- Founder/key person ceases to be involved in business
- Board majority changes without notice

#### 4.1.9 Fraud or Criminal Default
- Discovery of fraud, misrepresentation, or criminal conduct by founders/management
- Undisclosed liabilities > $[LIABILITY_THRESHOLD]
- Use of Loan proceeds for prohibited purposes
- Loan proceeds used to violate law or for illegal purpose

#### 4.1.10 Regulatory and Compliance Default
- Material violation of law or regulation discovered
- Regulatory enforcement action
- Loss of required license or permit
- Non-compliance with financial reporting regulations
- Violation of data privacy or cybersecurity laws

### 4.2 Consequences of Default

Upon Default (including any breach of covenant, whether or not Default declaration made), Lender may at its option:

#### 4.2.1 Acceleration of Loan
- Declare entire unpaid balance immediately due and payable
- Acceleration occurs automatically upon bankruptcy Default
- Applies to all future interest payments as well
- Borrower liable for all accrued but unpaid interest to date of repayment

#### 4.2.2 Default Interest Rate
