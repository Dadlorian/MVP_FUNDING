## EMAIL WRITING BEST PRACTICES

### Subject Line Tips
✓ **Be specific:** "[COMPANY] raised $500K from 247 investors" (better than "Campaign Update")
✓ **Use numbers:** "3 customers signed up this week" (better than "Progress update")
✓ **Create curiosity:** "Something unexpected happened during the campaign..." (but deliver on the promise)
✓ **Urgency when real:** "Campaign closes in 3 days—here's why you should invest" (not artificial urgency)

### Body Copy Tips
✓ **Lead with the most important news** - Put headline news in the first paragraph
✓ **Use short paragraphs** - 2-3 sentences max (easier to scan on mobile)
✓ **Highlight key metrics** - Use bold for numbers
✓ **Be honest about challenges** - Transparency builds trust
✓ **End with clear CTA** - "Invest here" or "Email me with questions"
✓ **Be personal** - Sign with your name, not "The Team"
✓ **Proof-read carefully** - Typos undermine credibility

### Frequency Guidelines
| Communication | Frequency | Best Day/Time |
|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| During campaign | 2-3/week | Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am |
| Monthly update | 1/month | First Monday of month, 9am |
| Quarterly webinar | 4/year | Midweek, 12pm or 6pm |
| Annual report | 1/year | Within 120 days of fiscal year-end |
| Crisis updates | As needed | ASAP - don't wait |
| Milestone news | As happens | Immediately after achieving |
| Team announcements | As happens | When person officially starts |

### What NOT to Do
✗ **Don't over-promise returns** - "Your investment will 10x" (that's illegal and unrealistic)
✗ **Don't hide bad news** - Investors will find out; transparency is better
✗ **Don't miss deadlines** - Form C/A filing is legally required within 120 days of fiscal year-end
✗ **Don't send generic emails** - Each investor has a name; use it
✗ **Don't go dark** - Silence makes investors nervous; keep them updated
✗ **Don't ask investors to sign NDAs** - They're equity holders, not confidants
✗ **Don't spam with daily updates** - Frequency fatigue is real

## COMPLIANCE NOTES

### Important Legal Reminders

**1. No False or Misleading Statements**
- Don't promise returns
- Don't make guarantees about equity value
- Don't misrepresent financial metrics
- Do be honest about risks and challenges

**2. Same Information for All Investors**
- Don't give different information to different investors
- All investors should see same cap table, financials, metrics
- All investors get same communication on same timeline

**3. Document Everything**
- Keep copies of all investor communications
- Save all responses to investor questions
- Track who received what communication and when
- This is your protection if issues arise later

**4. Required Annual Communications (Reg CF)**
- Form C/A filing within 120 days of fiscal year-end
- Send copy of Form C/A to all investors
- Include financial statements
- Be honest about company status

**5. Investor Accreditation**
- Verify investor accreditation before accepting investment (if applicable)
- Platform usually handles this
- Document verification for your records
