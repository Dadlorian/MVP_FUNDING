## SECTION G: RISK FACTORS (Required - Be Comprehensive)

### G.1 Business & Market Risks

**Risk 1: Early-stage company with limited operating history**
- We have been operating for [X] years
- Limited financial history and no profitable operations to date
- Past performance does not guarantee future success
- Significant capital requirements to scale operations

**Risk 2: Intense competition**
- Market is highly competitive with both direct and indirect competitors
- Competitors may have more resources, larger customer bases, or better technology
- We may not be able to compete effectively
- Price competition may reduce profitability

**Risk 3: Dependence on key personnel**
- Success depends heavily on [Founder Name] (CEO/CTO)
- Loss of key founders could materially harm business
- We have key person insurance of $[X] (if applicable)
- Recruitment and retention of technical talent is challenging

**Risk 4: Product/market fit uncertainties**
- While we have [X] customers, market adoption could slow
- Customer needs may change faster than we can adapt
- We may not achieve anticipated growth rates
- Product roadmap changes may upset existing customers

**Risk 5: Capital requirements**
- We will need additional capital in [X] months
- Future fundraising may be dilutive to existing shareholders
- May not be available on favorable terms
- Could force us to pursue less favorable strategic alternatives

### G.2 Regulatory & Legal Risks

**Risk 6: Regulatory compliance**
- Operate in regulated industry [IF APPLICABLE: healthcare, fintech, etc.]
- New regulations could increase costs or limit operations
- Compliance requires significant legal and accounting resources
- Failure to comply could result in fines or operational restrictions

**Risk 7: Intellectual property risks**
- Our success depends on protecting our IP (patents, trade secrets, brand)
- We may face patent infringement claims from competitors
- Trade secret protection is uncertain
- IP litigation is expensive

**Risk 8: Data privacy & security**
- We collect customer data [DESCRIBE TYPE]
- Data breaches could expose us to liability
- Privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) create compliance burdens
- Security failures could damage reputation and business

### G.3 Financial & Investment Risks

**Risk 9: Dilution to existing shareholders**
- Your ownership will be diluted by future fundraising
- Each new funding round increases shares outstanding
- Anti-dilution protections are limited in Reg CF
- Your voting power and economic interests will decrease

**Risk 10: No liquidity or exit timeline**
- Equity crowdfunding shares are illiquid
- No guaranteed secondary market
- You may not see return for 5-10+ years (if ever)
- Company may never achieve profitable exit
- Failure rate for startups is high

**Risk 11: Limited information rights**
- As small shareholders, you have limited ability to compel information
- No board seat or strategic influence
- Reliant on management's voluntary disclosures
- Information asymmetry risk

### G.4 Platform & Administrative Risks

**Risk 12: Platform dependence**
- Offering conducted through [PLATFORM_NAME] intermediary
- Platform closure would affect share administration
- Platform policies could change
- Our ability to communicate with investors limited by platform

**Risk 13: Shareholder management complexity**
- Hundreds of small shareholders create operational challenge
- Hosting investor updates, answering questions requires resources
- Cap table management becomes complex
- Future financing rounds may be complicated

### Glossary
- **Form C:** SEC notification form required for Reg CF offerings
- **Accredited Investor:** Individual with $200K+ income ($300K+ joint) or $1M+ net worth
- **Holding Period:** [1 YEAR minimum for Reg CF shares]
- **Reg CF:** SEC regulation allowing crowdfunding offerings up to $1.07M annually

**Risk of Total Loss:** Investment in early-stage companies involves substantial risk. Investors should be prepared to lose their entire investment. Only invest what you can afford to lose.

**No Secondary Market:** These shares are not traded on any exchange. Investors should expect to hold shares for many years with no guarantee of liquidity.

**Reliance on Management:** Investors rely on management's representations. There is no guarantee of accuracy or truthfulness beyond SEC liability protections.
