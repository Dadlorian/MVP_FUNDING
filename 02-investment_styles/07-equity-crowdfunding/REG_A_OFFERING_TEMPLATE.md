# Reg A+ Offering Template (Tier 2)

**SEC Form 1-A - Regulation A+ Offering Statement**

---

## IMPORTANT: TIER 2 COMPLEXITY

This template is for **Reg A+ Tier 2** offerings (up to $75M). Tier 2 requires:
- Audited or reviewed financial statements
- Extensive SEC disclosure
- 4-6 month qualification timeline
- Higher compliance cost ($30K-$75K)
- More sophisticated investor base
- Potential for secondary market trading

**For Tier 1 offerings ($20M max), many sections can be simplified.**
