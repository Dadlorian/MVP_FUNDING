## PLATFORM 1: WEFUNDER

### Overview
Wefunder is the largest equity crowdfunding platform (by investor count and capital raised). Founded by David Tisch in 2012, it specializes in technology and innovation companies.

**Key Stats:**
- Investors: 600K+ (highly engaged, early-adopter demographic)
- Total raised: $2.5B+
- Campaigns successful: 60%+ (highly curated/selective)
- Average raise: $500K-$1M
- Fee: 7.5%

### Investor Demographics
- **Age:** 25-45 (skew younger)
- **Location:** Silicon Valley, NYC, LA, other tech hubs
- **Income:** $50K-$200K+ (tech workers, entrepreneurs)
- **Investment style:** Early-stage tech bets, innovation-focused
- **Motivation:** Support founders, get in early on hot companies
- **Portfolio approach:** Serial investors (10+ investments each)

**Investor Profile:** Tech-savvy early adopters who understand startup risk and are building innovation portfolios.

### Platform Features

**Strengths:**
✓ **Elite curated campaigns** - Selective (rejection rate ~40%) = higher quality companies
✓ **Strong investor community** - Highly engaged, founder-friendly
✓ **Exceptional UX/design** - Easiest platform to use
✓ **Media coverage** - Best press machine (press mentions = momentum)
✓ **Acquisition by AngelList** - Combined with Raise platform (potential liquidity solutions)
✓ **Founder coaching** - Dedicated launch coach for each campaign
✓ **Best-in-class campaign optimization** - A/B testing, design iteration
✓ **Secondary market potential** - AngelList working on liquidity solutions

**Weaknesses:**
✗ **Highly competitive** - Only 60% of applicants approved to campaign
✗ **Audience concentration** - Mostly tech/biotech (harder for other sectors)
✗ **Duration pressure** - Fast campaigns (45 days) = must have momentum
✗ **Platform saturation** - Many campaigns competing for attention
✗ **Fees on higher end** - 7.5% is toward higher end
✗ **Smaller check sizes** - Average investment $1,500-$2,500
✗ **Limited Reg A+ support** - Primarily Reg CF platform

### Best For
- **Tech startups** - AI, SaaS, fintech, consumer apps
- **Biotech/medtech companies** - Life sciences innovation
- **Early-stage founders** - Pre-revenue or early revenue
- **Founders with strong narratives** - Compelling founder story is crucial
- **Companies seeking media buzz** - PR is part of Wefunder's value
- **Founders who want investor coaching** - Dedicated account managers

### Not Ideal For
- **B2B enterprise software** - Audience doesn't validate enterprise deals
- **Brick-and-mortar businesses** - Not tech-forward enough
- **Founders without strong presentation** - Audience is critical/sophisticated
- **Slow-moving campaigns** - Momentum culture (you need traction)

### Campaign Timeline
- **Application to approval:** 2-4 weeks
- **Campaign duration:** 45 days (fixed)
- **Post-campaign to funding:** 2-3 weeks (money transfer)
- **Total timeline:** 10-12 weeks

### Key Contacts & Support
- **Dedicated Launch Coach:** Assigned to each campaign
- **Investor Relations:** investors@wefunder.com
- **Founder Support:** Full onboarding + weekly check-ins during campaign
- **Best-in-class:** Wefunder historically has best founder experience

### Success Factors
**To win on Wefunder:**
1. **Founder credibility** - Wefunder investors bet on founders first
2. **Product story** - Compelling narrative of problem + solution
3. **Early traction** - Users, revenue, or strong signals of demand
4. **Visual/video quality** - Wefunder audience responds to production value
5. **Engagement** - Active founder during campaign (daily responses, webinars)
6. **Community building** - First 20% of raise must come from existing community
7. **Media strategy** - Plan for press coverage drives momentum

### Real Example Campaign

**Company:** Literal (fictional example matching Wefunder typical)
- **Sector:** AI-powered legal assistant
- **Raise:** $650,000
- **Valuation:** $10M pre-money
- **Timeline:** Launched Month 1, funded by Week 6
- **Investor count:** 843
- **Avg investment:** $771
- **Success factors:** Founder was ex-Google, product demo was slick, founder hosted 3 live webinars

## PLATFORM 2: REPUBLIC

### Overview
Republic is the most founder-friendly platform (in terms of terms). Founded by Kendrick Nguyen in 2016, it specializes in consumer brands, creative companies, and social impact ventures.

**Key Stats:**
- Investors: 1M+ (largest investor base)
- Total raised: $1B+
- Campaigns successful: 50%+ (less selective, more democratic)
- Average raise: $400K-$750K
- Fee: 7-10% (variable based on campaign type)

### Investor Demographics
- **Age:** 20-60 (broad demographic)
- **Location:** Distributed (not Bay Area focused)
- **Income:** $30K-$150K+ (more diverse)
- **Investment style:** Consumer brands, social impact, mission-driven companies
- **Motivation:** Use/support products they love, impact investing, community
- **Portfolio approach:** Mix of casual investors and serial investors

**Investor Profile:** Diverse, purpose-driven, interested in supporting companies with social impact or innovative products.

### Platform Features

**Strengths:**
✓ **Most founders welcome terms** - Best investor protections and founder-friendly structures
✓ **Largest investor base** - 1M+ investors = more potential customers
✓ **Strong community** - Very engaged, supportive community
✓ **Diverse sectors welcome** - Not just tech (consumer, social impact, etc.)
✓ **Flexible campaign structure** - Can optimize for your business
✓ **Good press support** - Medium-level PR support (less than Wefunder, more than StartEngine)
✓ **Secondary market potential** - Republic platform exploring liquidity solutions
✓ **International capability** - Can accept some international investors

**Weaknesses:**
✗ **Lower success rate** - 50% success (more campaigns fail)
✗ **Slower momentum** - Less "hype" culture than Wefunder
✗ **Smaller average checks** - Investors typically $1K-$1,500
✗ **Less polished platform** - UX not quite as good as Wefunder
✗ **Unpredictable timing** - Campaign duration varies (45-90 days)
✗ **Fewer institutional features** - Less corporate-ready than StartEngine
✗ **Limited Reg A+ option** - Primarily Reg CF

### Best For
- **Consumer brands** - Food, beverage, fashion, beauty
- **Social impact ventures** - Environmental, social, health companies
- **Creative companies** - Art, media, entertainment
- **Community-driven products** - Where customers become investors
- **Founder-friendly terms** - Looking for investor-protective structures
- **Companies with strong mission** - Beyond profit motive
- **International founders** - Some international capabilities

### Not Ideal For
- **Enterprise B2B software** - Wrong audience match
- **Highly technical products** - Investors want usable product
- **Stealth mode** - Requires public narrative
- **Pure play finance plays** - Investors want tangible products

### Campaign Timeline
- **Application to approval:** 2-3 weeks
- **Campaign duration:** 45-90 days (variable/flexible)
- **Post-campaign to funding:** 2-3 weeks
- **Total timeline:** 9-16 weeks (more variable)

### Key Contacts & Support
- **Founder Support:** launch@republic.co
- **Community:** Active Slack community for founders
- **Best feature:** Thoughtful founder onboarding and education

### Success Factors
**To win on Republic:**
1. **Mission clarity** - Why does this company matter beyond profit?
2. **Community fit** - Does audience naturally exist on Republic?
3. **Product quality** - Republic investors want usable, delightful products
4. **Founder authenticity** - Being real/vulnerable resonates
5. **Customer stories** - Testimonials and user enthusiasm matter most
6. **Visual identity** - Strong branding and aesthetic
7. **Community engagement** - Active founder during campaign (best factor)

### Real Example Campaign

**Company:** Good Earth (fictional example matching Republic typical)
- **Sector:** Sustainable fashion brand
- **Raise:** $500,000
- **Valuation:** $4M pre-money
- **Timeline:** Launched Month 1, funded by Week 8
- **Investor count:** 1,247
- **Avg investment:** $401
- **Success factors:** Strong mission alignment, existing customer base, founder shared personal story, authentic communication

## PLATFORM 3: STARTENGINE

### Overview
StartEngine is the most mature/corporate of the three platforms. Founded in 2012, it specializes in real estate, SaaS, and established startups (pre-Series A).

**Key Stats:**
- Investors: 500K+
- Total raised: $800M+
- Campaigns successful: 40%+ (all campaigns are listed, not curated down)
- Average raise: $300K-$600K
- Fee: 6.75% (lowest fee)

### Investor Demographics
- **Age:** 30-65 (slightly older demographic)
- **Location:** Distributed, more conservative regions
- **Income:** $50K-$200K+ (modest to upper-middle class)
- **Investment style:** Real estate, SaaS, established companies
- **Motivation:** Financial return (less mission-driven than Republic)
- **Portfolio approach:** Mix of casual and serial investors

**Investor Profile:** Slightly more traditional, return-focused, interested in less risky early-stage bets (SAFE as equity, established traction).

### Platform Features

**Strengths:**
✓ **Lowest fee** - 6.75% (most founder-friendly on fees)
✓ **Secondary market (Alts)** - StartEngine Alts allows share trading (rare feature)
✓ **Corporate-friendly features** - Better for more established companies
✓ **Longest campaign durations** - Up to 120 days (more time to momentum)
✓ **Less competitive** - Higher approval rate (more campaigns listed)
✓ **Educational resources** - Best learning library for founders
✓ **Real estate focus** - Strong in real estate syndication space
✓ **Regulatory expertise** - Best at handling complex structures (Reg A+)

**Weaknesses:**
✗ **Lower success rate** - 40%+ (many campaigns fail)
✗ **Less curated** - All applications approved (less prestige)
✗ **Smaller community feel** - Less founder-focused culture
✗ **Slower platforms** - UX/performance sometimes lags
✗ **Quieter PR machine** - Less media coverage for campaigns
✗ **Older investor base** - Less tech-savvy audience
✗ **Lower check sizes** - Average investment $800-$1,200
✗ **Secondary market complexity** - Share trading has friction

### Best For
- **Real estate syndications** - Primary market for their business
- **SaaS with traction** - Established SaaS companies
- **Companies with existing customers** - Prefer revenue over venture bets
- **Founders seeking secondary market** - Only platform with active trading
- **Longer campaign timelines** - Up to 120 days allowed
- **Founder who wants education** - Best learning resources
- **Cost-conscious founders** - Lowest fees at 6.75%

### Not Ideal For
- **Pre-revenue startups** - Audience prefers some traction
- **High-growth venture plays** - Less venture capital audience
- **Brand-focused launches** - Less media hype culture
- **First-time founders** - Platform less founder-hand-holding

### Campaign Timeline
- **Application to approval:** 3-5 weeks
- **Campaign duration:** 45-120 days (flexible)
- **Post-campaign to funding:** 2-3 weeks
- **Total timeline:** 10-17 weeks (longest of three)

### Key Contacts & Support
- **Founder Support:** hello@startengine.com
- **Education:** Best-in-class learning library and resources
- **Community:** Quarterly founder webinars

### Success Factors
**To win on StartEngine:**
1. **Traction evidence** - Revenue, customers, or strong metrics matter most
2. **Mature positioning** - Professional presentation > flashy startup narrative
3. **Financial clarity** - Clear unit economics and path to profitability
4. **Professional team** - Founder + team credibility emphasized
5. **Long campaign strategy** - Build momentum over 60-90 days (not 45)
6. **Investor education** - Clear explanations for less venture-savvy investors
7. **Legal clarity** - Properly structured securities appreciated

### Real Example Campaign

**Company:** SaaSToolCo (fictional example matching StartEngine typical)
- **Sector:** B2B SaaS platform for content management
- **Raise:** $450,000

## SPECIAL FEATURE DEEP DIVES

### Secondary Markets (for Liquidity)

**StartEngine Alts (Active Secondary Market):**
- Allows investors to buy/sell shares between each other
- Trading happens on platform with bid/ask pricing
- Provides some liquidity (though less than public markets)
- Best for: Companies with 2+ years post-campaign operations
- Fee: 8% on both sides (buyer and seller)
- Outcome: Some exits or partial liquidity possible

**Republic Secondary Market (In Development):**
- Republic exploring secondary market for campaigns
- Not yet active at time of writing
- Expected: Launch within 12 months
- Would provide liquidity path for earlier investors

**Wefunder Secondary Market (In Development):**
- Combined with AngelList, exploring secondary market
- AngelList has existing secondary market infrastructure
- Expected: Integration could provide some liquidity options
- Would be integrated into Raise platform

**Net impact:** StartEngine has only active secondary market currently (advantage for founders seeking liquidity story)

### Reg A+ Capability

**StartEngine:** Full Reg A+ support
- Can raise up to $75M (Tier 2)
- Experienced regulatory team
- Best platform for Reg A+ offerings
- 4-6 month timeline for qualification
- Higher costs ($30K-$75K for legal/compliance)

**Republic:** Limited Reg A+ support
- Can raise up to $75M (Tier 2)
- Some experience with Reg A+
- Not primary focus
- Fewer completed Reg A+ offerings

**Wefunder:** No Reg A+ support
- Reg CF only (up to $1.07M annually)
- No plans to support Reg A+
- Pure Reg CF platform

**Implication:** For founders seeking $5M+ raises, StartEngine or Republic (with RepublicSecurities) are better choices

## FAQ: PLATFORM SELECTION

### Q: Can I run campaigns on multiple platforms simultaneously?
**A:** No. You must choose one platform. Running on multiple at once would confuse investors and violate platform terms. Focus your efforts on one platform.

### Q: What if I apply to Wefunder and get rejected?
**A:** You can:
1. Reapply to Wefunder (address feedback)
2. Apply to Republic (different acceptance criteria)
3. Apply to StartEngine (highest acceptance rate)

Don't take rejection personally. Different platforms = different audiences. Move to next platform.

### Q: Which platform is "best" for raising the most money?
**A:** Wefunder (60% success rate, higher checks) and Republic (larger investor base) tend to raise the most. StartEngine is third.

But "best" depends on YOUR company fit (not platform quality).

### Q: Can I move from one platform to another after starting?
**A:** No. Once you start a campaign, you're committed. Switching platforms mid-campaign would damage credibility.

### Q: Which platform has the best investor experience?
**A:** Republic (by most accounts). Most founder-friendly terms, best community, most supportive.

Wefunder has best tech/UX. StartEngine has best education.

### Q: Do platforms provide ongoing support after campaign closes?
**A:** Yes:
- **Wefunder:** Minimal post-campaign support (expects you to manage investors)
- **Republic:** Good ongoing support and community
- **StartEngine:** Good ongoing education and resources
