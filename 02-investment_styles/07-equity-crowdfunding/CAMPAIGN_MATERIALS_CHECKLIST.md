# Equity Crowdfunding Campaign Materials Checklist

**Comprehensive pre-launch and launch checklist for equity crowdfunding campaigns (Reg CF & Reg A+)**

---

## PRE-CAMPAIGN PHASE (8-12 Weeks Before Launch)

### Legal & Compliance Foundation

#### Entity & Documentation
- [ ] Legal entity established (C-Corp or LLC, depending on strategy)
- [ ] Certificate of Incorporation/Articles of Organization filed
- [ ] Bylaws/Operating Agreement drafted and adopted
- [ ] Board resolutions authorizing offering
- [ ] Cap table documented and verified (100% accuracy required)
- [ ] Equity grants/option agreements reviewed
- [ ] Stock ledger updated
- [ ] 409A valuation completed (if offering at higher valuation)

#### Financial & Accounting
- [ ] CPA retained for financial statement preparation
- [ ] Financial statements prepared (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow)
- [ ] For Reg CF (if >$107K raised): Reviewed or audited financials
- [ ] For Reg A+: Audited or reviewed financials required
- [ ] Tax returns prepared and filed
- [ ] Bookkeeping system in place (QuickBooks, Xero, etc.)
- [ ] Monthly accounting reconciliation process documented
- [ ] Management discussion & analysis (MD&A) drafted

#### Legal Documents
- [ ] Standard form of investor agreement prepared
- [ ] Anti-dilution provisions defined
- [ ] Liquidation preference terms set
- [ ] Information rights documented
- [ ] Shareholder agreement drafted (if applicable)
- [ ] Terms of use for investor portal drafted
- [ ] Investor questionnaire for accreditation verification prepared

#### Compliance Checklist
- [ ] SEC rule compliance verified (Reg CF vs. Reg A+)
- [ ] State blue sky laws reviewed
- [ ] Anti-money laundering (AML) policy drafted
- [ ] Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures established
- [ ] Investor suitability requirements defined
- [ ] Escrow account established with qualified escrow agent

### Business Foundation

#### Company Information
- [ ] Legal business name finalized
- [ ] Company website domain finalized and live
- [ ] Company email addresses created
- [ ] Phone system in place
- [ ] Mailing address confirmed
- [ ] EIN obtained
- [ ] State business registration current

#### Offering Structure
- [ ] Security type defined (Common Stock standard for Reg CF)
- [ ] Share price determined and justified
- [ ] Total shares offered calculated
- [ ] Fully diluted cap table post-offering calculated
- [ ] Valuation methodology documented (comparable companies, DCF, etc.)
- [ ] Equity vs. debt vs. hybrid decision made
- [ ] Convertible note/SAFE vs. direct equity decision made

#### Platform Selection
- [ ] Chosen platform(s): [Platform name]
  - Wefunder: [ ]
  - Republic: [ ]
  - StartEngine: [ ]
  - [Other]: [ ]
- [ ] Platform eligibility verified
- [ ] Platform fee structure reviewed (typically 6.75-10%)
- [ ] Platform data requirements understood
- [ ] Dedicated launch coach/account manager assigned

### Content & Messaging Development

#### Brand & Positioning
- [ ] Brand positioning statement finalized
- [ ] Company mission/vision clarified
- [ ] Value proposition defined in 1-2 sentences
- [ ] Key messaging pillars identified (3-5 main points)
- [ ] Founder story/narrative written
- [ ] Problem statement clearly articulated
- [ ] Solution differentiation outlined

#### Content Assets (Draft Versions)
- [ ] Company overview document (500 words)
- [ ] Problem/solution narrative (1000 words)
- [ ] Market opportunity analysis (draft)
- [ ] Competitive positioning document (draft)
- [ ] Use of proceeds breakdown (detailed)
- [ ] Financial projections drafted (conservative + optimistic)
- [ ] Risk factors list compiled (10-15 initial)
- [ ] FAQ document started (30+ questions minimum)
- [ ] Pitch deck outline created

#### Visual & Media Assets (Planning)
- [ ] Brand colors/fonts defined
- [ ] Logo finalized
- [ ] Founder high-resolution photos selected
- [ ] Company office/team photos planned
- [ ] Product screenshots/mockups gathered
- [ ] Graphic design style guide drafted
- [ ] Video production timeline set

## OFFERING DOCUMENT PHASE (6-8 Weeks Before Launch)

### Form C / Form 1-A Preparation

#### Reg CF (Form C)
- [ ] Company information section completed
- [ ] Offering details finalized (amount, timeline, terms)
- [ ] Business description written (500-1000 words)
- [ ] Use of proceeds detailed with specific percentages
- [ ] Current capitalization table compiled
- [ ] Post-offering pro-forma cap table calculated
- [ ] Officer/director information compiled
- [ ] Management team bios written (500+ words each)
- [ ] Financial statements attached (audited if >$107K)
- [ ] Risk factors list finalized (20+ specific risks)
- [ ] Compensation disclosures completed
- [ ] Related party transactions disclosed
- [ ] Exhibits attached (cap table, certifications, etc.)

#### Reg A+ (Form 1-A)
- [ ] All Form C sections completed (above)
- [ ] Executive summary written (500+ words)
- [ ] Business overview expanded (1500+ words)
- [ ] Management discussion & analysis (MD&A) completed
- [ ] Audited financial statements prepared
- [ ] Dilution analysis included
- [ ] Offering timeline specified
- [ ] Minimum/maximum offering amounts set
- [ ] Material contracts attached as exhibits
- [ ] Officer certifications signed

#### Legal Review
- [ ] Securities attorney (licensed in your state) retained
- [ ] Form C/1-A reviewed by counsel
- [ ] Disclosure completeness verified
- [ ] Risk factors reviewed for adequacy
- [ ] Use of proceeds validated
- [ ] Anti-fraud disclosures reviewed
- [ ] State-specific blue sky compliance verified
- [ ] All legal language approved
- [ ] Final document signed and certified

#### Platform Submission
- [ ] Form C/1-A uploaded to platform
- [ ] Supporting documents uploaded (financials, cap table, etc.)
- [ ] Platform questionnaire completed
- [ ] Founder verification documents submitted (ID, address proof, etc.)
- [ ] Compliance review initiated with platform
- [ ] Platform feedback incorporated
- [ ] Approval received to proceed to launch phase

## LAUNCH DAY & CAMPAIGN EXECUTION (Weeks 1-8)

### Launch Day Execution (24 Hours)

#### 6 Hours Before Launch
- [ ] Final systems check (website, email, platform, analytics)
- [ ] Team briefing meeting held
- [ ] All team members at stations with communication channels open
- [ ] Phone lines monitored
- [ ] Email inbox monitored
- [ ] Analytics dashboard setup and live
- [ ] Platform refresh to confirm campaign is live

#### Launch Hour (T-0)
- [ ] Email campaign sent to full subscriber list
  - [ ] Subject line tested for spam filters
  - [ ] Preview text optimized
  - [ ] Campaign link verified
  - [ ] Timing optimized (typically 10-11am EST)
- [ ] Social media posts published across all channels
  - [ ] Instagram post + stories + reels
  - [ ] LinkedIn post from founder + company account
  - [ ] Twitter/X threads and regular posts
  - [ ] Facebook post
  - [ ] TikTok (if applicable)
- [ ] Press release distributed via wire service
- [ ] Paid ads launched
  - [ ] Facebook ads active
  - [ ] LinkedIn ads active
  - [ ] Google ads active
- [ ] Company website updated with campaign banner/link
- [ ] Team ready for investor inquiries

#### Hours 1-6 Post-Launch
- [ ] Monitor traffic, clicks, conversion metrics in real-time
- [ ] Respond to investor inquiries within 1-2 hours
- [ ] Engage with social media comments and messages
- [ ] First funding milestone monitor (track early traction)
- [ ] Technical issues addressed immediately
- [ ] Team energy maintained and morale high
- [ ] Update internal tracking spreadsheet

#### Day 1 Evening
- [ ] Day 1 metrics compiled and reviewed
  - [ ] Website visits: [#]
  - [ ] Campaign views: [#]

### Week 1 Campaign Activities

#### Daily Activities (Every Day)
- [ ] Monitor investor inquiries and respond within 4 hours
- [ ] Publish 3-5 social media posts (mix of platforms)
- [ ] Review analytics dashboard (traffic, conversion, spend efficiency)
- [ ] Adjust paid ads based on performance data
- [ ] Flag and resolve any technical issues immediately
- [ ] Track funding progress vs. pacing goal
- [ ] Compile daily metrics

#### Weekly Activities (First Full Week)
- [ ] Host founder AMA/live Q&A session on platform (if available)
- [ ] Publish first investor update email
- [ ] Publish blog post or Medium article about campaign
- [ ] Host podcast/media interview (if scheduled pre-launch)
- [ ] Pitch media outlets for coverage (follow-up on initial pitches)
- [ ] Engagement metrics review (impressions, clicks, conversion rate)
- [ ] Investor feedback compilation (common questions, concerns)
- [ ] Paid ad performance review and optimization
- [ ] Cap table and administrative setup

### Weeks 2-8 Campaign Momentum

#### Content & Communication Schedule
- [ ] 2 investor update emails per week (Tuesday/Thursday)
- [ ] 5-7 social media posts per day (across all platforms)
- [ ] 2-3 founder/team member posts or interviews per week
- [ ] Customer testimonial content (2 per week)
- [ ] Impact/use of proceeds updates (1 per week)
- [ ] Weekly blog post or published article
- [ ] Monthly webinar or live Q&A session

#### Investor Engagement
- [ ] Respond to all inquiries within 4 hours (24-hour online hours)
- [ ] Personal emails from founder to significant investors
- [ ] One-on-one founder calls for large prospective investors
- [ ] Investor referral incentive program (ask existing investors to invite others)
- [ ] Milestone announcements (at X% funded, etc.)
- [ ] Press coverage shared with investors and social media
- [ ] Customer success stories featured and shared

#### Campaign Momentum Management
- [ ] Maintain funding pace (target: 2-3% of goal funded per day)
- [ ] Address any momentum slowdowns immediately
  - [ ] Flash sale/limited-time bonus offer
  - [ ] Media push (press outreach, podcast blitz)
  - [ ] Investor referral bonus increase
  - [ ] Major milestone celebration
- [ ] Adjust messaging/positioning if needed based on investor feedback
- [ ] Paid advertising budget optimization
  - [ ] Reallocate spend to highest-performing channels
  - [ ] Pause underperforming ads
  - [ ] Test new creative/copy variations
  - [ ] Increase budgets on winners
