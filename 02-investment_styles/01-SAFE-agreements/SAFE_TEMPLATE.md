## 3. CONVERSION TERMS

### 3.1 Automatic Conversion
This SAFE shall automatically convert into [NUMBER_OF_SHARES OR PERCENTAGE] of Series [SERIES_LETTER] Preferred Stock (or equivalent security issued by the Company) at the same price per share paid by other investors in such financing round (the "**Next Equity Financing**"), on the following conditions:

**(a) Qualifying Equity Financing** means any bona fide sale of Series [SERIES_LETTER] or other series of Preferred Stock of the Company following the execution of this Agreement, resulting in gross proceeds to the Company of not less than $[MINIMUM_PROCEEDS] USD (the "**Financing Threshold**").

**(b) Conversion Mechanics:**
- **Conversion Price** = Amount Invested / Shares Outstanding (calculated on a fully-diluted basis)
- **Automatic Triggering** occurs upon the later of:
  - Execution of definitive agreements for the Next Equity Financing, OR
  - Closing of the Next Equity Financing

### 3.2 Investor Rights Upon Conversion
Upon conversion, Investor shall receive the same rights and preferences as investors in the Next Equity Financing, including but not limited to:
- Liquidation preferences equivalent to other Series [SERIES_LETTER] investors
- Anti-dilution protections (as applicable)
- Board observation rights (if applicable to other Series investors)
- Information and inspection rights
- Pro-rata participation rights in future equity raises

### 3.3 Pro-Rata Rights
[OPTIONAL - Include if desired]
Following conversion, Investor shall have the right to participate pro-rata (based on ownership percentage at the time of conversion) in any future equity financings conducted by the Company, on the same terms offered to other existing shareholders.

### 8.4 Anti-Dilution Protection
[OPTIONAL - Select if applicable]
- [ ] **Broad-Based Weighted Average:** In the event of a down round, the conversion price shall be adjusted using a broad-based weighted average formula
