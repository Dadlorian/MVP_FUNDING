## 3. CONVERSION TO EQUITY

### 3.1 Conversion Triggers

**Conversion Event 3: IPO**
Upon the closing of a registered public offering of the Company's Common Stock, the Note shall automatically convert into Common Stock at a price equal to [PLACEHOLDER: 80-85%] of the initial offering price.

### 3.2 Qualifying Financing Definition
A "**Qualifying Financing**" means a Series A or subsequent priced equity financing ("**Series Financing**") in which:

(a) The Company issues shares of preferred stock with standard investor protections, including:
- Board representation rights for lead investor
- Anti-dilution protection (weighted average)
- Dividend preferences
- Liquidation preferences

(b) The minimum amount raised in the Series Financing is [PLACEHOLDER: $500K-$2M];

(c) The Series Financing is led by a venture capital firm or institutional investor;

(d) The per-share purchase price of the Series Financing is at least [PLACEHOLDER: $X per share];

(e) The post-money valuation of the Company (including this Note conversion) is at least [PLACEHOLDER: $3M-$10M].

If a financing does not meet these criteria, the Company shall provide written notice to the Investor, and the parties shall discuss in good faith whether the financing qualifies as a Qualifying Financing.

### 3.3 Conversion Price
Upon a Qualifying Financing, the Note shall convert at the lower of:

**(a) Discount Method:**
The Series Financing per-share price multiplied by (1 minus the Discount Rate).
```
Conversion Price = Series Price × (1 - Discount Rate)

Example:
  Series A Price: $10 per share
  Discount Rate: 20%
  Conversion Price: $10 × (1 - 0.20) = $8 per share
```

**(b) Valuation Cap Method:**
The Valuation Cap divided by the fully-diluted share count immediately prior to the Qualifying Financing.
```
Conversion Price = Valuation Cap / Fully-Diluted Shares

Example:
  Valuation Cap: $5,000,000
  Fully-Diluted Shares: 1,000,000
  Conversion Price: $5,000,000 / 1,000,000 = $5 per share

If Series Price = $10/share, Discount gives price = $8/share
Investor uses Cap method = $5/share (more favorable to investor)
```

**Important:** The Investor receives the benefit of whichever method (discount or cap) results in the lowest price per share (most favorable to Investor).

### 3.4 Conversion Mechanics

(a) **Automatic Conversion:** Upon a Qualifying Financing, the Note automatically converts without further action required.

(b) **Share Issuance Calculation:**
```
Shares Issued = (Principal Amount + Accrued Interest) / Conversion Price

Example:
  Principal: $500,000
  Interest Accrued (2 years @ 5%): $50,000
  Total Amount: $550,000
  Conversion Price: $5/share

  Shares Issued = $550,000 / $5 = 110,000 shares
```

(c) **Share Certificate:** The Company shall issue a stock certificate representing the converted shares within [PLACEHOLDER: 10-15]] business days of conversion.

(d) **No Fractional Shares:** If conversion results in fractional shares, the Company shall round down to the nearest whole share. The Company shall pay the Investor cash equal to the value of any fractional shares.

(e) **Rights and Preferences:** Upon conversion, the Investor shall hold Common Stock with all rights and preferences of Common Stockholders, subject to any protective provisions in the preferred stock financing documents.

### 3.6 Change of Control Definition
A "**Change of Control**" means any event in which:

(a) A person or group acquires beneficial ownership of more than 50% of the voting power of the Company;

(b) The Company enters into an agreement to merge with, consolidate with, or sell all or substantially all of its assets to another entity;

(c) The Company liquidates, dissolves, or winds up;

(d) The Board of Directors and holders of a majority of the Company's voting stock approve such transaction.
