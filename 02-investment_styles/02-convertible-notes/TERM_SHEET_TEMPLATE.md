### 7. Conversion to Equity

#### 7.1 Automatic Conversion - Qualified Financing

```
CONVERSION TRIGGER:
Upon closing of a Qualified Financing (as defined below), the Note
automatically converts to Common Stock. No further action required.

CONVERSION PRICE (Lower of A or B):

A. DISCOUNT METHOD
   Conversion Price = Series A Price × (1 - Discount Rate)

   Example:
     Series A Price:      $10/share
     Discount Rate:       20%
     Conversion Price:    $10 × (1 - 0.20) = $8/share

   [ ] Discount Rate:    [PLACEHOLDER: 15-30%] % (typically 20%)

B. VALUATION CAP METHOD
   Conversion Price = Valuation Cap ÷ Fully-Diluted Shares
                     (immediately prior to Qualified Financing)

   Example:
     Valuation Cap:       $5,000,000
     Shares Outstanding:  1,000,000
     Conversion Price:    $5,000,000 ÷ 1,000,000 = $5/share

   [ ] Valuation Cap:    $[PLACEHOLDER: 3-5x seed investment]

ANTI-FOUNDER PROTECTION:
  Investor receives whichever method is MORE favorable
  (i.e., gives investor the LOWER price and MORE shares)

MOST FAVORED NATION (MFN):
  [ ] Included: If any other convertible note issued with better terms
               (lower cap, higher discount, etc.), investor automatically
               receives the better terms
  [ ] Not Included
```

#### 7.2 Share Issuance Upon Conversion

```
FORMULA:
Shares Issued = (Principal + Accrued Interest) / Conversion Price

EXAMPLE:
  Principal Amount:      $500,000
  Interest Accrued:      $50,000 (2 years @ 5%)
  Total to Convert:      $550,000

  Conversion Price (via Discount): $8/share
  Conversion Price (via Cap):      $5/share

  Using Cap method (more favorable):
  Shares = $550,000 / $5 = 110,000 shares

TIMING:
  Stock certificate issued within [PLACEHOLDER: 10-15] business days

FRACTIONAL SHARES:
  Rounded down to nearest whole share
  Cash payment for value of fractional share
```

#### 7.3 Qualified Financing Definition

```
A "Qualified Financing" means a Series A or later preferred stock
financing meeting ALL of the following:

REQUIREMENTS:
  ✓ Minimum Raise: $[PLACEHOLDER: $500K-$2M] in a single financing round
  ✓ Lead Investor: Institutional VC firm or [PLACEHOLDER: specify]
  ✓ Preferred Stock: Issuance of Series A or later preferred stock
  ✓ Valuation: Post-money valuation of at least $[PLACEHOLDER: $3M-$10M]
  ✓ Standard Investor Rights:
      - Board seat(s) for lead investor
      - Anti-dilution protection
      - Liquidation preferences
      - Information and inspection rights

NON-QUALIFYING FINANCINGS:
  The following do NOT trigger conversion:
    ✗ Strategic partnerships or development deals
    ✗ Debt financing or equipment financing
    ✗ Single angel investments
    ✗ Grants or non-dilutive funding
    ✗ Crowdfunding rounds
    ✗ Rolling convertible notes without Series A

DISPUTE RESOLUTION:
  If Company and Investor disagree whether financing is "Qualified":
  - Company provides written notice with financing details
  - Parties discuss in good faith for [PLACEHOLDER: 15] days
  - If still disagreed: [Arbitration / Mutually agreed expert review]
```

## NOTES FOR USERS

**Key Points:**
1. Term sheet should be 5-10 pages (this is comprehensive)
2. Key negotiation points are Sections 5, 7, 13
3. Share with legal counsel before exchanging
4. Binding only for confidentiality and exclusivity (if included)
5. Definitive Note typically follows within 2-3 weeks

**Typical Timeline:**
- Day 1: Term sheet signed
- Days 2-5: Legal drafting
- Days 6-10: Investor review and negotiation
- Days 11-20: Final document execution
- Day 20-30: Closing and fund transfer

**Common Negotiation Points:**
1. Interest rate (typically 4-7%)
2. Valuation cap (3-5x seed amount)
3. Discount percentage (15-25%)
4. Maturity date (3-5 years)
5. Qualified financing definition
6. Anti-dilution protection
7. Covenant restrictions
8. Board observation rights

**Red Flags in Negotiation:**
- Investor wants >30% discount (unusually favorable)
- Unreasonably low valuation cap
- Excessive covenants restricting operations
- Board seat demands for seed round (unusual)
- Insistence on full ratchet anti-dilution
- Unlimited follow-on investment rights
