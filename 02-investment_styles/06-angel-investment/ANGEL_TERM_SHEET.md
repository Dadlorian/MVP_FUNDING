## Investment Terms

### Type of Security
Choose one:
- [ ] **SAFE (Recommended for most angel rounds)**
  - Simple, 3-page document
  - Non-dilutive at this stage
  - Converts to equity at next priced round

- [ ] **Convertible Note**
  - Functions like a loan + future equity conversion
  - Better if Series A expected within 18-24 months
  - Investor gets interest + discount

- [ ] **Priced Equity (Series Seed)**
  - Immediate equity stake
  - Full investor rights and protections
  - More complex, slower to close

### Valuation Terms (if using SAFE or Note)

**Valuation Cap:** $[VALUATION_CAP]

*Explanation: This caps how much equity the investor can get. If you raise Series A at $8M, investor converts at cap of $5M (meaning they get more shares for same amount).*

**Discount Rate:** [DISCOUNT_PERCENTAGE]% (typically 20-30%)

*Explanation: Investor gets this percentage discount on Series A price. E.g., 20% discount means if Series A is $1/share, they pay $0.80/share.*

### For Convertible Note Only

**Interest Rate:** [INTEREST_RATE]% per annum (typically 3-8%)

*Explanation: Interest accrues on note until conversion or maturity.*

**Maturity Date:** [DATE - typically 2-3 years from investment]

*Explanation: If company hasn't raised Series A by this date, note becomes due (founder must repay or negotiate extension).*

**Conversion Triggers:**
- [ ] Automatic at next priced round >$[MINIMUM_ROUND_SIZE] (e.g., $500K+)
- [ ] Investor chooses to convert on demand
- [ ] Both required

## Investor Rights

### Information & Communication
- [ ] **Monthly Updates**: Founder sends brief email (product, metrics, challenges) to all investors
- [ ] **Quarterly Deep-Dive**: 30-min video call with all interested investors
- [ ] **Annual Financials**: Full P&L and balance sheet (even if pre-revenue)
- [ ] **Access to Data Room**: Shared folder with financials, cap table, customer info
- [ ] **Reasonable Founder Availability**: For questions/intro requests

### Pro-Rata Rights
- [ ] **YES**: Investor can participate in future rounds at same price as new investors (up to [X%] of their original stake)
- [ ] **LIMITED**: Investor can participate only if round is less than $[AMOUNT]
- [ ] **NO**: Investor has no pro-rata rights

*Pro-rata Example: Investor puts in $100K. Series A is $2M. Investor can invest $100K more (1 pro-rata share) if they choose.*

### Board & Governance Rights
- [ ] **No board seat**: Investor is shareholder only
- [ ] **Board observer**: Can attend meetings but not vote
- [ ] **Board seat**: For investors >$[AMOUNT] only
- [ ] **Information rights**: Monthly financials + cap table updates

### Liquidation Preference
- [ ] **No preference**: Investor is treated as common shareholder
- [ ] **Non-participating preferred**: Investor gets preferred return OR common return, whichever is higher
- [ ] **Participating preferred**: Investor gets full preferred return PLUS pro-rata common return

*Note: Most angels should have NO liquidation preference. This is saved for Series A/B investors.*

- [ ] Company has existing option pool: [X]%
- [ ] New option pool being created: [X]%

### Anti-Dilution
- [ ] **Broad-based weighted average**: Most common (protects investor from down rounds with adjustment)
- [ ] **Narrow-based weighted average**: Only founders + major investors count
- [ ] **Full ratchet**: Investor gets more shares if down round (harsh, rare)
- [ ] **None**: Investor has no anti-dilution protection

*Most SAFEs don't have anti-dilution. Convertible Notes sometimes do.*

## Miscellaneous Terms

### Confidentiality
- [ ] Investor agrees to keep financials/strategy confidential
- [ ] Investor may discuss general investment, not specifics
- [ ] Company information is proprietary and protected

### Dilution Expectations
Investor should expect future dilution:
- [ ] Series A (24-36 months): ~25-40% dilution typical
- [ ] Employee options (ongoing): ~0.5-2% per year
- [ ] Future rounds: Variable based on growth

**Example:** $100K investment (1%) becomes 0.6% after Series A. Plan accordingly.

### Founder Voting & Control
- [ ] Founder retains full voting control (for most angles)
- [ ] Major decisions (sale, liquidation, new rounds) may require investor consent if >20% ownership
- [ ] Standard protective provisions apply

### Assignment & Transfer
- [ ] Investor can transfer/sell shares to other accredited investors (with notice)
- [ ] Founder has right of first refusal
- [ ] No transfer to competitors without consent

## Checklist Before Finalizing

**For Founders:**
- [ ] Valuation cap is realistic (benchmarked against comparables)
- [ ] Pro-rata rights amount is sustainable (don't over-commit)
- [ ] You understand use of funds breakdown
- [ ] Board/governance structure is manageable
- [ ] You're comfortable with investor's involvement level
- [ ] Legal cost is budgeted
- [ ] All investors have signed version

**For Investors:**
- [ ] Understand fully diluted ownership
- [ ] Aware of future dilution expected
- [ ] Clear on follow-on capital expectations
- [ ] Comfortable with founder + team
- [ ] Understand use of funds and timeline
- [ ] Know how to reach founder + rhythm of updates
- [ ] Aware of exit timeline (5-7 year typical)
