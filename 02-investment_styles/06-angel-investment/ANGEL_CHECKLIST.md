# Angel Investment Checklist

**Complete operational checklist for founders raising from angels + investors evaluating angel investments**

---

## FOUNDER CHECKLIST: Pre-Fundraising

### Strategic Setup (2 weeks before outreach)

#### Fundraising Goals
- [ ] **Target amount:** $[AMOUNT]
- [ ] **Timeline:** Raise by [DATE]
- [ ] **Valuation cap:** $[VALUATION]
- [ ] **Check size min/max:** $[MIN]-$[MAX]
- [ ] **Target investor count:** [X] investors
- [ ] **Equity to give:** [X]% total (post-Series A estimate)

#### Financial Preparation
- [ ] **12-month financial projection**
  - Monthly revenue projection
  - Monthly expense budget
  - Burn rate calculation
  - Runway months

- [ ] **Use of funds breakdown**
  - Engineering: $[X] ([X]%)
  - Sales/Marketing: $[X] ([X]%)
  - Operations: $[X] ([X]%)
  - Total = Investment amount

- [ ] **Cap table**
  - All founders and shares
  - All employees and grants
  - All option pools
  - Total shares outstanding
  - Pro-forma cap table post-investment

#### Materials Preparation
- [ ] **Pitch deck** (10-15 slides, if requested)
  - Problem
  - Solution
  - Market size
  - Traction
  - Founders
  - Use of funds
  - Why now

- [ ] **One-pager** (single page, leave-behind)
  - Company name + mission
  - Problem + solution
  - Traction to date
  - Founders + team
  - Use of funds
  - How to reach you

- [ ] **Pitch email** (5 sentences)
  - Hook (problem statement)
  - Solution
  - What you're raising (amount + cap)
  - Founders
  - Call to action

#### Investor List
- [ ] **Build list of 100+ potential investors**
  - Warm intro opportunities
  - LinkedIn connections
  - AngelList profiles
  - Prior investors in similar startups
  - Industry experts who could advise

- [ ] **Tier by priority**
  - Tier 1: Must have (strong leads, strategic)
  - Tier 2: Good fit (relevant background)
  - Tier 3: Maybe (some fit)

- [ ] **Find warm intro paths**
  - Ask advisors for intros
  - Ask customers for intros
  - Ask other investors for intros
  - LinkedIn: Who can introduce you?

## FOUNDER CHECKLIST: Outreach

### Lead Generation (Weeks 1-2 of outreach)

#### Warm Intro Requests
- [ ] **Email to connector** (advisor, mentor, customer)
  - Reference your relationship
  - Give 2-3 investor names
  - Short description of company
  - Why you think match is good
  - Share one-pager

- [ ] **Track responses**
  - Spreadsheet: Connector name, investor name, status
  - Follow up with non-respondents after 5 days

#### Intro Meetings
- [ ] **Call with investor** (if warm intro succeeds)
  - 15-30 min intro call
  - Founder intro (who you are, what you're building)
  - Problem + solution (2 min)
  - Traction (1 min)
  - Ask: Interested in learning more? Any intros?
  - Goal: Get next meeting or good feedback

- [ ] **Track results**
  - Interested: Schedule follow-up
  - Maybe: Ask for feedback, follow up in 2 weeks
  - Pass: Thank them, ask for intros

#### Investor Meeting Schedule
- [ ] **Aim for 10-15 investor meetings**
- [ ] **Block calendar:** Tuesday-Thursday afternoons (best for availability)
- [ ] **Time slots:** 30 min minimum, 1 hour okay
- [ ] **Batch meetings:** 2-3 per day max (mental energy)

## FOUNDER CHECKLIST: Pitch & Deep Dives

### Investor Meeting Preparation

#### Before Each Meeting
- [ ] **Research investor**
  - What are their past investments?
  - What's their check size?
  - What industries do they focus on?
  - Find common connections

- [ ] **Customize your pitch**
  - Reference their portfolio companies (if relevant)
  - Highlight aspects they care about
  - Ask them specific questions (shows prep)

- [ ] **Prepare materials**
  - Laptop + demo ready (if software)
  - One-pager printed (or digital)
  - Pitch deck on laptop (if needed)
  - Financials accessible (if asked)

#### During Meeting
- [ ] **Intro (2 min)**
  - Name, background, current role
  - One sentence on what you're building

- [ ] **Problem (3 min)**
  - Who has the problem?
  - How much does it cost them?
  - How are they solving it now?
  - Why now?

- [ ] **Solution (3 min)**
  - What are you building?
  - How is it different?
  - Why can you win?
  - Demo (if applicable)

- [ ] **Traction (2 min)**
  - What's your proof point?
  - Users, revenue, partnerships
  - What's working, what's not

- [ ] **Founders (1 min)**
  - Who's on team?
  - Why are you qualified?
  - Why now?

- [ ] **Ask (1 min)**
  - Raising $[X] at $[X] cap
  - Using funds for [X, Y, Z]
  - Timeline: Closing by [DATE]

- [ ] **Q&A (10+ min)**
  - Listen carefully
  - Answer directly (don't evade)
  - Admit when you don't know
  - Ask for feedback

#### After Meeting
- [ ] **Thank you email** (within 2 hours)
  - Thank for time
  - Key points from conversation
  - Next steps or materials (if requested)

- [ ] **Track in spreadsheet**
  - Investor name, date, outcome
  - Key questions asked
  - Interest level (high/medium/low)
  - Next steps

- [ ] **Iterate on pitch**
  - What questions came up repeatedly?
  - What's working in your pitch?
  - What needs refinement?

## FOUNDER CHECKLIST: Term Negotiation

### Preparing Term Sheet

#### Set Your Terms
- [ ] **Choose security type**
  - [ ] SAFE (simplest, fastest)
  - [ ] Convertible Note (more investor protection)
  - [ ] Priced Equity (full documentation)

- [ ] **Set valuation cap** (if SAFE/Note)
  - Research comparables
  - Benchmark against similar stage companies
  - Get input from 2-3 advisors
  - Write down your reasoning

- [ ] **Set discount rate** (if SAFE/Note)
  - Typical: 20-30%
  - First investment round: 20-25%
  - Why: Early investor bonus

- [ ] **Set interest rate** (if Convertible Note only)
  - Typical: 5-8%
  - Higher if later stage
  - Lower if you want fast close

- [ ] **Set maturity date** (if Convertible Note only)
  - Typical: 2-3 years out
  - Should align with expected Series A

- [ ] **Pro-rata rights**
  - Yes or No
  - If yes, what percentage (e.g., 10% of original stake)

- [ ] **Information rights**
  - Monthly updates? Quarterly? Annual?
  - What information (financials, metrics, etc.)

#### Create Term Sheet
- [ ] **Use template:** ANGEL_TERM_SHEET.md
- [ ] **Customize with:**
  - Company name, legal details
  - Investor name, address
  - Investment amount
  - All terms (cap, discount, pro-rata, etc.)
  - Timeline to close

- [ ] **Get legal review**
  - Your attorney: $2-5K
  - Use template version (cheaper than custom)
  - Make sure terms match intent

#### Send to Interested Investors
- [ ] **Identify interested investors** (from meetings)
- [ ] **Prioritize lead investors** (willing to negotiate)
- [ ] **Send term sheet** (with cover email)
  - "Here are our terms. Any questions?"
  - Point to key terms (cap, discount, pro-rata)
  - Timeline: Decision by [DATE]

- [ ] **Negotiate with lead**
  - Other investors follow on same terms
  - 2-3 rounds of back-and-forth typical
  - Non-negotiable: Same terms for all investors
  - Negotiable: Cap, discount, interest rate

#### Reach Deal on Terms
- [ ] **Get lead investor buy-in**
  - They agree to terms
  - They commit amount
  - They confirm timeline

- [ ] **Get other investors to commit**
  - "Lead investor is in at these terms"
  - "We're closing by [DATE]"
  - Collect commitments (non-binding until doc signed)

- [ ] **Confirm total raised**
  - Target: $[AMOUNT]
  - Committed: $[AMOUNT]
  - Status: On track / Need more / Over

## FOUNDER CHECKLIST: Legal & Closing

### Document Preparation

#### Choose Document Type
- [ ] **If SAFE:**
  - Use template: ANGEL_SAFE_TEMPLATE.md
  - Single-page security per investor
  - Company issues individual SAFE to each investor

- [ ] **If Convertible Note:**
  - Use template: ANGEL_CONVERTIBLE_NOTE.md
  - Promissory note structure
  - Individual note per investor
  - Interest + conversion terms

- [ ] **If Priced Equity:**
  - Use Series Seed template
  - Full terms (preferences, board, etc.)
  - More complex, slower

#### Prepare Legal Documents
- [ ] **Customize template**
  - Company legal name and address
  - Investor name(s)
  - Investment amount
  - Valuation cap / Discount / Interest / Maturity
  - Pro-rata, information rights
  - Governing law (usually your state)

- [ ] **Get legal review**
  - Attorney reviews all terms
  - Confirms math is right
  - Checks for company protection
  - Cost: $2-5K for SAFE template

- [ ] **Get investor feedback** (optional)
  - Some investors have their lawyer review
  - Investor pays their own counsel
  - Collect feedback, make reasonable changes
  - Goal: All investors comfortable with terms

#### Execute Documents
- [ ] **Share final docs with all investors**
- [ ] **Collect signatures**
  - DocuSign or printed + scanned
  - Investor signature required
  - Company founder signature required
  - Get fully executed copy from each investor

- [ ] **Wire instructions confirmed**
  - Company bank account verified
  - Investor confirms wire amount
  - Investor initiates wire transfer
  - Company confirms receipt

#### Update Cap Table
- [ ] **Post-close cap table**
  - Add each investor to SAFE/Note row
  - Document investment amount
  - Document valuation cap
  - Fully diluted ownership (including options)
  - Get cap table to all investors

#### Post-Close Deliverables
- [ ] **Send to each investor:**
  - Fully executed SAFE/Note (countersigned)
  - Updated cap table
  - Investor information form (collect tax ID, address)
  - Welcome packet with founder intro
  - Data room access (if applicable)
  - First investor update (within 2 weeks)

- [ ] **Set up investor communications**
  - Monthly email template
  - Quarterly meeting (optional)
  - Annual report
  - Investor contact list (internal)

## INVESTOR CHECKLIST: Due Diligence

### Initial Evaluation

#### Founder Evaluation
- [ ] **Background check**
  - LinkedIn profile review
  - Prior companies/roles
  - Track record (if any)
  - Other investments/board roles

- [ ] **Founder call debrief**
  - Can I understand what they're building? (clearly explained)
  - Do I believe in the founder? (conviction)
  - Is there a real problem? (customer validation)
  - Can they execute? (team + track record)

- [ ] **Reference calls** (2-3 people)
  - Prior investors (do they trust founder?)
  - Prior employees (would they work with founder again?)
  - Customers (are they happy?)
  - Questions: "Would you invest/work with them again?"

#### Problem/Market Validation
- [ ] **Problem is real**
  - [ ] Spoken to 10+ potential customers
  - [ ] They describe same problem
  - [ ] They'd pay money to solve it
  - [ ] Problem is growing (market tailwinds)

- [ ] **Market size is adequate**
  - [ ] Minimum $50-100M addressable market
  - [ ] Path to $1B+ is possible (not required yet)
  - [ ] Market is growing, not shrinking
  - [ ] Competition is manageable

- [ ] **Understand competitive landscape**
  - [ ] Who else is playing in this space?
  - [ ] How is company different?
  - [ ] Is the difference defensible?
  - [ ] Can big company copy this?

#### Product/Traction Validation
- [ ] **Product exists and works**
  - [ ] Demo / live product visible
  - [ ] Can see roadmap
  - [ ] Technical approach is sound
  - [ ] Scalability isn't a blocker

- [ ] **Traction is evident**
  - [ ] For pre-revenue: 100+ waitlist, 10+ customer conversations, clear GTM
  - [ ] For early revenue: $5K-50K MRR, repeat customers, improving metrics
  - [ ] For growth: $50K+ MRR, positive unit economics, clear path to $100K MRR

- [ ] **Key metrics are healthy**
  - [ ] CAC (customer acquisition cost) reasonable
  - [ ] LTV (lifetime value) is 3x+ CAC
  - [ ] Churn is low (<5% monthly)
  - [ ] NPS is strong (40+)

#### Financial Sense Check
- [ ] **Financials are realistic**
  - [ ] Projections based on data, not fantasy
  - [ ] Assumptions are documented
  - [ ] Burn rate sustainable (18+ months runway)
  - [ ] Use of funds is clear

- [ ] **Capitalization table is clean**
  - [ ] No surprises (undisclosed investors, equity claims)
  - [ ] Founder equity is reasonable (50%+ total)
  - [ ] Option pool is allocated properly
  - [ ] No clawback provisions or unusual terms

- [ ] **No red flags in structure**
  - [ ] No convertible debt already issued (unless disclosed)
  - [ ] No side agreements
  - [ ] No preferential shareholders already
  - [ ] No unusual governance already in place

### Due Diligence Decision

#### Investment Thesis Alignment
- [ ] **Does this match my thesis?**
  - Industry: [YES/NO]
  - Stage: [YES/NO]
  - Geography: [YES/NO]
  - Founder type: [YES/NO]

If NO to more than one → PASS

#### Scoring (1-10 Scale)
- [ ] **Founder quality:** [X]/10
  - Track record, resilience, market knowledge, integrity
- [ ] **Problem/market:** [X]/10
  - Real problem, big enough, growing, defensible
- [ ] **Product/traction:** [X]/10
  - Product exists, traction evident, scalable
- [ ] **Terms/fit:** [X]/10
  - Valuation reasonable, terms favorable, clear role
- [ ] **TOTAL:** [X]/40

#### Decision Framework
```
Score 35-40: STRONG YES (Invest with conviction)
Score 30-35: YES (Good investment)
Score 25-30: MAYBE (Only if capital available + excited)
Score 20-25: PASS (Not excited enough)
Score <20: HARD PASS (Multiple concerns)
```

- [ ] **Final decision:** [INVEST / PASS]
- [ ] **Conviction level:** [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]
- [ ] **Check size:** $[AMOUNT]

## INVESTOR CHECKLIST: Terms Review

### Understanding the Deal

#### SAFE Review (if applicable)
- [ ] **Valuation cap:**
  - Company: $[CAP]
  - Reasonableness: [REASONABLE / HIGH / LOW]
  - Your analysis: If Series A at $[X], converts at $[X]/share

- [ ] **Discount rate:**
  - Discount: [X]%
  - Reasonableness: [15-30% typical]
  - Your analysis: If Series A at $[X], you get $[X]/share (after discount)

- [ ] **Pro-rata rights:**
  - Included: [YES / NO]
  - Your analysis: In Series A, can invest up to [X]% to maintain stake

- [ ] **Information rights:**
  - Frequency: [MONTHLY / QUARTERLY / ANNUAL / NONE]
  - What data: [METRICS / FINANCIALS / UPDATES]

#### Convertible Note Review (if applicable)
- [ ] **Principal amount:**
  - Amount: $[PRINCIPAL]
  - Matches what you're willing to invest: [YES / NO]

- [ ] **Interest rate:**
  - Rate: [X]%
  - Reasonableness: [REASONABLE / HIGH / LOW]
  - Accrual method: [SIMPLE / COMPOUND]

- [ ] **Maturity date:**
  - Date: [MATURITY_DATE]
  - Time to potential Series A: [MONTHS]
  - Risk if no Series A: [UNDERSTAND / NEED CLARIFICATION]

- [ ] **Conversion mechanics:**
  - Convert on Series A? [YES]
  - Discount: [X]%
  - Valuation cap: $[CAP]
  - What if Series A doesn't happen? [CONVERT AT CAP / NOTE MATURES]

- [ ] **Pro-rata and MFN:**
  - Pro-rata rights: [YES / NO]
  - MFN clause: [YES / NO]

#### Priced Equity Review (if applicable)
- [ ] **Valuation:**
  - Pre-money valuation: $[VALUATION]
  - Your assessment: [REASONABLE / HIGH / LOW]
  - Benchmark: Similar companies raising at $[X]

- [ ] **Equity percentage:**
  - Amount: $[INVESTMENT] / $[VALUATION] = [X]%
  - Your comfort: [COMFORTABLE / CONCERNED]

- [ ] **Liquidation preference:**
  - Type: [NON-PARTICIPATING / PARTICIPATING / FULL RATCHET]
  - Your preference: [NON-PARTICIPATING typical]

- [ ] **Board seat / Governance:**
  - Board seat: [YES / NO]
  - Protective provisions: [LIST]
  - Drag-along / tag-along: [DOCUMENTED]

## INVESTOR CHECKLIST: Closing

### Legal Review & Execution

#### Get Your Advisor's Input
- [ ] **Lawyer review** (optional but recommended)
  - Cost: $500-$2K for document review
  - Review: SAFE/Note terms, cap table, company reps
  - Feedback: Any concerns or unusual terms?

- [ ] **Tax/Financial advisor review** (optional)
  - Tax treatment of investment
  - Any risks in structure
  - Reporting requirements

#### Confirm Company Details
- [ ] **Company capitalization table**
  - Current ownership documented
  - Option pool reserved
  - No undisclosed equity claims
  - No weird agreements that affect you

- [ ] **Company representations confirmed**
  - Properly incorporated
  - No undisclosed liabilities
  - No litigation risk
  - Founders have authority to take investment

#### Prepare for Wire Transfer
- [ ] **Confirm wire details**
  - Bank name, routing number, account number
  - Account holder (should be company)
  - Reference code
  - Confirm: Is this the right account?

- [ ] **Wire instructions** (from company)
  - Get in writing from founder email
  - Confirm with second call (fraud prevention)
  - Only wire after signed SAFE/Note

#### Execute Legal Documents
- [ ] **Review final documents**
  - All terms match term sheet
  - Your name is spelled correctly
  - Investment amount is correct
  - No unexpected changes

- [ ] **Sign documents**
  - Use DocuSign or print + scan
  - Make sure company signs too
  - Get back fully executed copies

- [ ] **Initiate wire transfer**
  - Contact your bank
  - Provide all wire instructions
  - Confirm amount: $[AMOUNT]
  - Confirm destination: [COMPANY_NAME]

#### Confirm Receipt
- [ ] **Wait for founder confirmation**
  - Email: "Received your wire"
  - Date received
  - Amount received

- [ ] **Update your records**
  - Investment date: [DATE]
  - Amount invested: $[AMOUNT]
  - Company: [NAME]
  - Type: [SAFE / NOTE / EQUITY]
  - Terms: Cap [X], Discount [X]%, etc.
  - Set calendar reminder: Series A follow-up in [X] months

## POST-INVESTMENT CHECKLISTS

### FOUNDER POST-CLOSE

#### Investor Onboarding
- [ ] **Send welcome materials** (within 1 week)
  - Thank you email
  - Company overview (1-page)
  - Founder bios
  - Link to data room (if applicable)

- [ ] **Collect investor information**
  - Full legal name
  - Address
  - Tax ID / W-9 (if applicable)
  - Preferred email for updates
  - Preferred communication (email/phone/both)

- [ ] **Update cap table**
  - Add investor to cap table
  - Document investment amount
  - Document valuation cap (if SAFE/Note)
  - Pro-rata information
  - Share with investor for confirmation

#### Monthly Investor Updates
- [ ] **Create template email** (reusable each month)
  - Metrics (users, revenue, etc.)
  - Product updates
  - Team updates
  - Challenges
  - What's coming next

- [ ] **Send monthly on same day**
  - Consistency = credibility
  - Pick a day (15th? End of month?)
  - Send even if news is mixed

- [ ] **Track updates**
  - Spreadsheet of what you sent
  - Feedback from investors
  - Questions asked (for FAQ)

#### Quarterly Investor Call (Optional)
- [ ] **Schedule quarterly** (optional but recommended)
  - Every 3 months
  - 30-60 minutes
  - 5-10 investors max
  - Q&A on updates

- [ ] **Prepare for call**
  - Updated metrics
  - Key milestones achieved
  - Challenges being worked on
  - Upcoming priorities

#### Annual Investor Review
- [ ] **Send annual report**
  - Year-end financials (P&L, balance sheet)
  - Major achievements
  - Lessons learned
  - Plans for next year
  - Updated cap table

### INVESTOR POST-CLOSE

#### Investment Tracking
- [ ] **Create portfolio spreadsheet**
  - Company name
  - Investment date
  - Amount invested
  - Type (SAFE/Note/Equity)
  - Key terms (cap, discount, etc.)
  - Status (active, exited, failed)
  - Current value (estimate)

- [ ] **Set quarterly reminders**
  - Check in on company (did they send update?)
  - Review progress against expectations
  - Any need for help/intros?

- [ ] **Annual portfolio review**
  - How many companies succeeded/failed?
  - What's your estimated return?
  - What did you learn?
  - Adjust future investment criteria

#### Active Investor Engagement
- [ ] **Read founder updates** (monthly)
  - Note key metrics
  - Identify any concerns
  - Look for places to help

- [ ] **Attend quarterly calls** (if offered)
  - Ask thoughtful questions
  - Offer relevant help
  - But don't micromanage

- [ ] **Make introductions**
  - What does founder need?
  - Who in your network can help?
  - Make warm intro, then get out of way

#### Series A Follow-On Decision (Year 1.5+)
- [ ] **Review company progress**
  - Did they hit milestones?
  - Are metrics strong?
  - Is founder still executing?
  - Has market changed?

- [ ] **Evaluate Series A opportunity**
  - What's the valuation?
  - What's the lead investor's conviction?
  - Will you participate? (YES / MAYBE / NO)
  - What's your commitment?

- [ ] **Make decision**
  - [ ] YES: Use pro-rata rights, invest more
  - [ ] NO: Let other investors know you're passing
  - [ ] PARTIAL: Put in less than pro-rata
  - [ ] SELL: If available, sell stake to lead investor

## Ongoing Admin Checklists

### FOUNDER: Ongoing Responsibilities

#### Monthly
- [ ] Send investor update (by [DAY])
- [ ] Review key metrics vs. plan
- [ ] Update cap table if any changes
- [ ] Note investor feedback/asks
- [ ] Make progress on milestones

#### Quarterly
- [ ] Investor call (if doing them)
- [ ] Financial deep-dive (P&L, metrics)
- [ ] Cap table check (any new hires affecting options?)
- [ ] Investor feedback synthesis (themes?)

#### Annually
- [ ] Annual investor report
- [ ] Full cap table review
- [ ] Financial audit (if raising Series A)
- [ ] Investor summit (celebrate wins)

#### Series A Preparation (Months 15-18)
- [ ] Is Series A needed?
- [ ] Are metrics strong enough?
- [ ] Should you approach existing investors first?
- [ ] Will you raise from VCs or stay independent?

### INVESTOR: Ongoing Responsibilities

#### Monthly
- [ ] Read founder update
- [ ] Track against expectations
- [ ] Note any concerns

#### Quarterly
- [ ] Attend investor call (if applicable)
- [ ] Check in on any specific asks
- [ ] Consider: Can I make an intro?

#### Annually
- [ ] Portfolio review
- [ ] Update investment records
- [ ] Reflect: What's working? What isn't?

#### Series A Time (Months 18-24)
- [ ] Review company for Series A
- [ ] Decide: Participate or pass?
- [ ] If participating: Commit follow-on amount
- [ ] If not: How to exit gracefully?

## Final Checklist: Before You Close

### Founder Final Check
- [ ] Valuation cap is realistic and defensible
- [ ] Pro-rata amount doesn't overcommit you
- [ ] Legal docs have been reviewed by attorney
- [ ] Cap table is accurate and updated
- [ ] All investors have signed
- [ ] All wire transfers received and confirmed
- [ ] Thank you emails sent to all investors
- [ ] Investor data room is set up
- [ ] First investor update scheduled
- [ ] Monthly update cadence established

### Investor Final Check
- [ ] I understand the terms fully
- [ ] I've done reference checks
- [ ] I've confirmed this fits my investment thesis
- [ ] I understand the risks (likely to lose money)
- [ ] Legal documents reviewed
- [ ] Wire transfer ready to go
- [ ] I understand Series A timeline
- [ ] I understand my pro-rata commitment (if any)
- [ ] I've added to my portfolio tracker
- [ ] I have calendar reminder for updates

---

**Use these checklists to stay organized and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.**
