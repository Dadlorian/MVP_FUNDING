## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Capitalized Terms

**"Equity Financing"** means a bona fide transaction in which Company sells Preferred Stock and the Company receives at least $[MINIMUM_SERIES_A] in new capital. [Typical: $250K-$1M]

**Example:** Series A, Series B, Priced seed round >$500K

**"Valuation Cap"** = $[VALUATION_CAP]

This is the maximum valuation at which investor's SAFE converts to equity. Lower cap = more shares for investor.

**"Discount Rate"** = [X]% [Typical: 20-30%]

Investor gets this % discount on per-share price in Equity Financing.

**"Pro-Rata Rights"** = [YES/NO]

If YES, investor can participate in future rounds up to their ownership percentage.

**"Optional Conversion Event"** = [DEFINE IF APPLICABLE]

## 3. Events

### 3.1 Equity Financing

Upon the occurrence of an Equity Financing, this SAFE will convert into Preferred Stock.

**Conversion Calculation:**

```
Number of shares = Investment Amount / Effective Price Per Share

Where:
Effective Price Per Share = Series A price × (1 - Discount Rate)

OR

Effective Price Per Share = [Series A price, capped at Valuation Cap]

Whichever is LOWER (better for investor)
```

**Example:**
- Investor puts in: $100,000
- Series A price: $2.00/share
- Discount: 20%
- Valuation Cap: $5,000,000

Calculations:
- Option A: $2.00 × (1 - 0.20) = $1.60/share
- Option B: Series A price capped at $5M cap = [Company raised $2M at $5M cap = $2.50/share]
- WINNER: Option A ($1.60 = lower price, more shares)

Result: $100,000 / $1.60 = 62,500 shares

### 3.2 Conversion Details

**Upon Equity Financing:**

1. SAFE automatically converts (no further action needed)
2. Company issues Preferred Stock to Investor
3. Investor becomes preferred shareholder (same rights as other Series A investors)
4. Any accrued rights/preferences apply

**Investor Treatment:**
- [ ] Same terms as new investors (most common)
- [ ] Better terms than new investors (less common, negotiate)
- [ ] Pro-rata participation option: YES / NO

### 3.3 Equity Financing Timing

If no Equity Financing by [DATE]:

- [ ] **SAFE remains outstanding**: Can convert later with no time limit
- [ ] **SAFE matures**: Becomes due on [DATE] (treat as Convertible Note)
- [ ] **SAFE converts to equity**: Founder decides timing, valuation cap applies

*Most angels use no maturity date (SAFE remains until Series A).*

## 4. Special Provisions

### 4.1 Pro-Rata Rights

**Does Investor Have Pro-Rata Rights?** [YES / NO]

If YES:
- Investor has right to invest in future rounds
- Can invest up to [X]% of future round to maintain ownership
- Must execute same documents as other investors
- Standard terms apply (no special negotiation)

**Pro-Rata Rights Example:**
- Investor invests $100K (becomes 1% owner)
- Series A is $5M
- Investor can put in additional $50K to stay at 1% (maintaining pro-rata share)

### 4.2 Most Favored Nation (MFN) Rights

**Does Investor Have MFN Rights?** [YES / NO / MODIFIED]

If YES:
- If other angels get better terms (lower cap, higher discount), this investor gets same terms
- "Better" typically means: lower valuation cap OR higher discount
- Does NOT apply to priced equity rounds

**Typical MFN Clause:**
"If Company issues another SAFE with a lower Valuation Cap or higher Discount Rate within 12 months, Investor's SAFE will be automatically amended to match the better terms."

### 4.3 Investor Information Rights

**Investor will receive:**
- [ ] Monthly updates via email (brief)
- [ ] Quarterly financials (P&L, metrics)
- [ ] Annual audit (if applicable)
- [ ] Access to data room (login credentials)
- [ ] Notice of major events (hiring, partnerships, funding)

**Information Frequency:**
- Monthly: Product/business update email
- Quarterly: Metrics, financials, challenges
- Annual: Comprehensive year-end report

**Investor Obligations:**
- Keep information confidential
- Use for own investment purposes only
- Don't share with competitors or media

### 4.4 Anti-Dilution (If Applicable)

**Does Investor have Anti-Dilution Protection?** [YES / NO]

Most SAFEs do NOT include anti-dilution. If YES:

- **Weighted Average**: If down round occurs, SAFE conversion terms adjust to protect investor
- **Full Ratchet** (rare): Investor gets full benefit of any lower pricing

*Anti-dilution is MORE common in Convertible Notes. Most SAFE investors accept the risk.*

### 4.5 Company Right to Repay SAFE

**Can Company Repay This SAFE for Cash?** [YES / NO]

If YES:
- Company can repay full principal at any time
- Investor must accept repayment
- No interest accrued
- Useful if founder wants to buy out investor early

*Rare. Most founders don't repay SAFEs.*

## 8. Liquidation Scenarios

### 8.1 What Happens If Company Gets Sold?

**If Company is Acquired (Sold) Before Series A:**

SAFE has liquidation preference based on structure:

**Option A: Standard SAFE (No Preference)**
- Investor gets treated as common shareholder
- Receives pro-rata share of proceeds (based on ownership %)
- Usually RESULTS IN: Investor loses all/most capital

**Option B: Investor-Favorable SAFE**
- Investor gets paid [X] multiple back OR equity percentage, whichever is higher
- Example: Get $100K back + any profits above that
- Better protection but less founder-friendly

*Most angels use Option A (standard). Most lose money in acquihires.*

### 8.2 What Happens If Company Fails?

- SAFE has no priority in bankruptcy
- Investor likely loses entire investment
- This is standard investment risk

### 8.3 Down Round Protection

**If Series A is at Lower Valuation (Down Round):**

- SAFE converts at cap (NOT down-round adjusted price)
- Investor gets protection from cap
- Downside protection is primary reason for Valuation Cap

## 10. Complete Mechanics Example

### Walkthrough: SAFE Conversion at Series A

**Initial Investment:**
- Investor: Sarah Chen
- Amount: $100,000
- Valuation Cap: $5,000,000
- Discount Rate: 20%

**Series A Occurs (18 months later):**
- Company raises Series A
- New investors value company at $8,000,000 (pre-money)
- Price per share: $2.00

**Conversion Calculation:**

Step 1: Apply discount to Series A price
- Discounted price = $2.00 × (1 - 0.20) = $1.60/share

Step 2: Cap the price at valuation cap
- Max price based on cap = $5M cap / [total shares] = $1.25/share
- Actual Series A price: $2.00/share
- Capped price: $1.25/share (lower, so use this)

Step 3: Compare (use whichever is LOWER for investor)
- Discounted: $1.60/share
- Capped: $1.25/share
- **WINNER: $1.25/share** (better for investor)

Step 4: Calculate shares
- Shares = $100,000 / $1.25 = **80,000 shares**

**Result:**
- Sarah owns 80,000 shares
- New investors own [X] shares at $2.00
- Sarah is now preferred shareholder with same rights as Series A investors

## 15. FAQ for SAFE Execution

**Q: What happens if the company has a secondary sale or financing before Series A?**
A: SAFE typically doesn't convert until Series A (equity financing). Company can negotiate with investor if needed.

**Q: What if I want to be more protective as an investor?**
A: Consider Convertible Note instead (has interest + maturity). Or use Priced Equity (Series Seed).

**Q: Can I negotiate better terms?**
A: Valuation Cap and Discount are primary negotiation points. Most other terms are standard.

**Q: How is this different from a Convertible Note?**
A: SAFE is simpler, faster, non-dilutive at raise. Note is more protective (has interest + maturity). Both convert to equity.

**Q: What if the company pivots or changes strategy?**
A: Your SAFE terms don't change. You convert on same basis. You own equity in the new strategy.

## 16. Common Modifications (When Negotiated)

**If Founder & Investor Negotiate Changes:**

**Modification 1: Most Favored Nation (MFN)**
"If other investors get a Valuation Cap lower than $[X] within 12 months, this investor's Cap is automatically adjusted to the lower amount."

**Modification 2: Pro-Rata Rights**
"Investor has right to invest in Series A and future rounds to maintain ownership %, up to [X]% of round size."

**Modification 3: Early Conversion Option**
"If Company reaches [$1M ARR / 10K users / other milestone], Investor can choose to convert to equity at Valuation Cap / [X discount]."

**Modification 4: Investor Advisor Role**
"Investor commits to [5 hours/month] advisement on [Product/Sales/Operations]. In exchange, Company will seek investor input on major decisions."

**Modification 5: Follow-On Right**
"Investor will have first right to participate in Series A at same terms as lead investor, for up to [X]% of round."

## 17. Amendment Clause (For Future Changes)

**If Parties Want to Amend SAFE:**

This SAFE can be amended only by written agreement signed by both Company and Investor. Common amendments:

- Change in Valuation Cap (e.g., up-round gives investor additional security)
- Early conversion trigger
- Pro-rata right modifications
- Information rights changes

**Amendments not required for:**
- Equity Financing occurs (automatic conversion)
- Company changes address/legal entity
- Stock splits or recapitalizations (pro-rata adjustment)
