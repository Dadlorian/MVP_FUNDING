# Angel Syndicate Agreement

**For Coordinating Multiple Angel Investors**

Use this when you have a lead investor organizing multiple angels on the same terms.

---

## Overview

An angel syndicate allows:
- **Lead Investor** to organize and negotiate terms once
- **Other Angels** to invest on same pre-negotiated terms
- **Founder** to deal with one lead investor, not 10 different terms
- **Cleaner cap table** with consolidated investor group

## 3. Lead Investor Responsibilities

### 3.1 Negotiation & Terms

Lead investor:
- [ ] Negotiates valuation cap and discount with company
- [ ] Negotiates pro-rata, information, and governance rights
- [ ] Finalizes SAFE/Note legal docs
- [ ] Acts as sole negotiating party (other investors do NOT negotiate)

### 3.2 Due Diligence (Shared)

Lead investor will facilitate:
- [ ] Company information sharing (pitch deck, financials, model)
- [ ] Founder introduction calls (group or individual)
- [ ] Reference calls (other investors, customers, etc.)
- [ ] Q&A document for questions from syndicate members

### 3.3 Communications

Lead investor serves as:
- **Primary contact:** Company calls/emails go to lead
- **Aggregator:** Collects questions from syndicate, sends to founder
- **Translator:** Explains company feedback to syndicate members
- **Closer:** Coordinates execution and closing

**Cadence:**
- Weekly updates during due diligence
- Single investor call with founder (not 10 individual calls)
- Post-investment monthly updates distributed to all

### 3.4 Post-Investment Representation

After close, lead investor:
- [ ] Collects investor information (tax ID, address) from all members
- [ ] Maintains investor list
- [ ] Distributes company updates
- [ ] Represents investor interests at board meetings (if applicable)
- [ ] Organizes investor calls/meetings

---

## 4. Syndicate Member Responsibilities

### 4.1 Due Diligence Participation

Each syndicate member:
- [ ] Reviews company materials provided by lead
- [ ] May ask questions via lead investor
- [ ] Conducts own references/due diligence if desired
- [ ] Makes own investment decision
- [ ] Signals commitment by [DATE]

### 4.2 Investment Commitment

Each member commits to invest:
- **Amount:** $[AMOUNT]
- **By Date:** [CLOSE_DATE]
- **Terms:** Per syndicated SAFE/Note (no modifications)

**Non-Binding Until Signed:** This syndicate agreement is non-binding. Investor commits only upon executing SAFE/Note.

### 4.3 Communication Protocol

Investors agree:
- [ ] Direct questions to lead investor (not founder)
- [ ] Respect founder's time (lead filters questions)
- [ ] Limit to [X] individual calls with founder
- [ ] Abide by NDA and confidentiality
- [ ] Don't discuss investment publicly without consent

## 5. Anti-Dilution & Pro-Rata Rights

### 5.1 Identical Terms for All Investors

**No investor gets special terms:**
- Same valuation cap
- Same discount rate
- Same pro-rata percentage
- Same information rights
- Same follow-on participation

**If investor wants better terms:**
- They may negotiate separately with lead
- If improved, all syndicate members get same improved terms (MFN)
- Or investor can pass on investment

### 5.2 Pro-Rata Rights (If Included)

If syndicated SAFE/Note includes pro-rata:
- Each investor has right to participate in Series A at same pro-rata level
- Pro-rata calculated as: (Original Investment / Target Total) × Series A Size
- Example: $50K investor in $500K syndicate = 10% pro-rata in future rounds

**Lead Investor Management:**
- [ ] Lead manages pro-rata pool in Series A
- [ ] Communicates allocation to each member
- [ ] Each investor decides whether to participate
- [ ] No obligation to follow (pro-rata is optional)

---

## 6. MFN (Most Favored Nation) Clause

### 6.1 Standard MFN in Syndicates

**If lead investor negotiates better terms:**

"If company issues another round (SAFE, Note, or other) with better terms within [12 months], all syndicate members automatically receive the better terms, applied pro-rata."

**Better terms include:**
- Lower valuation cap
- Higher discount rate
- Lower interest rate
- Additional rights (board seat, etc.)

**Does NOT Include:**
- Different security type (SAFE vs. Note)
- Smaller minimum checks
- Different follow-on terms

## 7. Investor Communications & Updates

### 7.1 Information Rights (Post-Investment)

**All syndicate members receive:**

**Monthly:** Brief email update
- Product/feature launches
- Key metrics (usage, revenue, etc.)
- Major hiring/partnerships
- Any concerns/challenges

**Quarterly:** Financial update
- P&L (if revenue-generating)
- Burn rate / runway
- Updated cap table
- Progress on milestones

**Annual:** Comprehensive report
- Year-end financials
- Strategy update
- Major achievements
- Plans for upcoming year

### 7.2 Investor Meeting (Optional)

**Annual or Semi-Annual:**
- [ ] Group call with all syndicate members + founder
- [ ] Q&A on company progress
- [ ] Update on fundraising plans
- [ ] 60-90 minutes, recorded for absentees

### 7.3 Information Confidentiality

All syndicate members agree:
- Keep financial/strategy info confidential
- Don't share with press, competitors, or public
- Can share with spouse, tax/legal advisors
- Company may share with future investors (under NDA)

## 8. Follow-On Investment & Series A

### 8.1 Pro-Rata Participation

If Series A occurs:

**Lead investor:**
- [ ] Communicates Series A details (terms, valuation, size)
- [ ] Calculates each member's pro-rata allocation
- [ ] Invites members to participate on pro-rata basis
- [ ] Coordinates response (who's in, who's out)

**Each member:**
- [ ] Receives pro-rata allocation
- [ ] Can participate (yes), pass (no), or partial
- [ ] Has [X days] to decide
- [ ] No obligation; can choose not to follow

### 8.2 Allocation Example

```
Syndicate Overview:
- Total raised in seed: $500K
- You invested: $50K (10% of syndicate)
- Series A: $2M total

Your Pro-Rata:
- Pro-rata allocation: 10% × $2M = $200K
- You can invest up to $200K at Series A terms
- You're NOT required to invest
- You can invest $100K (half) or $0K (pass)
```

### 8.3 Syndicate Dissolution

Syndicate ends when:
- [ ] All members' SAFEs/Notes convert to Series A
- [ ] Lead investor steps down (designate new lead)
- [ ] Majority votes to dissolve

After Series A, members are individual preferred shareholders (no longer syndicate).

---

## 9. Decision Making & Voting

### 9.1 Lead Investor Authority

**Lead investor has authority to:**
- [ ] Negotiate terms with company
- [ ] Make final decisions on deal terms
- [ ] Represent investors in communications with founder
- [ ] Delegate tasks to syndicate members

**Lead investor CANNOT:**
- Change deal terms without syndicate member approval
- Invest more than announced commitment
- Commit to follow-on without asking syndicate
- Make governance decisions (unless board seat)

### 9.2 Investor Vote (If Needed)

**Syndicate members vote if:**
- Unexpected terms arise (new investor category, etc.)
- Major pivot occurs before Series A
- Lead investor proposes to extend/modify agreement

**Voting:**
- Simple majority (50%+ by amount invested)
- Lead investor has 1 vote like others
- Remote voting okay (email)

---

## 10. Lead Investor Compensation

### 10.1 Lead Investor Fee (Optional)

**Does lead investor receive compensation?**

**Option A: No fee**
- Lead is passionate investor
- Doesn't charge administrative fee
- Typical for early-stage angel syndicates

**Option B: Carry**
- Founder grants [X]% carry to lead investor
- Carry = profit share on exit (not on capital)
- Example: 2-5% carry on profits above 1x return
- Only paid if/when company exits

**Option C: Administrative fee**
- Lead charges small fee ($2,500-$10,000)
- Covers legal, admin, closing costs
- Paid from investor capital
- Example: $10K fee on $500K raise = 2%

**Option D: Mentor shares**
- Lead gets option grant (0.25-0.5%)
- Vests like employee options
- Recognition of advisor role
- Separate from investment

*Most angel syndicates use Option A or C.*

### 10.2 If Compensation is Provided

"Lead investor receives [COMPENSATION_TYPE] at [TERMS]. This is separate from investment return and clearly disclosed to all syndicate members."

## 11. Conflicts & Resolution

### 11.1 Conflicts of Interest

Lead investor discloses any conflicts:
- [ ] Competitive interest in similar companies
- [ ] Financial interest beyond this investment
- [ ] Relationship with founder (family, former colleague)
- [ ] Other investments that might compete

**Remedy:** If significant conflict, lead recuses self or other investors can opt out.

### 11.2 Dispute Resolution

If dispute arises:
1. Good faith negotiation (14 days)
2. Mediation with neutral third party
3. Arbitration (if mediation fails)

---

## 12. Termination & Dissolution

### 12.1 When Syndicate Ends

Syndicate is dissolved when:
- All members' investments convert to Series A equity, OR
- Majority votes to dissolve by written consent, OR
- Lead investor steps down and no one takes over, OR
- Company exits (sale, IPO, liquidation)

### 12.2 If Lead Investor Steps Down

If lead wants to exit before Series A:
- Designate successor lead investor (or member votes on new lead)
- Successor inherits all responsibilities
- Original lead remains investor, just not lead

### 12.3 Investor Can Exit (Before Series A)

Individual syndicate member can exit by:
- Transferring their SAFE/Note to new investor (with founder approval)
- Requesting refund (only if no legal docs signed yet)
- Asking to be released (founder must agree)

## 13. Special Provisions

### 13.1 Founder Concessions

Founder makes these concessions to syndicate:
- [ ] Will not create additional investor categories (everyone gets same terms)
- [ ] Will not negotiate individually with syndicate members
- [ ] Will respect investor time (consolidated communications)
- [ ] Will provide consistent updates to all investors equally

### 13.2 Information Sharing Within Syndicate

Members can:
- [ ] Share information with other syndicate members
- [ ] Discuss due diligence findings
- [ ] Ask each other questions
- [ ] Compare observations on founder/team

Members cannot:
- Share with outside investors without consent
- Discuss investment terms with non-syndicate members
- Share with press or public

### 13.3 Follow-On Commitment (Non-Binding Intent)

**Lead investor intent (non-binding):**
- [ ] Will participate in Series A (if good deal)
- [ ] Will allocate pro-rata share to all members
- [ ] Will communicate Series A terms transparently

Actual Series A participation is optional for all investors.

## 14. Master Legal Documents

### 14.1 Individual Investment Docs

Each syndicate member executes **individual** SAFE or Convertible Note:
- Separate document for each investor
- Identical terms for all SAFEs/Notes
- Company issues one to each investor

**Example:**
- Investor 1: Individual SAFE for $50K
- Investor 2: Individual SAFE for $75K
- Investor 3: Individual SAFE for $50K
- All three SAFEs have identical cap/discount/terms

### 14.2 This Syndicate Agreement

Separate document:
- [ ] Outlines lead investor responsibilities
- [ ] Sets communication protocols
- [ ] Defines pro-rata/follow-on process
- [ ] Non-binding (except confidentiality)
- [ ] Signed by lead + other investors (optional but recommended)

## 19. Common Questions on Angel Syndicates

**Q: What's the advantage over individual investments?**
A: Simpler for founder (one negotiation), cleaner cap table (fewer investors), better deal terms (lead negotiates once), and coordinated follow-on in Series A.

**Q: Can I negotiate different terms?**
A: No. Syndicate is only valuable if all terms are identical. If you want different terms, pass and invest separately.

**Q: What if I want to invest more?**
A: Talk to lead investor. You can invest more individually outside syndicate, but syndicate allocation is fixed.

**Q: What if I disagree with lead investor's terms?**
A: Before signing, you can ask for changes (which affects all investors). If lead won't negotiate, you can pass.

**Q: Can I sell my SAFE to another investor?**
A: Maybe (depends on doc terms). Lead investor typically has right of first refusal. You can transfer with founder approval.

## 20. Checklist for Lead Investors

- [ ] Identify 5-7 potential syndicate members
- [ ] Pitch deal to each (commitment + timeline)
- [ ] Confirm target total and minimum
- [ ] Negotiate terms with founder once
- [ ] Prepare investor materials (deck, financials, model)
- [ ] Facilitate founder call (group or individual options)
- [ ] Collect questions and send to founder
- [ ] Confirm final commitments by deadline
- [ ] Coordinate legal docs (SAFE/Note for each investor)
- [ ] Send to all investors for signature
- [ ] Collect wire instructions and execute wires
- [ ] Close syndicate agreement (optional but recommended)
- [ ] Create investor list and communication method
- [ ] Send first update within 2 weeks of close
- [ ] Establish monthly update rhythm

---

**Final Note:** Angel syndicates work best when there's trust between lead and other investors. Pick a lead investor who is fair, communicative, and committed to the deal.
