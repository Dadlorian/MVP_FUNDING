## DETAILED MATRIX BY DIMENSION

### 1. DILUTION IMPACT

| **Vehicle** | **Typical Range** | **Best Case** | **Worst Case** | **Multiple Rounds** | **Notes** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAFE** | 5-15% seed | 3% | 25% | Compounds with gaps | No dilution until Series A |
| **Convertible Note** | 5-15% seed | 3% | 25% | Compounds with gaps | Includes discount impact |
| **Priced Equity** | 15-30% per round | 10% (small raise) | 50% (large raise) | Severe compounding | Direct equity dilution |
| **Revenue-Based** | 0% equity | 0% | 0% | No dilution | Growth obligation instead |
| **Venture Debt** | 0% equity | 0% | 0% | No dilution | Repayment obligation |
| **Angel** | 10-25% | 5% | 40% | Varies per investor | Depends on negotiation |
| **Equity Crowdfunding** | 10-20% | 8% | 30% | Multiple small investors | Fragmented cap table |
| **Bootstrap** | 0% | 0% | 0% | No dilution | Sweat equity only |
| **Grants** | 0% | 0% | 0% | No dilution | Non-dilutive |
| **Strategic VC** | 5-25% | 3% | 35% | Depends on strategy | May include licensing deals |

**Analysis:**
- **Zero-dilution options:** Venture Debt, Revenue-Based Financing, Grants, Bootstrap
- **Highest dilution:** Priced Equity Rounds (especially Series B+)
- **Most predictable:** Revenue-Based Financing, Venture Debt, Grants
- **Most variable:** Strategic VC, Angel Investment

### 2. CONTROL IMPLICATIONS

| **Vehicle** | **Pre-Investment Control** | **Post-Investment Control** | **Board Rights** | **Governance Rights** | **Veto Rights** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAFE** | 100% founder | 100% founder (until convert) | None | None | None |
| **Convertible Note** | 100% founder | 100% founder (until convert) | None | None | None |
| **Priced Equity** | 100% founder | ~70-85% founder | Often 1+ seats | Voting rights | Anti-dilution, dividend |
| **Revenue-Based** | 100% founder | 100% founder | None | None | Limited (metrics-based) |
| **Venture Debt** | 100% founder | ~95% founder | None | None | Financial covenants |
| **Angel** | 100% founder | ~75-90% founder | Often 1 seat | Varies | Information rights |
| **Equity Crowdfunding** | 100% founder | Diluted control | None | Crowd voting possible | Information rights |
| **Bootstrap** | 100% founder | 100% founder | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| **Grants** | 100% founder | 100% founder | None | Some reporting | Limited |
| **Strategic VC** | 100% founder | ~70-80% founder | Often seat | Strategic input | Possible veto |

**Key Insights:**
- **Maximum Control:** Bootstrap, SAFEs (pre-conversion), Convertible Notes, Grants
- **Minimum Control:** Priced Equity, Strategic VC, Equity Crowdfunding
- **Control Trade-offs:** More capital = less control in most vehicles

## QUICK DECISION MATRIX

**Use this matrix to quickly identify the right vehicle:**

| **Your Situation** | **Best Vehicles** | **Avoid** |
|---|---|---|
| **Need capital fast, pre-product** | SAFEs, Convertible Notes | Equity Crowdfunding, Grants |
| **Strong traction, closing Series A** | Priced Equity, SAFEs | Bootstrap, Grants |
| **Capital but want no dilution** | Venture Debt, Revenue-Based | Priced Equity, Angel |
| **Conservative founder, avoid dilution** | Bootstrap, Grants, Venture Debt | Equity Crowdfunding, Strategic VC |
| **Need investor expertise, not just capital** | Angel Investment, Strategic VC | Bootstrap, Revenue-Based |
| **Want investor governance + protection** | Priced Equity, Strategic VC | SAFEs, Revenue-Based |
| **Bootstrapping possible but want money** | Revenue-Based, Grants | Priced Equity (too dilutive) |
| **Many small investors (crowdfunding)** | Equity Crowdfunding | SAFEs (too few), Venture Debt |
| **Strategic partnership + capital** | Strategic Corporate VC | SAFEs, Grants |
| **Need money desperately, any terms** | Venture Debt, Angel | Selective - be careful |
