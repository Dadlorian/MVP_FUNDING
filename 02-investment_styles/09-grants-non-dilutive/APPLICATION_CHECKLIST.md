# Grant Application Pre-Submission Checklist

**Comprehensive verification checklist before submitting any grant application**

---

## Overview

Use this checklist 1-2 weeks before your submission deadline to ensure your grant application is complete, compliant, and competitive. Walk through each section systematically and mark items complete.

---

## PART 1: PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS & ELIGIBILITY

### Program Understanding
- [ ] Reviewed and understood all program guidelines
- [ ] Downloaded the most recent program solicitation (with today's date)
- [ ] Read all agency FAQs and guidance documents
- [ ] Identified any recent program updates or changes
- [ ] Confirmed program timeline and phase structure
- [ ] Understood evaluation criteria and scoring methodology
- [ ] Reviewed 2-3 past winning proposals (if available)
- [ ] Attended optional program information sessions or webinars

### Eligibility Verification
- [ ] Confirmed company meets size requirements (e.g., <500 employees for SBIR)
- [ ] Verified ownership structure meets requirements (e.g., 51% U.S. owned for SBIR)
- [ ] Confirmed business is organized as C-Corp, S-Corp, or LLC (if required)
- [ ] Checked that company is registered in required state/location
- [ ] Verified company is not debarred from federal contracts (SAM.gov)
- [ ] Confirmed industry/technology focus aligns with program scope
- [ ] For STTR: Verified university partnership eligibility
- [ ] For corporate grants: Verified alignment with corporate focus areas
- [ ] For competitions: Confirmed company stage matches competition tier

### Program Officer Contact
- [ ] Identified assigned program officer (if applicable)
- [ ] Found program officer email and phone
- [ ] Scheduled pre-application meeting (if encouraged)
- [ ] Discussed proposal concept with program officer
- [ ] Asked for guidance on competitive positioning
- [ ] Clarified any ambiguous program requirements

---

## PART 2: TECHNICAL CONTENT & NARRATIVE

### Problem Statement
- [ ] Problem clearly articulated in 1-2 compelling sentences
- [ ] Problem is quantified with specific data points
- [ ] Market size for problem is estimated ($X market opportunity)
- [ ] Number of affected companies/customers is stated
- [ ] Relevant industry/market context is provided
- [ ] Problem statement is specific to your solution, not generic
- [ ] Problem has supporting evidence or customer validation
- [ ] Broader impact is explained (why does this matter?)
- [ ] Problem aligns with program priorities and topic area

### Solution/Technical Approach
- [ ] Solution is clearly explained (what are you building?)
- [ ] Solution directly addresses stated problem
- [ ] Approach is feasible within proposed timeline
- [ ] Technical approach shows innovation/differentiation
- [ ] Approach is grounded in preliminary data/results
- [ ] Key assumptions are stated and justified
- [ ] Technical risks are identified and mitigated
- [ ] Solution is not overly complex or pie-in-the-sky
- [ ] Relationship between problem and solution is clear

### Detailed Technical Plan
- [ ] Plan breaks work into discrete tasks with clear objectives
- [ ] Each task has specific deliverables (not vague outputs)
- [ ] Timeline shows monthly milestones (6-month Phase I)
- [ ] Task dependencies are identified
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable and specific
- [ ] Realistic effort estimates are provided for each task
- [ ] Key risks for each task are identified
- [ ] Mitigation strategies are provided for key risks
- [ ] Plan accounts for technology maturation trajectory (TRL)
- [ ] Plan includes contingency time/budget for uncertainties

### Competitive Advantage & Innovation
- [ ] Solution is differentiated from existing approaches
- [ ] Comparison table provided showing competitor positioning
- [ ] Competitive advantages are specific (not generic "better")
- [ ] Barriers to competition are identified and explained
- [ ] Prior art is cited and distinguished from your approach
- [ ] Innovation is explained in language reviewers understand
- [ ] Competitive advantage is defensible (via IP, speed, integration, etc.)
- [ ] You explain why others haven't solved this already

### Team & Qualifications
- [ ] Team bios highlight relevant expertise
- [ ] Each team member's role is clearly defined
- [ ] Key personnel time allocation is specified
- [ ] Team has complementary skills (not duplicate expertise)
- [ ] Team has experience in problem domain
- [ ] Team has successfully executed similar projects
- [ ] Advisory board/board members add credibility
- [ ] External partners/collaborators are committed (letters included)
- [ ] CV/resumes highlight relevant accomplishments
- [ ] For STTR: University partnership is clearly explained

### Commercialization Plan
- [ ] Target market is clearly identified
- [ ] Market size is quantified ($X TAM, SAM, etc.)
- [ ] Revenue model is specific and justified
- [ ] Pricing strategy is explained
- [ ] Customer acquisition strategy is realistic
- [ ] Go-to-market timeline is detailed
- [ ] Phase III funding sources are identified
- [ ] Customer validation is demonstrated (pilots, LOIs, pre-orders)
- [ ] Path to profitability is shown
- [ ] Competitive market positioning is clear

### Risk Analysis
- [ ] Technical risks are identified and specific
- [ ] Each risk has estimated probability (High/Med/Low)
- [ ] Impact of each risk is assessed
- [ ] Mitigation strategy is provided for each risk
- [ ] Commercialization risks are identified
- [ ] Market/adoption risks are addressed
- [ ] Team risks are considered
- [ ] Regulatory/compliance risks are mentioned (if applicable)
- [ ] Risks are addressed proactively (not hidden)

### Broader Impact (if required)
- [ ] Broader impacts are explained (beyond commercial)
- [ ] Social/environmental/economic impact is articulated
- [ ] Broader outcomes are measurable
- [ ] Workforce development aspects are included (if applicable)
- [ ] STEM education engagement is described (if applicable)
- [ ] Diversity and inclusion aspects are addressed (if applicable)

---

## PART 3: BUDGET & FINANCIAL PLAN

### Budget Summary
- [ ] Total budget is within program limits
- [ ] Budget reflects project scope and timeline
- [ ] Budget breakdown is clear and itemized
- [ ] Personnel costs are reasonable for market rates
- [ ] Equipment costs are justified
- [ ] No obvious budget padding or red flags
- [ ] Indirect costs (F&A) are calculated at correct approved rate
- [ ] Match funds (if required) are identified and committed

### Budget Narrative & Justification
- [ ] Budget narrative explains every significant expense
- [ ] Personnel rates are justified (market benchmarks)
- [ ] Equipment purchases are justified (why needed for project)
- [ ] Travel is itemized (who, where, why)
- [ ] Materials/supplies are specifically listed
- [ ] Subcontractor costs are detailed
- [ ] Contingency is included but not excessive
- [ ] Budget aligns with technical plan (tasks require resources listed)

### Financial Projections (if required)
- [ ] Revenue projections are realistic and conservative
- [ ] Assumptions are stated (CAC, LTV, churn, etc.)
- [ ] Year-over-year growth is justified
- [ ] Profitability timeline is shown
- [ ] Financial metrics are competitive (gross margin, payback period)
- [ ] Projections are supported by market data/benchmarks
- [ ] Pro forma financials are professionally formatted

## PART 4: SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS & MATERIALS

### Key Personnel Information
- [ ] PI/PD CV included (2-page limit, if specified)
- [ ] Key personnel CVs included (focused on relevant experience)
- [ ] All CVs are current and formatted consistently
- [ ] CVs highlight relevant accomplishments and credentials
- [ ] Current & Pending Support form completed (if required)
- [ ] Letters of support from key advisors/partners included

### Organizational Documentation
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation or formation included
- [ ] Business registration/licensing documentation included
- [ ] Proof of small business status (for SBIR/small business programs)
- [ ] DUNS number included (for federal grants)
- [ ] SAM.gov registration confirmation (for federal grants)
- [ ] Tax identification numbers correct
- [ ] Organizational chart showing key personnel

### Customer/Partner Commitments
- [ ] Letters of intent from potential customers included
- [ ] Letters of commitment from partners/collaborators
- [ ] University partnership letters (for STTR)
- [ ] Customer testimonials or validation statements
- [ ] Joint venture agreements (if applicable)
- [ ] Non-disclosure agreements in place (if needed)

### Technical Documentation
- [ ] Literature review and references included (if required)
- [ ] Preliminary data/results supporting feasibility included
- [ ] Technology demonstration (prototype, pilot results)
- [ ] Technical diagrams or architecture drawings
- [ ] Patent search results or IP landscape analysis
- [ ] Prior publications or presentations cited
- [ ] Data management plan (if required)

### Compliance & Certifications
- [ ] Certifications/compliance statements are signed
- [ ] Conflict of interest disclosures completed
- [ ] Foreign collaboration disclosures completed (if applicable)
- [ ] Federal debt checks completed
- [ ] All required signature blocks are present
- [ ] Applicant certification form is signed/dated
- [ ] Cost-sharing agreements signed (if applicable)

## PART 5: FORMATTING & COMPLIANCE

### Document Formatting
- [ ] Follows agency formatting requirements exactly:
  - [ ] Font size: [X pt minimum] (typically 12pt)
  - [ ] Margins: [X inches] (typically 1" minimum)
  - [ ] Page limits respected for all sections
  - [ ] Line spacing: [Single/1.5/Double]
  - [ ] Headers/footers include required information
- [ ] Section headers are clear and labeled
- [ ] Page numbers included on every page
- [ ] Tables and figures are properly labeled and referenced
- [ ] Equations are properly formatted and referenced
- [ ] All text is readable (not shrunk to fit)
- [ ] PDF is searchable (not scanned images)
- [ ] File names follow required format (if specified)

### Page Limits & Compliance
- [ ] Technical narrative: [# pages] actual vs [# pages] limit
- [ ] Budget narrative: [# pages] actual vs [# pages] limit
- [ ] All required sections included, none missing
- [ ] Appendices follow page limit rules
- [ ] References don't count toward page limit (if stated)
- [ ] Figures/tables/equations format is compliant
- [ ] No content "hidden" in margins or small fonts

### Writing Quality
- [ ] Clear, jargon-free writing for broad audience
- [ ] All acronyms defined on first use
- [ ] Technical terms explained if not obvious
- [ ] Active voice used instead of passive
- [ ] Sentence structure is varied and engaging
- [ ] Paragraph length is reasonable (not walls of text)
- [ ] Transitions between sections are clear
- [ ] Narrative flows logically from problem → solution → impact

### Proofreading
- [ ] Spell-check completed (no red squiggles)
- [ ] Grammar-checked (no obvious errors)
- [ ] Consistent terminology throughout
- [ ] Consistent formatting (fonts, styles, spacing)
- [ ] All references and citations are accurate
- [ ] No placeholder text left in document
- [ ] No confidential information included
- [ ] Professional tone maintained throughout

## PART 6: CONTENT EVALUATION AGAINST CRITERIA

### For SBIR/STTR Proposals
**Technical Merit (40-50%)**
- [ ] Problem statement demonstrates clear technical need
- [ ] Solution approach is innovative and feasible
- [ ] Technical plan is detailed and realistic
- [ ] Preliminary results support feasibility
- [ ] Team expertise matches technical requirements
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable

**Commercialization Potential (30-40%)**
- [ ] Market opportunity is substantial ($X market size)
- [ ] Competitive advantage is clear and defensible
- [ ] Commercialization plan is realistic
- [ ] Customer validation is demonstrated
- [ ] Revenue model is viable
- [ ] Go-to-market strategy is detailed

**Feasibility & Cost (10-20%)**
- [ ] Project can be completed in 6 months (Phase I)
- [ ] Budget is realistic and well-justified
- [ ] Budget/timeline ratio is reasonable
- [ ] Team has capacity to execute
- [ ] Risk mitigation is adequate

**Broader Impacts (varies)**
- [ ] Project contributes to national/societal benefit
- [ ] Workforce development aspects are included
- [ ] Benefits extended beyond direct commercial application

### For Corporate Grants
- [ ] Alignment with corporate strategic priorities is clear
- [ ] Solution integrates with corporate technology/platform
- [ ] Value to corporate partner is demonstrated
- [ ] Revenue/TAM expansion opportunity is clear
- [ ] Pilot/validation plan is detailed
- [ ] Timeline is realistic and achievable
- [ ] Team expertise matches requirements
- [ ] Go-to-market plan includes corporate partnership value

### For Competitions/Pitch Competitions
- [ ] Pitch is compelling and tells a clear story
- [ ] Problem is quantified and relatable
- [ ] Solution is clear and differentiated
- [ ] Traction demonstrates progress/validation
- [ ] Team credentials are strong
- [ ] Business model is clear
- [ ] Ask is specific and reasonable
- [ ] Presentation quality is professional

## PART 7: FINAL SUBMISSION CHECKLIST

### Pre-Submission (1 week before deadline)
- [ ] All content sections completed and reviewed
- [ ] All required attachments are included and named correctly
- [ ] All CVs, letters, and supporting docs are finalized
- [ ] Budget is final and signed off by finance
- [ ] Document is saved with version control
- [ ] Backup copies are created and stored safely
- [ ] Grant officer approval obtained (if required for pre-submission)

### Submission System Setup
- [ ] Grants.gov account created (if federal grant)
- [ ] All required system registrations completed
- [ ] DUNS number obtained (if required)
- [ ] SAM.gov registration current (if required)
- [ ] Employer Identification Number (EIN) verified
- [ ] Authorized official identified and ready to sign
- [ ] Test submission completed (if allowed by system)

### Final Quality Assurance (48 hours before deadline)
- [ ] Document proofread one final time by different person
- [ ] All hyperlinks and references verified to work
- [ ] All attachments included and can be opened
- [ ] PDF pages are all present and in correct order
- [ ] File sizes are within limits (if specified)
- [ ] File names match requirements (if specified)
- [ ] No confidential information is visible
- [ ] Formatting looks correct when printed/viewed in PDF viewer

### Submission & Confirmation
- [ ] Application submitted [X] hours before deadline
- [ ] Confirmation of submission received and saved
- [ ] Submission ID/number recorded and saved
- [ ] Submission confirmation screenshot taken
- [ ] All submission documentation saved in secure location
- [ ] Team notified of successful submission
- [ ] Deadlines for post-submission activities noted
- [ ] Tracking sheet updated with submission details

## PART 8: POST-SUBMISSION

### Immediately After Submission (within 24 hours)
- [ ] Email sent to program officer confirming submission (if appropriate)
- [ ] Submission documentation filed in project folder
- [ ] Backup of all submission materials created
- [ ] Grant tracking sheet updated
- [ ] Team meeting held to debrief
- [ ] Lessons learned documented
- [ ] Next steps identified (Phase II planning, other grant opportunities)

### During Review Period
- [ ] Program timeline and decision date recorded
- [ ] Monitoring system set up for decision notification
- [ ] Calendar reminders set for expected decision date
- [ ] Follow-up communication plan identified (if allowed)
- [ ] Contingency plan developed (what if rejected?)
- [ ] Next grant opportunity research begun

### If Awarded
- [ ] Award notification documented
- [ ] Award letter reviewed and clarified with program officer
- [ ] Grant agreement reviewed by legal
- [ ] Reporting requirements documented and calendared
- [ ] Compliance processes established
- [ ] Project execution plan finalized
- [ ] Phase II proposal planning begun (if Phase I award)

### If Rejected
- [ ] Reviewer feedback obtained and analyzed
- [ ] Rejection debriefing meeting held with team
- [ ] Key improvement areas identified
- [ ] Resubmission plan created (if appropriate)
- [ ] Lessons learned documented
- [ ] Next grant opportunity identified

## SCORING GUIDE: Self-Assessment

### Content Quality Score (0-40 points)

**Technical Merit (10 points)**
- 10: Problem is innovative, solution is feasible, approach is detailed
- 7-9: Problem is clear, solution is sound, approach is reasonable
- 4-6: Problem is understandable but limited innovation; approach needs more detail
- 1-3: Problem unclear or approach too vague
- 0: No technical substance

**Business Potential (10 points)**
- 10: Clear market opportunity, strong competitive position, viable revenue model
- 7-9: Reasonable market size, competitive advantage defined, revenue model plausible
- 4-6: Market opportunity exists but not thoroughly validated; competitive advantage weak
- 1-3: Limited market opportunity or unclear revenue model
- 0: No commercial potential

**Feasibility (10 points)**
- 10: Plan is realistic, timeline achievable, budget appropriate, team capable
- 7-9: Plan is reasonable, timeline realistic, budget justified, team qualified
- 4-6: Plan needs refinement, timeline tight, budget questioned, team gaps
- 1-3: Plan unrealistic, timeline questionable, budget excessive, team inexperienced
- 0: Not feasible

**Team Quality (10 points)**
- 10: Exceptional team with proven track record in this domain
- 7-9: Strong team with relevant experience
- 4-6: Adequate team with some gaps
- 1-3: Weak team credentials or significant gaps
- 0: No qualified team

### Presentation Quality Score (0-10 points)

- 10: Professional writing, clear organization, compelling narrative
- 7-9: Good writing, organized well, engaging
- 4-6: Acceptable writing, some organization issues, average engagement
- 1-3: Poor writing, disorganized, unclear narrative
- 0: Unprofessional, incomprehensible

### Compliance Score (0-10 points)

- 10: All requirements met, all documents included, fully compliant
- 7-9: All requirements met, minor formatting issues
- 4-6: Most requirements met, some missing documents
- 1-3: Several requirements not met
- 0: Major compliance failures

### Total Possible Score: 60 points

**Target Score Before Submission: 50-55 out of 60 (83-92%)**

If your self-assessment score is <45, consider revisions before submission.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
- ❌ Problem statement lacks quantified data
- ❌ Solution is described vaguely ("we'll use AI")
- ❌ No demonstration of feasibility (no preliminary results)
- ❌ Weak competitive analysis or positioning
- ❌ Unrealistic timeline or budget
- ❌ Team credentials don't match project requirements
- ❌ No customer validation or market proof
- ❌ Vague success criteria ("develop prototype" vs. "achieve 95% accuracy")

### Formatting Mistakes
- ❌ Exceeds page limits by shrinking fonts
- ❌ Missing required sections or documents
- ❌ Poor formatting (inconsistent fonts, spacing)
- ❌ Spelling/grammar errors
- ❌ Figures/tables that don't add value
- ❌ Jargon without explanation

### Submission Mistakes
- ❌ Submitted after deadline
- ❌ Missing required signatures
- ❌ Attachments won't open or are corrupted
- ❌ PDF is unsearchable (scanned image)
- ❌ File names don't match requirements
- ❌ No confirmation of submission
