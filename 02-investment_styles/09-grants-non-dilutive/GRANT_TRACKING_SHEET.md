## Checklist for New Grant Applications

### Pre-Application Phase (2-4 weeks before deadline)
- [ ] Research grant requirements and guidelines thoroughly
- [ ] Review scoring rubric and evaluation criteria
- [ ] Identify program officer contact and email
- [ ] Find 2-3 past winning proposals (if available)
- [ ] Check eligibility requirements (company size, location, industry)
- [ ] Assess team capacity to complete application
- [ ] Assign grant lead and contributors

### Application Development (4-6 weeks before deadline)
- [ ] Outline sections and assign ownership
- [ ] Develop problem statement with quantified impact
- [ ] Create solution overview and technical approach
- [ ] Compile team bios and letters of support
- [ ] Develop detailed budget and budget narrative
- [ ] Draft risk analysis and mitigation strategies
- [ ] Create market analysis and competitive positioning
- [ ] Develop financial projections

### Review & Refinement (2-3 weeks before deadline)
- [ ] Internal review by core team
- [ ] External review by non-expert (clarity check)
- [ ] Technical review by subject matter expert
- [ ] Strategic review for alignment
- [ ] Compliance check (format, page limits, requirements)
- [ ] Proofread for grammar and spelling
- [ ] Final formatting and document preparation

### Submission Phase (1 week before deadline)
- [ ] Create grants.gov account (if federal grant)
- [ ] Test all links and attachments
- [ ] Submit draft for early feedback
- [ ] Make final revisions based on feedback
- [ ] Submit final application 24-48 hours before deadline
- [ ] Confirm successful submission
- [ ] Archive submission confirmation and all materials

### Post-Submission Phase
- [ ] Update tracking sheet with submission details
- [ ] Schedule follow-up activities (if allowed)
- [ ] Begin planning Phase II proposal (if Phase I)
- [ ] Monitor for decision timeline
- [ ] Document lessons learned
- [ ] Identify next grant opportunities
