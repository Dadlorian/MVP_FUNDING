## SECTION 2: REVENUE SHARING STRUCTURE

### 2.1 Revenue Share Percentage

**Primary Rate:**
- **[X]% of Monthly Revenue** for [NUMBER] months
- Paid monthly within [X] days of month-end

**Escalating/Declining Schedule** (Optional):
- Months 1-12: [X]% monthly
- Months 13-24: [X]% monthly
- Months 25+: [X]% monthly

### 2.2 Revenue Definition

Revenue defined as **Net Revenue per GAAP ASC 606**:

**INCLUDED:**
- Subscription/SaaS monthly recurring revenue
- One-time product sales (hardware, software licenses)
- Services revenue and professional services
- Licensing fees and royalties
- Marketplace or platform transaction fees
- [OTHER_INCLUDED_REVENUE]

**EXCLUDED:**
- Financing proceeds (debt, equity, grants)
- Investment income or interest earned
- Asset sales or non-recurring transactions
- Returns and refunds (already netted)
- Tax refunds or government credits
- Affiliate commissions or third-party reseller payments
- [OTHER_EXCLUSIONS]

**Recognition Standard:** U.S. GAAP ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers)

### 2.3 Revenue Share Adjustment Mechanisms

#### Option A: Tiered by Revenue Level
```
Monthly Revenue Level    |  Revenue Share %
Under $50K              |  5%
$50K - $150K            |  4%
$150K - $300K           |  3%
Over $300K              |  2%
```

#### Option B: Time-Based Decline
```
Months 1-6:   5% (early support)
Months 7-12:  4% (established business)
Months 13-24: 3% (mature revenue)
```

#### Option C: Performance-Based
```
If cumulative payments reach cap 50% earlier than expected:
Revenue share reduces from 5% to 3%

If trailing 6-month revenue growth <5%:
Revenue share increases to 6%
```

### 2.4 Payment Mechanics

- **Calculation Method**: Revenue Share % × Reported Net Revenue
- **Minimum Payment**: [No minimum / $[AMOUNT] per month]
- **Maximum Payment**: [No maximum / $[MAXIMUM_AMOUNT] per month, whichever is less]
- **Payment Method**: Wire transfer to [INVESTOR_ACCOUNT]
- **Due Date**: [X]th day of following month
- **Late Payment Fee**: [0% / [X]% monthly interest on late payments]

**Example Calculation:**
```
Month Revenue:        $250,000
Revenue Share %:      4%
Monthly Payment:      $10,000
Cumulative to Date:   $45,000
```

## SECTION 3: REPAYMENT TERMS

### 3.1 Repayment Cap (Return Multiple)

**Cap Multiple:** [X]x (e.g., 2.0x)

**Cap Amount:** [X]x × $[INVESTMENT_AMOUNT] = **$[CAP_AMOUNT]**

**Interpretation:**
- Investor receives maximum of $[CAP_AMOUNT] total
- Agreement terminates when cumulative payments reach cap
- All revenue beyond cap remains with Company 100%
- Cap is **not** contingent on timeframe (whenever reached)

**Example:**
```
Investment Amount:      $250,000
Cap Multiple:          2.0x
Repayment Cap:         $500,000

Investor's return capped at $500,000 regardless of future success
```

### 3.2 Term/Duration

**Term Type:** [Perpetual / Fixed Term]

**If Perpetual:**
- Revenue-sharing continues indefinitely until cap is reached
- Either party can [NOT / CAN] terminate for convenience

**If Fixed Term:**
- Term Duration: [X] months from Effective Date
- If cap not reached by [DATE], agreement terminates
- Any payments made count toward cap (proportionally credited to future funding)

**Typical Scenarios:**
```
Scenario 1: 24-month term
├─ Payments made for 24 months or until cap (whichever comes first)
├─ Expected payback: 18-24 months
└─ Fallback: If cap not reached in 24 months, obligation ceases

Scenario 2: 48-month term
├─ Longer payback expected
├─ For slower-growth or lower-revenue companies
└─ Provides extended repayment runway
```

### 3.3 Early Repayment

Company may repay outstanding obligation early:

- **Timing**: Any time before Term expires
- **Amount**: [Remaining balance / Remaining cap balance]
- **Effect**: Immediately terminates agreement
- **Penalty**: [No penalty / [X]% early repayment fee]

**Example:**
```
Repayment Cap:         $500,000
Cumulative Paid:       $350,000
Remaining Balance:     $150,000

Company elects early repayment:
Pays $150,000 → Agreement terminates immediately
All future revenue retained by Company
```

## SECTION 4: REPORTING AND AUDIT RIGHTS

### 4.1 Monthly Reporting

**Due Date:** [X] days after month-end
**Format:** Standardized monthly revenue report
**Contents:**
- Total revenue by category
- Material transactions or adjustments
- Reconciliation to accounting records
- Certification by [CEO / CFO]

### 4.2 Quarterly Reporting

**Due Date:** [X] days after quarter-end
**Contents:**
- Unaudited financial statements (P&L, cash flow)
- 6-month revenue forecast
- Key business metrics and milestones
- Material changes since last report

### 4.3 Annual Audit

**Frequency:** Once per calendar year (or triggered by variance)

**Investor Rights:**
- Engage independent accountant at Company's expense
- Audit fee cap: $[AMOUNT] per audit
- Examine Company books and records
- Verify Revenue calculations and bank deposits
- Request customer agreements or contracts if needed

**Audit Trigger:** If reported Revenue variance >±15% from previous forecast

### 4.4 Revenue Dispute Resolution

If Investor disputes reported Revenue:

1. **Notification**: Investor raises concern within 30 days of report date
2. **Investigation**: Company provides documentation within 15 days
3. **Negotiation**: Parties negotiate in good faith for 15 days
4. **Resolution**: Discrepancy adjusted prospectively (not retroactively)
5. **Documentation**: Both parties document agreed resolution

**Example:**
```
Reported Revenue:      $500,000
Investor Questions:    $75,000 in disputed refunds

Resolution:
- Refunds were legitimate customer returns (not inflated)
- Actual reportable revenue: $475,000
- Payment adjustment: $19,000 instead of $20,000
- Both parties confirm revenue recognition going forward
```

## SECTION 7: EXIT & CHANGE OF CONTROL SCENARIOS

### 7.1 Acquisition/Merger

If Company is acquired or merged:

**Option A - Assumption:**
- Buyer assumes all payment obligations
- Payment terms unchanged
- Investor continues receiving revenue shares

**Option B - Buyout:**
- Buyer pays Investor: [X]% of gross transaction proceeds
- Investor obligation terminates
- Payment is credit against Investor's remaining cap balance

**Option C - Acceleration:**
- Investor may elect lump-sum payment of:
- [125%] of remaining cap amount
- Payment made from transaction proceeds

**Illustrative Scenario:**
```
Acquisition Price:       $10,000,000
Repayment Cap:          $500,000
Cumulative Payments:    $300,000
Remaining Cap:          $200,000

Option A: Buyer pays $200,000 to Investor (assumes obligation)
Option B: Buyer pays $1,500,000 to Investor (20% of gross)
Option C: Investor receives $250,000 (125% of remaining)
```

### 7.2 IPO or Public Company

If Company becomes publicly traded:

- Revenue sharing may convert to [X]% of revenue or cash dividend equivalent
- Investor may elect to [LIQUIDATE / CONTINUE]
- Terms shall be adjusted to reflect public company status

### 7.3 Company Failure/Dissolution

If Company:
- Ceases operations or winds down

- Has no remaining revenue

**Treatment:**
