## 2. REVENUE SHARING OBLIGATIONS

### 2.1 Revenue Share Percentage
From the Effective Date until the earlier of (i) the date the Cumulative Payments equal the Repayment Cap (Section 2.4), or (ii) the Termination Date (Section 2.6), Company shall pay to Investor a monthly revenue share calculated as follows:

**Monthly Payment = [REVENUE_SHARE_PERCENTAGE] × [REPORTED_MONTHLY_REVENUE]**

**Key Terms:**
- Revenue Share Percentage: **[X%]** (e.g., 4% per month)
- Revenue Definition: [REVENUE_DEFINITION] (See Section 2.2)
- Payment Frequency: Monthly, due by the [X]th day of following month

### 2.2 Revenue Definition

For purposes of this Agreement, "**Revenue**" shall mean:

**Included in Revenue:**
- All sales of Company products/services, net of refunds and returns
- Subscription and recurring revenue
- Licensing fees and royalties
- Service revenue and consulting
- [OTHER_REVENUE_ITEMS]

**Excluded from Revenue:**
- Proceeds from financing activities (debt, equity, grants)
- Interest, dividends, or investment income
- Proceeds from asset sales or business lines
- Non-cash consideration (equity grants, barter)
- Tax refunds and government credits
- Affiliate payments and commissions (unless product of Company)
- [OTHER_EXCLUSIONS]

**Revenue Recognition Standard:**
All Revenue shall be recognized in accordance with U.S. GAAP, ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers).

**Example Calculation:**
```
Gross Sales:                    $300,000
Less: Refunds/Returns:           -$15,000
Less: Credit Card Processing:    -$8,500
= Reportable Revenue:           $276,500

Monthly Payment = 4% × $276,500 = $11,060
```

### 2.3 Revenue Share Schedule (if Multi-Tranche)

Revenue share percentage may adjust based on funding tranche and timeline:

| Period | Revenue Share % | Notes |
|--------|-----------------|-------|
| Months 1-12 | [X]% | Initial tranche period |
| Months 13-24 | [X]% | (Optional: reduced rate) |
| Months 25-36 | [X]% | (Optional: reduced rate) |
| After Cap Hit | 0% | No further payments |

**Example:**
- Months 1-18: 5% of revenue
- Months 19-36: 3% of revenue (to incentivize reaching cap faster)
- After cumulative payments reach cap: 0%

### 2.4 Repayment Cap

Investor's total receipt under this Agreement shall not exceed the following amount:

**Repayment Cap = [CAP_MULTIPLE] × [INVESTMENT_AMOUNT] = $[CAP_AMOUNT]**

**Example Calculation:**
```
Investment Amount:    $250,000
Cap Multiple:         2.0x
Repayment Cap:        $500,000

This means Investor receives a maximum of $500,000 total
(regardless of how much additional revenue is generated)
```

The Agreement automatically terminates when cumulative monthly payments equal the Repayment Cap. Any revenue generated after this point is retained entirely by Company.

### 2.5 Monthly Payment Process

#### 2.5.1 Revenue Reporting
By [REPORTING_DUE_DATE] of each month, Company shall submit to Investor:
- Monthly revenue report certified by [CFO/CEO]
- Detailed breakdown by revenue category (if applicable)
- Reconciliation to accounting records
- Any material changes or disputes

**Report Format:**
```
MONTHLY REVENUE REPORT - [MONTH/YEAR]

Total Gross Revenue:        $[AMOUNT]
Less: Refunds/Returns:      -$[AMOUNT]
Less: [ADJUSTMENT]:         -$[AMOUNT]
= Reportable Revenue:       $[NET_AMOUNT]

Monthly Payment Due:        $[AMOUNT]

Cumulative Payments to Date: $[TOTAL_TO_DATE]
Cumulative Cap:             $[CAP]
Remaining:                  $[REMAINING]
```

#### 2.5.2 Payment Processing
- **Payment Amount**: Monthly Payment calculated per Section 2.1
- **Payment Deadline**: Due by [PAYMENT_DUE_DATE] (e.g., 5th of following month)
- **Payment Method**: Wire transfer to [WIRE_INSTRUCTIONS], or [OTHER_METHOD]
- **Late Payment**: [NO_INTEREST/Interest at [X]% per month]
- **Minimum Payment**: [NO_MINIMUM / $[MINIMUM_AMOUNT], even if revenue is lower]
- **Maximum Payment**: [NO_MAXIMUM / Capped at $[MAXIMUM_AMOUNT] per month]

#### 2.5.3 Example Payment Schedule
```
Month 1: Revenue $150,000 → Payment $6,000 (4% × $150,000)
Month 2: Revenue $175,000 → Payment $7,000 (4% × $175,000)
Month 3: Revenue $200,000 → Payment $8,000 (4% × $200,000)
Month 4: Revenue $225,000 → Payment $9,000 (4% × $225,000)
```

## 3. FINANCIAL REPORTING AND AUDIT RIGHTS

### 3.1 Monthly Reporting
Company shall provide monthly financial statements within [X] days of month-end, including:
- Revenue summary by category

- Material changes in business operations

### 3.2 Quarterly Reporting
Quarterly submissions shall include:

- Revenue forecast for next 6 months
- Status of key business metrics
- [ANY_OTHER_REQUIRED_REPORTS]

### 3.3 Annual Reporting
Annual submissions shall include:
- Audited or reviewed financial statements
- Full year revenue summary
- Certification by CEO and CFO
- Written acknowledgment of Cumulative Payments and remaining balance

### 3.4 Audit and Verification Rights
Investor shall have the right to, at Company's expense (not to exceed $[MAX_AUDIT_COST] per audit):

1. **Annual Audit**: Conduct annual audit of Revenue calculations (once per calendar year)
2. **Audit Trigger**: Conduct special audit if reported Revenue variance exceeds ±15% from forecast
3. **Third-Party Verification**: Engage independent accountant to verify Revenue
4. **Inspection Rights**: Review Company books and records related to Revenue
5. **Payment Verification**: Request bank statements and customer lists to verify Revenue

### 3.5 Dispute Resolution for Revenue

If Investor disputes reported Revenue:

1. **Notice Period**: Investor has 30 days after receiving report to dispute
2. **Company Response**: Company has 15 days to provide clarification/documentation
3. **Negotiation**: Parties shall negotiate in good faith for 15 days
4. **Mediation**: If unresolved, parties shall mediate at shared cost
5. **Adjustment**: If dispute resolved, payments adjusted prospectively (prior months not reopened unless fraud)

**Example Dispute:**
```
Month reported: $500,000 revenue
Investor questions: $50K in disputed refunds

Resolution:
- Company provides refund documentation
- Both agree on actual revenue: $475,000
- Payment adjustment: $19,000 instead of $20,000
- Future months: Both confirm revenue recognition process
```

## 4. TERMINATION AND EARLY REPAYMENT

### 4.1 Automatic Termination
This Agreement automatically terminates upon the earlier of:
1. Cumulative Payments equal Repayment Cap (Section 2.4)
2. [TERMINATION_DATE] - [DATE], if Cap not reached by this date
3. Company achieves [EXIT_TRIGGER] (acquisition, IPO, etc.)

Upon termination, Company has no further obligation to make payments.

### 4.2 Early Repayment Option
Company may optionally prepay outstanding obligations early:

- **Early Repayment Amount**: Remaining balance to reach Repayment Cap
- **Timing**: Can be done at any time before Termination Date
- **Method**: Lump sum wire transfer to [WIRE_INSTRUCTIONS]
- **Effect**: Immediately terminates Agreement upon receipt

**Example:**
```
Repayment Cap:              $500,000
Cumulative Payments to Date: $400,000
Early Repayment Amount:      $100,000

Company pays $100,000 now to eliminate remaining obligation
Agreement terminates immediately
All future revenue is retained by Company
```

### 4.3 Termination Upon Company Events

#### 4.3.1 Acquisition or Merger
If Company is acquired, merged, or sold:
- **Treatment**: Buyer's successor company assumes payment obligations
- **Acceleration**: Investor may elect to (a) convert share to [X]% discount note, or (b) receive [X]% of gross proceeds from transaction
- **Condition**: If sale proceeds less than remaining cap, payment obligation reduced proportionally

#### 4.3.2 Bankruptcy or Insolvency
If Company files for bankruptcy or becomes insolvent:
- **Status**: RBF treated as [EQUITY-LIKE / SENIOR DEBT / JUNIOR DEBT]
- **Priority**: Ranked [PARI PASSU] with equity
- **Recovery**: Investor participates pro-rata in remaining assets after senior creditors

#### 4.3.3 Company Dissolution
If Company voluntarily dissolves or winds down:
- **Surviving Obligation**: Payment obligation survives (but may be reduced by ability to pay)
- **Payment Source**: Any remaining assets go to investors per priority
- **Wind-down Sales**: Investor continues receiving [X]% of any wind-down revenue

### 4.4 Change of Control Provision
If Company undergoes a change of control where:
- New owner acquires >50% of voting shares, or
- Company is sold or merged into third party

Then, at Investor's election:
1. **Option A**: Acquirer/new company assumes all obligations hereunder
2. **Option B**: Buyer pays Investor: [X]% of gross transaction proceeds (credited against remaining cap)
3. **Option C**: Investor receives accelerated payment of [125%] of remaining cap amount

## 5. COMPANY COVENANTS

### 5.1 Affirmative Covenants
Company covenants to:

1. **Financial Reporting**: Provide accurate, timely financial reports per Section 3
2. **Revenue Recognition**: Use consistent GAAP revenue recognition (ASC 606)
3. **Business Operations**: Operate business in ordinary course consistent with historical practice
4. **Compliance**: Maintain all licenses, permits, and regulatory compliance
5. **No Diversion**: Not divert significant revenue to related parties or unusual transactions
6. **Insurance**: Maintain standard business insurance (property, liability, directors & officers)
7. **Books & Records**: Maintain accurate accounting records available for audit

### 5.2 Negative Covenants
Company shall NOT, without Investor's written consent:

1. **Revenue Diversion**: Enter into unusual transactions reducing Revenue:
   - Large non-cash sales or barter arrangements
   - Selling revenue to third parties
   - Unusual rebates, discounts, or promotional giveaways >10% of revenue

2. **Debt Issuance**: Incur additional revenue-based financing from other parties, except:
   - Up to [X] additional RBF providers allowed
   - Total RBF revenue obligations capped at [X]% monthly

3. **Asset Sales**: Sell material assets representing >20% of revenue-generating capability

4. **Major Transactions**: Merge, consolidate, or sell business (governed by Section 4.3)

5. **Dividend or Distributions**: Pay dividends or make distributions to shareholders

6. **Line of Business Changes**: Exit or substantially change major revenue lines (>20% of revenue)

### 5.3 Operating Metrics
Company shall maintain (or provide evidence of working toward):

- **Runway**: Minimum [X] months of operating runway at all times
- **Growth Rate**: [OPTIONAL] Minimum [X]% monthly revenue growth for [X] months
- **Churn**: [OPTIONAL] Customer churn rate not to exceed [X]% monthly
- **Cash Reserves**: Maintain minimum [X] weeks of operating expenses in cash

If Company falls below these metrics, Investor may trigger audit rights or require remedial plan.
