## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This Information Rights Agreement governs the provision of information from [PLACEHOLDER: Company Name] to [PLACEHOLDER: Corporate Investor Name], establishing clear boundaries around:

1. **What information** is disclosed and at what level of detail
2. **Who receives** the information within the corporate investor
3. **How information** is protected and used
4. **When information** is disclosed (reporting schedule)
5. **Restrictions** on use and further disclosure

This agreement recognizes that strategic investors require deep visibility into portfolio companies for legitimate business purposes, while portfolio companies need to protect sensitive competitive information.
