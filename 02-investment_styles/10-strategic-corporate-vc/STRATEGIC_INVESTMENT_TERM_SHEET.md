## VI. ANTI-DILUTION & DILUTION MANAGEMENT

### A. Anti-Dilution Provisions

**Type**:
- [ ] **Weighted Average** - (Broad-based / Narrow-based)
- [ ] **Full Ratchet** - [STRATEGIC CONSIDERATION] Often negotiated away in corporate contexts
- [ ] **None** - (rarely acceptable to investors)

**Weighted Average Formula** (if applicable):
```
CP = (A + B) / (C + D)

Where:
CP = New conversion price
A = Old capitalization
B = New issue price x New shares issued
C = Old shares outstanding
D = New shares issued
```

**Carve-Outs** (Events not triggering anti-dilution):
