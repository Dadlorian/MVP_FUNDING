## V. EXCEPTIONS TO CONFIDENTIALITY

### A. Information Not Considered Confidential

Confidential Information EXCLUDES information that:

1. **Already Public**
   - Was already publicly available at time of disclosure
   - Becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement

2. **Independently Developed**
   - Independently developed by Investor without access to Company's information
   - Documented evidence of independent development

3. **Prior Knowledge**
   - Known to Investor prior to disclosure by Company
   - Documented evidence of prior knowledge

4. **Rightfully Received**
   - Rightfully received from third party without confidentiality obligation
   - Third-party source documented

5. **General Knowledge**
   - General knowledge or skills acquired through Permitted Recipients' experience
   - Does not include specific Company information

6. **Approval to Disclose**
   - Disclosed with Company's prior written approval
   - [PLACEHOLDER: Other exceptions]

**Burden of Proof**: Investor bears burden of proving information falls within exception
