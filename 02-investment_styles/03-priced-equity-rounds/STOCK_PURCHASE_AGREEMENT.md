## ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATIONS

### 1.1 Defined Terms

**Capitalization Table ("Cap Table")**: The schedule attached as **Exhibit A** showing all equity holders, options, warrants, and fully diluted ownership.

**Certificate of Incorporation ("Certificate")**: The certificate of incorporation of the Company, as amended and in effect immediately prior to closing.

**Common Stock**: The common stock of the Company, par value $[___] per share.

**Fully Diluted Capitalization**: Total outstanding equity assuming:
- Exercise/conversion of all options outstanding
- Exercise of all warrants outstanding
- Conversion of all convertible securities
- Issuance from reserved option pool

**Liquidation Event**: Any sale, merger, consolidation, liquidation, or IPO of the Company.

**Preferred Stock**: Series [SEED/A/B] Preferred Stock, designated and issued pursuant to this Agreement.

**Securities Act**: Securities Act of 1933, as amended.

**Exchange Act**: Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.

## ARTICLE III: REPRESENTATIONS & WARRANTIES

### 3.1 Representations of Company

**Company represents and warrants to Investor**:

#### A. Organization & Authority

**3.1.1 Organization & Good Standing**:
- Duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing in [STATE]
- Full power and authority to conduct business and own property
- All necessary authority to execute and perform this Agreement

**3.1.2 Authority for Transaction**:
- Authorized to issue Preferred Stock
- All corporate action required for issuance has been taken
- No conflicts with Certificate, Bylaws, or law

**3.1.3 Capitalization Accuracy**:
- Capitalization table (Exhibit A) is true, correct, and complete
- All shares, options, RSUs, warrants, and convertibles are listed
- Exercise prices and vesting schedules are accurate
- No undisclosed equity obligations or commitments

#### B. Financial Information

**3.1.4 Financial Statements**:
- Financial statements provided are accurate and prepared per GAAP (or consistent basis)
- Fairly represent financial condition of Company
- No material undisclosed liabilities
- Cash, receivables, and inventory values are accurate

**3.1.5 No Material Adverse Change**:
- No material adverse change in Company's business since [DATE]
- Company continues to operate in ordinary course
- No loss of material customers or contracts
- No material increase in liabilities

#### C. Contracts & Obligations

**3.1.6 Contracts**:
- All material contracts listed in Schedule [__]
- Contracts are valid, binding, and enforceable
- No breaches by Company
- No defaults under any material contract
- No liens, security interests, or claims against Company

**3.1.7 Debt & Liabilities**:
- All debt properly documented and disclosed
- Debt obligations are current
- No off-balance sheet arrangements
- No contingent liabilities undisclosed

#### D. Intellectual Property

**3.1.8 IP Ownership**:
- All intellectual property is owned by Company free and clear
- Proper assignments obtained from all founders, employees, contractors
- All prior inventions documented
- Non-compete agreements in place from founders (if applicable)

**3.1.9 IP Registration**:
- All registered IP (trademarks, patents) properly registered or applied for
- Registrations are current and in good standing
- No non-infringement issues

**3.1.10 No IP Infringement**:
- Company does not infringe any third-party IP rights
- No claims or allegations of infringement received
- All open-source licenses properly documented and compliant

#### E. Employees & Contractors

**3.1.11 Employment Agreements**:
- All employees have signed employment agreements
- All key employees have assigned IP to Company
- Non-compete/non-solicitation agreements where applicable
- No side letters or understandings

**3.1.12 Options & Equity**:
- All option grants properly documented
- Option pool properly reserved and authorized
- Vesting schedules and exercise prices documented
- Option agreements signed by all holders
- No undisclosed equity promises or commitments

**3.1.13 No Key Person Dependencies**:
- No undisclosed dependencies on key employees
- No pending departures known
- Key employees have agreed to continue employment

#### F. Compliance & Legal

**3.1.14 Compliance with Laws**:
- Company in compliance with all laws and regulations
- All required licenses, permits, certifications obtained
- No violations of any law

**3.1.15 No Litigation**:
- No pending or threatened litigation
- No regulatory investigations
- No government inquiries or audits
- No material disputes with customers or vendors

**3.1.16 Tax Matters**:
- All tax returns filed and accurate
- All taxes paid
- No disputes with IRS or tax authorities
- No liens for unpaid taxes

#### G. Environmental & Other Matters

**3.1.17 Environmental**:
- [If applicable] No environmental liabilities
- Compliance with environmental laws

**3.1.18 Insurance**:
- Adequate insurance in place (general liability, [__])
- All claims properly disclosed

**3.1.19 Regulatory Compliance**:
- [If applicable] Regulatory compliance (healthcare, financial services, etc.)
- All required filings and disclosures made

### 3.2 Representations of Investor

**Investor represents and warrants to Company**:

**3.2.1 Authority**:
- Authority to execute this Agreement and consummate investment
- No conflicts with law or other agreements

**3.2.2 Accredited Investor Status** (if applicable):
- Investor is accredited investor as defined in SEC Regulation D
- [Income/net worth thresholds met]

**3.2.3 Investment Purpose**:
- Investment for own account and benefit

**3.2.4 Investment Experience**:
- Investor is sophisticated investor with startup investment experience
