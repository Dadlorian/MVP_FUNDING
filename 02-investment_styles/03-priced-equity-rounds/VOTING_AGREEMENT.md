### 2.1 Board Composition

**Board Composition Breakdown**:

| Director Type | Designating Party | Count |
|---------------|------------------|-------|

### 2.2 Founder Director Election

**Holders of Common Stock (Founders) shall have the right to designate and elect one director** to the Board.

**Election Mechanics**:
- Founders with [___]% of Common Stock can designate one director
- [FOUNDER_NAME] shall serve as initial director (CEO)
- Upon resignation/removal of founder director, remaining founders designate replacement
- Multiple founders can jointly designate (if ownership split)

**Founder Director Rights**:
- Full Board voting rights
- All director duties and obligations
- Fiduciary duties to Company and all stockholders

### 2.3 Preferred Stockholder Director Election

**Holders of Series [SEED/A/B] Preferred Stock shall have the right to elect one director** if they hold at least [___]% of Preferred Stock (or [___]% of Company on fully diluted basis).

**Holder of Series [Prior] Preferred Stock shall have the right to elect one director** if they hold at least [___]% of their respective preferred class.

**Preferred Director Designation**:
- Lead investor in each series typically designates director
- Designated director represents investor class interests
- Director subject to same fiduciary duties as all directors

### 2.4 Independent Director Election

**Independent Director Designation**:
- Remaining board seats filled by independent directors
- Selected by mutual consent of Common and Preferred stockholders
- Served as neutral/tiebreaker if needed
- Cannot be affiliated with Company, founders, or investors

**Independent Director Qualifications**:
- Business/industry experience
- Investment experience (preferred)
- References from both founders and investors
- No conflicts of interest

**Selection Process**:
- Investors and founders prepare list of candidates
- Mutual interview and discussion
- Agree on 1-2 independent candidates
- Board unanimously elects independent directors

### 2.5 Observer Rights (Non-Board Members)

**Significant stockholders not on Board shall have observer rights**:
- Applicable to holders of [___]% or more of their respective preferred class
- Right to attend Board meetings (except executive sessions)
- Right to receive Board materials
- No voting rights as observers
- Bound by confidentiality obligations

## ARTICLE IV: STOCKHOLDER VOTING AGREEMENTS

### 4.1 Cumulative Voting Elimination

**Cumulative voting is eliminated** if provided under Company bylaws.

- Stockholders cannot cumulate votes for directors
- Each stockholder votes per share × number of director seats
- Prevents minority from electing directors through vote concentration

### 4.2 Common Stockholder Voting

**Holders of Common Stock shall vote all Common shares**:
- In favor of directors designated by common stockholders
- To authorize/approve matters agreed by common stockholders
- Voting as block to coordinate with investor preferences (if negotiated)

### 4.3 Preferred Stockholder Voting

**Holders of Preferred Stock shall vote**:
- In favor of directors designated by Preferred stockholders
- To approve matters covered under protective provisions
- To protect preferred interests (liquidation preference, etc.)

### 4.4 Voting Agreements - Removal of Directors

**Directors elected by each class can only be removed**:
- By holders of the class that elected them (or super-majority of all stockholders)
- For cause: breach of fiduciary duty, misconduct
- Without cause: if approved by stockholders generally

**Procedure for Director Removal**:
1. Stockholder meeting called (or written consent)
2. Motion to remove director
3. Vote taken (majority of stockholders or designated class)
4. If approved, director removed
5. Replacement director elected per original designation rights

### 4.5 Deadlock Resolution

**If Board deadlocked** on major decision (e.g., 2-2 vote on 4-person board):
- Issue referred to Founder-Investor mediation
- [__]-day mediation period
- If not resolved, matter escalated to:
  - Stockholder vote
  - Or arbitration per Section 4.6
  - Or third-party mediator/arbitrator

### 4.6 Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

**If Board/stockholders deadlocked on material matter**:
- Either party can trigger arbitration
- Single arbitrator selected by mutual consent
- Or three arbitrators: one selected by each side, third by mutual consent
- Arbitration per [JAMS/AAA] rules
- Location: [CITY], [STATE]
- Cost split equally between parties

### 5.1 Matters Requiring Preferred Stockholder Consent

**The following matters require the approval of holders of [___]% of Series [SEED/A/B] Preferred Stock** (these are the "Protective Provisions"):

#### 5.1.1 Capitalization Matters
- [ ] Create, authorize, or issue any new series of stock
- [ ] Increase or decrease authorized shares of any class
- [ ] Adjust conversion rates, preferences, or privileges of preferred stock
- [ ] Repurchase stock (except lapse of options upon termination)
- [ ] Declare or pay any dividend or distribution
- [ ] Recapitalize or reclassify stock
- [ ] Any action materially affecting rights of preferred stockholders

#### 5.1.2 Fundamental Changes
- [ ] Sell all or substantially all assets of Company
- [ ] Merge, consolidate, or combine Company with another entity
- [ ] Liquidate or dissolve Company
- [ ] Materially change the business of Company
- [ ] Enter any new line of business material to Company

#### 5.1.3 Governance & Operations
- [ ] Change size of Board of Directors
- [ ] Amend Certificate of Incorporation or Bylaws in manner affecting Preferred Stock
- [ ] Related party transactions exceeding $[__]
- [ ] Hire, fire, or change compensation of CEO or CFO (over $[__])
- [ ] Material change to executive compensation policy
- [ ] Enter into service contracts with founders/related parties over $[__]

#### 5.1.4 Finance & Budget
- [ ] Approve or materially modify annual operating budget
- [ ] Make material capital expenditures over $[__] not in budget
- [ ] Incur indebtedness over $[__] (excluding trade payables)
- [ ] Create liens or security interests on Company assets
- [ ] Lease or purchase real property over $[__]

#### 5.1.5 Equity & Compensation
- [ ] Issue options/RSUs outside of approved option pool
- [ ] Approve new option pool or expand existing pool
- [ ] Accelerate vesting of options for any person
- [ ] Material changes to incentive compensation plans
- [ ] Approve options/equity grants over $[__]

#### 5.1.6 Financing & Capital Raising
- [ ] Authorize new series of preferred stock
- [ ] Authorize debt with equity features or conversion rights
- [ ] Approve IPO or go-public process
- [ ] Any financing at valuation lower than prior round

### 5.2 Waiver of Protective Provisions

**Protective provisions may be waived**:
- If holders of [___]% of preferred consent in writing
- Waiver is specific (can waive one action, not all protective provisions)
- Waiver effective only for action described

### 5.3 Procedure for Obtaining Protective Provision Consent

**When protective provision approval needed**:

1. Company provides written notice to all affected preferred stockholders
2. Notice includes:
   - Description of proposed action
   - Financial/business justification
   - Timeline for decision needed
   - Materials for informed decision-making
3. Stockholders have [__] days to review and respond
4. If not approved within [__] days, deemed approved (optional waiver of deadline)
5. Once approved, Board/Company can proceed

### 5.4 Information Rights to Monitor Protective Provisions

**To enable protective provision enforcement**, Company shall:
- Provide quarterly financial statements
- Provide annual budgets and plans
- Notify investors of material contracts/partnerships
- Notify investors of material litigation or regulatory matters
- Notify investors of key employee departures

## ARTICLE VI: VOTING MECHANICS & PROCEDURES

### 6.1 Voting Procedures

**Stockholders may vote**:
- In person at stockholder meeting
- By proxy (written authorization to vote on behalf of stockholder)
- By written consent (approval without meeting)
- By electronic ballot (if authorized by Board)

### 6.2 Proxy Voting

**Stockholder may designate proxy**:
- By signed proxy card or letter
- Proxy card specifies matters to be voted
- Proxy valid until revoked
- Proxy can be conditional (vote only if certain conditions met)

**Proxy Voting Restrictions**:
- Proxy must be from stockholder to person
- No proxy from proxy to another person (cannot chain proxies)
- Proxy revocable at any time

### 6.3 Voting Agreements - Lock-Up Voting

**Common and Preferred stockholders agree**:
- To coordinate voting to elect designated directors
- To coordinate voting on protective provisions
- To vote against competing proposals/proxies
- Not to vote differently without mutual discussion

**Exceptions to Lock-Up Voting**:
- If founder becomes competitor (can revote)
- If investor becomes competitor (can revote)
- If fiduciary duties require different vote
- If stockholder transferred their shares (no longer applies)

### 6.4 Deadlock Provisions

**If Board or stockholders in deadlock**:
- Either party may request mediation
- [__]-day mediation period with neutral mediator
- If not resolved, escalate to arbitration per Article IV

## ARTICLE VIII: TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS & VOTING AGREEMENTS

### 8.1 Voting Survives Transfer (Generally)

**Upon transfer of shares**:
- New stockholder must sign this Voting Agreement
- New stockholder assumes voting obligations
- Voting agreement binds new holder

**Exception**: Transfers to permitted assignees (typically affiliates of investor) may not require re-signing (per assignment language).

### 8.2 Non-Transferability of Director Designations

**Director designation rights do not transfer**:
- If founder sells common shares, designation right terminates
- If investor sells preferred shares, designation right terminates
- Remaining holders select new directors per this Agreement

### 8.3 Permitted Transfers

**Stockholder can transfer shares without consent if**:
- Transfer to affiliate (same ownership structure)
- Transfer to spouse or family member
- Transfer upon death (to estate/beneficiary)

**Other transfers require**:
- Consent of Board (per Right of First Refusal Agreement)
- Compliance with securities laws
