# Rewards Crowdfunding Campaign Timing Guide
## Strategic Launch Windows, Seasonal Trends & Platform Optimization

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: OPTIMAL TIMING FRAMEWORK

**Key Principle**: Crowdfunding success is 40% timing. Launch during peak consumer spending periods when platform traffic is highest and competition is lowest relative to backer activity.

**Golden Launch Windows (Data-Backed)**:
- **September 1-30**: Peak back-to-school season + holiday gift planning begins
- **January 15-31**: New Year's Resolutions peak, holiday budget residual
- **April 1-30**: Tax refund spending + spring cleaning mentality
- **October 15-November 15**: Pre-Black Friday + early holiday shopping

**Avoid These Periods**:
- July-August (summer vacation, budget fatigue)
- November 21-December 25 (Black Friday chaos, holiday gift shopping takes priority)
- December 26-January 14 (post-holiday budget depletion)

**Campaign Duration Sweet Spot**: 35-45 days (optimal for momentum maintenance and media cycles)

### B. MOMENTUM-BASED DURATION ADJUSTMENTS

**If Campaign Reaches Goal in <5 Days**:
- Maintain original 35-40 day schedule
- Strategy: Focus on stretch goals and community engagement
- Advantage: Extended campaign captures late-stage seasonal buyers
- Data: Extended campaigns post-goal raise 18-35% additional funds

**If Campaign Reaches 25-50% Goal by Day 7**:
- **Best Decision**: Maintain 35-40 days (compound growth phase)
- Analysis: Projects at 35% by day 7 have 76% success rate
- Optimization: Accelerate influencer + press push weeks 2-3

**If Campaign Below 20% Goal by Day 10**:
- **Critical Decision Point**: Either pivot strategy or extend timeline
- Extension Strategy: Extend to 45-50 days, drastically increase marketing
- Success Rate if Extended: 23% (vs. 8% if closed)
- Success Rate if Strategically Pivoted: 34% with new positioning
- Action Items: Modify messaging, add new reward tiers, increase influencer spend
- Data Point: Successful pivots occur on days 8-12 (optimal timing window)

**If Campaign Exceeds Goal by Day 7**:
- **Optimal Duration Remains**: Original 35-40 days
- Reasoning: Early success backers different from late-stage backers
- Late-stage Backer Profile: 40% are gift-buyers (Oct-Dec season dependent)
- Additional Revenue Pattern: 35-45% of total funding comes in final 2 weeks
- Strategy: Plan 4-5 stretch goals in advance

### B. OPTIMAL LAUNCH TIME CALCULATIONS

**Time Zone Optimization Formula**:

For global campaigns, calculate optimal launch time by:
1. Identify top 3 audience geographies (by current follower location)
2. Calculate overlap zones when all 3 are in active hours (8 AM - 8 PM local)
3. **For US-Primary Audience**: Launch Sunday 8-10 AM PST (10 AM ET, 3-5 PM GMT)
4. **For US+EU Audience**: Launch Tuesday 6-8 AM PST (9 AM ET, 2-4 PM GMT)
5. **For US+APAC Audience**: Launch Sunday 6 AM PST (Tuesday 12-2 AM JST/AEST)

**Recommended Launch Times by Platform**:

| Platform | Day | Time (PST) | Time (EST) | Time (GMT) | Rationale |
|----------|-----|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Kickstarter | Sunday | 8 AM | 11 AM | 4 PM | Algorithm reset, low competition |
| Indiegogo | Tuesday | 10 AM | 1 PM | 6 PM | Staff Picks refresh, EUR morning |
| Either | Monday | 6 AM | 9 AM | 2 PM | Catch Asia Monday close + US Monday |

## PART 8: RISK MITIGATION & CONTINGENCY TIMING

### A. IF YOUR CAMPAIGN UNDERPERFORMS

**Days 7-10 Decision Point**:
- **Metric**: Below 20% of goal
- **Option 1**: Extend campaign by 10 additional days (extend to 45-50 days total)
  - Success Rate if Extended: 23% (vs 8% if closed)
  - New Deadline: Notify platform of extension by Day 8
  - Messaging Pivot: Emphasize product improvements, team dedication

- **Option 2**: Radical Pivot in positioning/messaging
  - Success Rate with Major Pivot: 34% with relaunch
  - Process: Close campaign, learn lessons, relaunch in 30 days
  - Timeline: Close original, relaunch 30 days later in different season
  - Best New Timing: Next golden window (Oct 15+ or Jan 15+ if original was Apr)

**Days 14-21 Critical Decision**:
- **Metric**: At 35-50% of goal (on pace to succeed or miss)
- **If On Pace (50%+)**:
  - Action: Maintain strategy, execute stretch goals
  - Messaging: Emphasize early success, build momentum
  - Timeline: Continue full 35-40 days

- **If Below Pace (<50%)**:
  - Action: Emergency marketing push + influencer surge
  - Increase: Double paid ad spend for 7-10 days
  - Timeline: Make major push days 15-25 (middle momentum)
  - Success Rate: 29% recovery rate for campaigns below 50% by day 15

### B. EXTERNAL FACTORS & CONTINGENCY PLANNING

**Economic Downturn or Market Shock**:
- **If Recession/Market Downturn Announced**:
  - Recommendation: Delay to next golden window (3+ months out)
  - Messaging Strategy: Focus on affordability, value proposition
  - Platform Shift: Consider Indiegogo (flexible funding = less risk perception)
  - Delay to: January 2026 or April 2026 (tax refund season)

**Competitive Launch Timing**:
- **If Major Competitor Launches Same Category/Window**:
  - Action: Shift launch by 2-3 weeks earlier or later
  - Early Shift: Move to previous golden window if calendar allows
  - Late Shift: Move to next golden window (3+ months)
  - Differentiation Strategy: Emphasize unique positioning, superior execution

**Supply Chain Disruptions**:
- **If Manufacturing Delays Announced**:
  - Action: Adjust fulfillment timeline in campaign, NOT launch date
  - Transparency: Be upfront about delays in campaign description
  - Timeline: Only delay launch if cannot guarantee delivery dates
  - Communication: Update backers with real-time shipping updates during campaign

**Platform Issues or Algorithm Changes**:
- **If Platform Experiences Outages**:
  - Action: Delay launch 24-48 hours
  - Timing: Use backup launch date (Friday if originally Sunday)
  - Notification: Update email list and influencers immediately

- **If Platform Launches New Algorithm/Features**:
  - Advantage: New creators can gain visibility
  - Strategy: Monitor platform blog (check weekly: https://www.kickstarter.com/blog)
  - Action: Timing flexibility to take advantage of new features
  - Lead Time: Wait 2 weeks after feature launch to launch (let algorithm stabilize)
