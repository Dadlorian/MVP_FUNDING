# Family Office Non-Equity Deal: Term Sheet Templates and Legal Language

## Overview

This document provides actual legal language and template term sheets for common family office non-equity structures: revenue share agreements, licensing agreements, prepayment agreements, and philanthropic grants. These templates are based on real deals and are meant to be starting points for legal conversations, not final documents.

**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**: These are educational templates. Do not execute without professional legal review by a securities attorney licensed in your jurisdiction. Family office structures vary by state/country and may trigger securities regulations.

---

## Structure 1: Revenue Share Agreement

### When to Use This Structure

- Company has revenue or clear path to revenue
- Family office wants ongoing participation in upside
- Company wants to avoid equity dilution
- 3-7 year commitment acceptable
- Revenue is measurable and verifiable

### Sample Term Sheet: Revenue Share

```
CONFIDENTIAL

FAMILY OFFICE REVENUE SHARE INVESTMENT TERM SHEET

Date: [DATE]

Investor: [FAMILY OFFICE NAME] ("Investor")
Company: [COMPANY NAME], a [State] [Entity Type] ("Company")
Company Contact: [FOUNDER NAME], [TITLE] ([EMAIL], [PHONE])

---

1. INVESTMENT STRUCTURE

1.1 Investment Components
The Investor will provide capital to Company as follows:

Option A: Pure Revenue Share
- Amount: $[_________]
- Structure: [X]% of gross monthly revenue
- Term: [X] years from funding date
- Termination: Upon receipt of $[_________] total (cap)

Option B: Grant + Revenue Share (Recommended)
- Grant Component: $[_________] ([X]% of total commitment)
- Revenue Share Component: $[_________] ([X]% of total commitment)
- Revenue Share Terms: [X]% of gross monthly revenue for [X] years
- Total Commitment: $[_________]

1.2 Capital Deployment
Investor to provide capital according to following schedule:
- [Date 1]: $[_________] (upon execution of definitive agreements)
- [Date 2]: $[_________] (upon achievement of [milestone])
- [Date 3]: $[_________] (upon [milestone])

---

2. REVENUE SHARE TERMS

2.1 Revenue Definition
"Revenue" shall mean all payments received by Company from customers for products and services,
specifically defined as:

a) Subscription fees and recurring charges
b) Professional services fees
c) License fees
d) Usage-based fees or consumption charges
e) Transaction-based revenue

"Revenue" shall NOT include:
- Refunds or returns (authorized within [30/60] days of original transaction)
- Sales taxes, VAT, or other government-collected fees
- Payments passed through to third parties (e.g., payment processing fees, partner revenue shares)
- Intercompany revenue or affiliate transactions
- Investment income or non-operating income

Revenue shall be calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP),
specifically ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers).

2.2 Revenue Share Calculation

Monthly Revenue Share = (Total Gross Revenue for Month) × (Revenue Share Percentage)

Company shall provide monthly revenue reports by [5th business day] of following month,
including:
- Gross revenue by product/service line
- Major customer names and amounts (customers >$5K/month)
- Any significant changes from prior month
- Reconciliation to audited/reviewed financial statements

2.3 Revenue Share Cap

Revenue share payments shall cease upon Investor's receipt of cumulative revenue share
payments equal to $[_________] (the "Cap"). Company shall provide notice within 5 business days
when 80% of Cap is reached, and within 1 business day when Cap is reached.

This Cap provides important certainty to both parties: Investor knows maximum exposure,
Company knows maximum obligation.

2.4 Revenue Share Payment Schedule

Revenue share payments shall be made monthly via [wire transfer/ACH] to Investor's designated
account, due within [15] business days of month-end.

Payment details:
- Wire/ACH instructions: [to be provided by Investor]
- Contact for questions: [Name] ([Email], [Phone])

If payment is late:
- 1-5 days late: No penalty, but Investor may request call to discuss
- 6-10 days late: 1.5% monthly interest on unpaid amount
- 11+ days late: 1.5% monthly interest + Investor may accelerate remaining payments

Late payment interest caps out at [8%] annually.

---

3. USE OF CAPITAL

Grant component (if applicable) shall be used exclusively for:
- Product development and engineering
- Hiring of core team (engineering, product, sales)
- Infrastructure and third-party services
- Marketing to acquire customers

Company shall provide quarterly summary of how capital was deployed, tied to planned uses.
Any major deviations from planned use (>$50K shift) shall require Investor approval.

---

4. FINANCIAL INFORMATION AND REPORTING RIGHTS

4.1 Monthly Reporting (due by [5th business day] after month-end)
- Revenue report (see Section 2.2)
- Brief business update (2-3 sentences on key accomplishments/challenges)
- Any significant customer wins/losses
- Run rate revenue and month-over-month growth

4.2 Quarterly Reporting (due within [30] days of quarter-end)
- Detailed P&L statement (actual vs. budget)
- Cash flow summary
- Updated customer list and ARR/MRR calculation
- Strategic update: progress on key initiatives, market traction
- Challenges and mitigation plans

4.3 Annual Reporting (due within [60] days of year-end)
- Audited or reviewed financial statements (prepared by third-party accountant)
- Annual report to all investors
- In-person meeting with Investor (30-45 minutes)
- Updated financial projections for next 2 years

4.4 Quarterly Business Calls
Company shall arrange quarterly video calls ([30-45] minutes) with Investor to discuss:
- Financial performance vs. projections
- Product roadmap and upcoming releases
- Competitive landscape changes
- Revenue share run rate and pathway to cap
- Any concerns or questions Investor may have

Calls to occur within [30] days of quarter-end. Company to provide agenda [7] days in advance.

4.5 Information Access
Company shall provide Investor with:
- Reasonable access to Company facilities
- Access to major customers for reference calls (if needed for due diligence)
- Access to board materials and minutes (if Investor has board seat)
- Annual audited financial statements

---

5. GOVERNANCE AND INVESTOR RIGHTS

5.1 Information Rights (Investor entitled to):
- Quarterly revenue statements and business updates (as per Section 4)
- Quarterly video calls with Investor
- Annual in-person meeting
- Notice of any material adverse change in business
- Copies of all material customer contracts (on confidential basis)

5.2 Board Seat (Optional - only if revenue share >$[500K] or special arrangement)
If included: Investor shall have right to appoint [1] board observer who may:
- Attend all board meetings (in observer capacity)
- Receive board materials and minutes
- Participate in discussions (non-voting)

Board observer is bound by confidentiality and fiduciary duties.

5.3 Protective Provisions (Investor approval required for):
- Sale of Company (entire company)
- Merger or major acquisition
- Liquidation of Company assets
- Change of control transaction
- Issuance of equity securities >$[500K]
- Related party transactions >$[100K]

Investor approval not to be unreasonably withheld for actions in ordinary course of business.

5.4 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)
If Company raises future equity investment, Investor shall have ROFR to maintain pro-rata
ownership interest. If Investor declines ROFR, revenue share agreement remains in full effect.

---

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 Term
Agreement shall commence on [CLOSING DATE] and continue for [5/7] years ("Initial Term"),
unless earlier terminated per Section 6.3.

6.2 Renewal
Upon expiration of Initial Term (if Cap not yet reached), parties shall negotiate in good
faith regarding continued revenue share or transition to other relationship structure.

If parties cannot agree within [60] days of term expiration, agreement shall terminate and
Company shall have no further revenue share obligations.

6.3 Termination Scenarios

Early Termination by Investor (if):
a) Company materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within [30] days of written notice
b) Company fails to deliver revenue reports for [2] consecutive months
c) Company fails to deliver required payments for [2] consecutive months
d) Company is acquired or merged with major competitor (per Section 5.3 requirements)

Upon early termination, all remaining revenue share obligations continue until Cap is reached.

Early Termination by Company:
- Company may terminate upon [90] days written notice, provided Company pays Investor
  remaining amount to reach [80%] of Cap as lump sum payment
- Or alternatively, Company continues payments until Cap naturally reached

Examples:
- If Investor entitled to $500K total cap, and $300K has been paid:
  Company owes remaining $200K upon 90-day notice termination
- Or Company continues payments until $500K naturally reached

6.4 Termination Due to Company Acquisition
If Company is acquired:
- If purchase price <$[2M]: Acquirer assumes revenue share obligation OR Company pays
  Investor immediately (lump sum) to reach [80%] of Cap
- If purchase price >$[2M]: Acquirer assumes revenue share obligation OR pays out at
  [X]% of acquisition price (to be negotiated)

---

7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Company Representations
Company represents and warrants that:
- Company is duly formed and authorized to do business
- Execution of this Agreement is authorized
- Company owns or controls all technology/IP underlying products
- Company has not received any written notice of IP claims
- Financial information provided is complete and accurate
- No material adverse change has occurred since [date]
- Company is in compliance with all laws and regulations

7.2 Investor Representations
Investor represents and warrants that:
- Investor has authority to make this investment
- Investor is accredited investor (if applicable)
- Investor has completed own due diligence
- Investor understands risks of this investment

---

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Mutual Confidentiality
Both parties agree that all financial information, customer information, and business details
shared under this Agreement are confidential.

Exceptions:
- Information required to be disclosed to accountants, tax advisors, attorneys
- Information required by law or court order
- Information already publicly available
- Information disclosed with prior written consent

8.2 Use of Investor Name
Company may identify Investor as "non-equity investor" in marketing materials, unless
Investor requests confidentiality.

---

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Revenue Calculation Disputes
If Investor disputes revenue calculation:
- Investor must object within [30] days of receiving monthly report
- Parties shall meet and attempt resolution within [15] days
- If unresolved, parties shall hire independent CPA to audit revenue for [that month/that quarter]
- Cost of audit split equally
- Auditor's determination shall be binding

9.2 Mediation
Before litigation, parties agree to mediate disputes through [mediator/service] for [1-2] months.

9.3 Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by laws of [State], without regard to conflicts of law.
Any litigation shall be in [County] courts.

---

10. MISCELLANEOUS

10.1 Amendment
This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

10.2 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes entire agreement between parties regarding non-equity investment,
superseding all prior discussions and proposals.

10.3 Successors
This Agreement binds and benefits successors and assigns of both parties.

Investor may not assign without Company consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).
Company may not assign without Investor consent.

10.4 Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts (electronic signatures acceptable).

---

SIGNATURE PAGE

By signing below, both parties acknowledge they have reviewed this Agreement with legal
counsel (or had opportunity to do so), understand the terms, and agree to be bound.

INVESTOR:

[FAMILY OFFICE NAME]

By: _________________________________
Name: _______________________________
Title: ________________________________
Date: ________________________________


COMPANY:

[COMPANY NAME]

By: _________________________________
Name: _______________________________
Title: CEO
Date: ________________________________
```

## Structure 2: Licensing Agreement

### When to Use This Structure

- Geographic territory or customer segment exclusivity desired
- Licensee (family office) will operate/distribute product
- Long-term relationship desired (10+ years typical)
- Family office has operational/distribution expertise
- Clear performance metrics can be defined

### Sample Term Sheet: Exclusive Licensing

```
EXCLUSIVE LICENSE AGREEMENT
(Family Office Territory)

This Exclusive License Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [DATE] by and between:

LICENSOR: [COMPANY NAME], a [State] [Entity Type] ("Company")
LICENSEE: [FAMILY OFFICE/OPERATING ENTITY NAME] ("Licensee")

---

1. GRANT OF LICENSE

1.1 Exclusive License
Company hereby grants Licensee an exclusive, non-transferable license to:
- Use Company's [Product/Service Name] ("Licensed Product")
- Operate in [Geographic Territory] (the "Territory")
- Serve customers within the Territory

Territory definition:
- Geographic: [State/Region/Countries]
- Customer segments: [if applicable - e.g., "mid-market manufacturers" or "all customers"]
- Customer size: [optional - e.g., "companies with 50-500 employees"]

1.2 License Rights
Licensee may:
- Market and sell Licensed Product in Territory
- Use Company's brand name (with approval per Section 3.1)
- Hire and train sales/service teams
- Collect payments directly from customers
- Operate company-hosted or self-hosted deployment (per Company approval)

Licensee may NOT:
- Sell/license to customers outside Territory
- Sublicense or transfer to third parties without Company written consent
- Reverse engineer or modify Company IP
- Disclose proprietary information outside licensed operations

1.3 License Fee (Upfront Payment to Company)
Licensee shall pay Company $[_________] upon execution of this Agreement as upfront license fee.
This payment is non-refundable.

---

2. FINANCIAL TERMS

2.1 Revenue Share / Royalty Payments

Monthly Royalty = (Territory Revenue Collected by Licensee) × (Royalty Rate)

Royalty Rate: [X]% of gross revenue from Territory customers

"Gross Revenue" includes:
- All customer payments for Licensed Product (subscriptions, per-seat, usage-based)
- Fees for professional services in Territory
- Implementation and deployment fees
- Training and support fees

"Gross Revenue" excludes:
- Refunds and credits (<[30] days of transaction)
- Value-added taxes and sales taxes
- Payment processing fees
- Revenue from non-Territory customers

2.2 Revenue Royalty Payment Schedule
Royalties due monthly within [20] business days of month-end, calculated as:

1. Licensee calculates Territory gross revenue
2. Licensee applies Royalty Rate
3. Licensee wires or ACHs payment to Company
4. Licensee provides monthly report with calculation details

Example:
- Territory revenue Month 1: $50,000
- Royalty rate: 15%
- Royalty due: $7,500
- Due date: [20th business day of following month]

2.3 Minimum Annual Guarantee (MAG)
To ensure both parties are committed, Licensee shall generate minimum Territory revenue as follows:
- Year 1: $[500,000] minimum revenue
- Year 2: $[1,000,000] minimum revenue
- Year 3+: $[1,000,000] minimum revenue annually

If actual Territory revenue falls short of MAG, Licensee shall pay the shortfall to Company
within [30] days of year-end.

If MAG not met for 2 consecutive years, Company may:
- Terminate this Agreement (per Section 4.2), OR
- Renegotiate Territory or royalty rates

Exception: If Market Downturn affects entire industry (documented by third-party research),
parties shall negotiate in good faith regarding MAG adjustment.

2.4 Quarterly True-Up and Adjustment
Company may audit Licensee's revenue calculations quarterly. If discrepancy >5%, cost of
reconciliation borne by Licensee.

---

3. COMPANY SUPPORT AND OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Product Support
Company shall:
- Provide technical support for Licensed Product ([X] hours per week, [X] days per week)
- Deliver quarterly product updates and enhancements
- Maintain uptime SLA of [X]% (if Company hosts)
- Provide training for Licensee's teams (initial [X] hours included, additional at $[XXX]/hour)
- Respond to critical technical issues within [4] business hours

3.2 Product Roadmap
Company shall share product roadmap [quarterly] with Licensee and solicit feedback.
Licensee has right to request [up to X features per year] for Territory-specific needs.
Company will prioritize Territory needs in roadmap (good faith basis).

3.3 Marketing and Brand Usage
Company shall:
- Provide Licensee with marketing materials (brochures, case studies, templates)
- Allow use of Company name/logo in Territory marketing (subject to brand guidelines)
- Support Territory customer reference calls (up to [X] per quarter]
- Highlight Licensee as Territory partner in Company marketing (where appropriate)

Company shall not:
- Market directly to customers in Territory
- Support competitors in Territory (covered by exclusivity)
- Disclose Licensee's Territory performance publicly without approval

---

4. TERM AND TERMINATION

4.1 Initial Term
License granted for [10] year period beginning [DATE] ("Initial Term").

4.2 Renewal
Upon expiration of Initial Term, license shall automatically renew for successive [5] year
periods unless either party provides [180] days written notice of non-renewal.

4.3 Early Termination for Cause

Company may terminate for cause if:
a) Licensee fails to pay royalties for [2] consecutive months
b) Licensee materially breaches confidentiality or non-compete obligations
c) Licensee fails to meet MAG for [2] consecutive years
d) Licensee transfers license to third party without Company consent
e) Licensee files bankruptcy or becomes insolvent

Cure period: [30] days after written notice (except for confidentiality breach or bankruptcy).

4.4 Termination Without Cause
Either party may terminate without cause upon [180] days written notice, provided:
- If Company terminates: Licensee has [60] days to transition customers, then transfers
  customer list and accounts to Company
- If Licensee terminates: No additional compensation owed

4.5 Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- License rights immediately cease
- Licensee shall not solicit Territory customers for competing products within [6] months
- Licensee shall provide all customer data and usage information to Company
- Company may reclaim Territory customers and offer existing/new products
- All unpaid royalties immediately due within [15] days

---

5. PERFORMANCE METRICS AND ACCOUNTABILITY

5.1 Territory Revenue Targets
Licensee and Company shall establish annual Territory revenue targets, reviewed quarterly:

Target for Year 1: $[_________]
Target for Year 2: $[_________]
Target for Year 3: $[_________]

These are targets (not guarantees), but Company may inquire if actual falls >25% below
target for [2] consecutive quarters.

5.2 Territory Investment Commitments
Licensee commits to:
- Sales team of [at least X people] dedicated to Territory
- Marketing budget of [at least $X or X% of revenue] for Territory promotion
- Customer success/support team of [at least X people]

If Licensee falls below staffing commitments, Company may:
- Reduce exclusivity to market non-exclusively in Territory, OR
- Require renegotiation of royalty rates

5.3 Customer Satisfaction and Quality Standards
Licensee shall maintain:
- Customer NPS score of [at least X]
- Support response time of [X hours for critical issues]
- Monthly system uptime of [X]% (if Licensee hosts)
- Compliance with all data protection and privacy laws

If Licensee falls below standards, Company may:
- Provide additional training and support
- Audit customer satisfaction
- In extreme cases, seek alternative Territory operator

---

6. TERRITORY EXCLUSIVITY AND NON-COMPETE

6.1 Company Exclusivity Obligation
During term of this Agreement, Company shall NOT:
- Directly market or sell Licensed Product to customers in Territory
- License to other parties within Territory
- Market competing products in Territory

Exception: Company may maintain relationships with existing customers who operated
in Territory before license grant (existing customer accounts).

6.2 Licensee Non-Compete Obligation
Licensee shall NOT:
- Promote or sell competing products within Territory during [this Agreement + 2 years after]
- Solicit Territory customers to purchase competing products
- Facilitate Territory customers' use of competing products

For purposes of this clause, "competing products" means [defined list of competing products
and product categories].

6.3 Territory Boundary Disputes
If customer location is ambiguous (e.g., multi-location company), parties shall determine
together based on:
- Customer's primary place of business
- If unclear, where customer's procurement happens
- Good faith determination by Company (with Licensee input)

---

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

7.1 Company IP Ownership
All intellectual property in Licensed Product remains owned by Company, including:
- Software code and algorithms
- Documentation
- Brand names and logos
- Trademarks

Licensee may not modify, create derivative works, or claim ownership.

7.2 Territory Customizations
If Licensee creates Territory-specific customizations or modifications:
- Licensee owns the customization code/work product
- Company receives non-exclusive license to use Territory customizations
- Licensee must offer Company opportunity to fold customizations into standard product

7.3 Feedback and Suggestions
Any feedback Licensee provides regarding product improvements is owned by Company
and may be used without further compensation.

---

8. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

8.1 Company Reps
Company represents and warrants that:
- Company owns or has rights to all IP in Licensed Product
- Licensed Product does not infringe any third-party IP
- Company has authority to grant this license
- Company is not bound by conflicting obligations

8.2 Licensee Reps
Licensee represents and warrants that:
- Licensee has authority to execute this Agreement
- Licensee has financial capability to meet MAG obligations
- Licensee will operate in compliance with all laws
- Licensee has no conflicting obligations

---

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Mutual Confidentiality
Both parties shall maintain confidentiality of:
- Financial terms of this Agreement
- Territory revenue/performance data
- Product roadmap and feature plans
- Customer information (names, usage, issues)

Permitted disclosures:
- To employees/contractors on need-to-know basis
- To accountants and tax advisors
- To legal counsel
- If required by law

---

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 Revenue Calculation Disputes
If dispute over royalty calculation:
- Company must object within [30] days of receiving monthly report
- Parties meet and attempt resolution
- If unresolved, hire independent CPA to audit Territory revenue for dispute period
- CPA's determination is binding
- Costs split equally

10.2 Governing Law
Agreement governed by laws of [State], without regard to conflicts of law principles.

---

SIGNATURE PAGE

[Signatures as in Revenue Share Agreement template above]

```

## Structure 3: Prepayment / Advance Purchase Agreement

### When to Use This Structure

- Hardware company or capital equipment provider
- Family office will purchase/use product themselves
- Near-term delivery timeline (6-18 months)
- Clear specifications and milestone-based delivery
- Family office wants discount on bulk purchase

### Sample Term Sheet: Advance Purchase Agreement

```
ADVANCE PURCHASE AGREEMENT
(Family Office Equipment/Product)

This Advance Purchase Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of [DATE] between:

SELLER: [COMPANY NAME], a [State] [Entity Type] ("Company")
BUYER: [FAMILY OFFICE/OPERATING ENTITY NAME] ("Buyer")

---

1. PURCHASE COMMITMENT

1.1 Product and Quantity
Buyer commits to purchase:
- Product: [Hardware/Equipment Description]
- Quantity: [X] units
- Unit price: $[X] (prepaid in advance; represents [X]% discount from expected retail price)
- Total commitment: $[_________]

1.2 Product Specifications
Product shall meet specifications attached as Exhibit A:
- Performance specifications: [listed]
- Quality standards: [listed]
- Testing and certification: [listed]

1.3 Unit Delivery Schedule

Company commits to deliver units per following schedule:

Month 1-3 (by [DATE]): [X] units at [Company location / Buyer location]
Month 4-6 (by [DATE]): [X] units
Month 7-12 (by [DATE]): [X] units
Month 13-18 (by [DATE]): [X] units

Total: [X] units by [DATE]

Delivery definition: Goods delivered FIB [Incoterms term], title and risk of loss transfer
to Buyer upon delivery to designated location.

---

2. PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 Advance Payment Schedule

Company shall invoice per following schedule:

- Upon execution: $[_________] ([X]%)
- Upon [Milestone 1 completion]: $[_________] ([X]%)
- Upon [Milestone 2 completion]: $[_________] ([X]%)
- Upon final delivery: $[_________] ([X]%)

Buyer shall pay invoices within [15] business days of invoice date via wire transfer.

2.2 Advance Payment Risk
Buyer acknowledges that prepayment represents advance capital to Company for:
- Manufacturing materials and labor
- Component procurement
- Production tooling
- Working capital

This capital is at-risk should Company fail to deliver per Section 4 (Termination Provisions).

2.3 No Refund Unless Non-Delivery
Except as provided in Section 4 (clawback for non-delivery), Buyer's advance payments
are NON-REFUNDABLE. Buyer cannot cancel order for convenience.

---

3. DELIVERY AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Delivery Logistics
Company shall deliver units to:
- Location: [Address]
- Contact: [Name], [Phone], [Email]
- Packaging: [Specifications for packaging/handling]

3.2 Buyer Acceptance
Buyer shall have [10] business days after delivery to inspect units for:
- Conformity with specifications (Exhibit A)
- Damage in transit
- Defects or malfunctions

If units meet specifications, Buyer shall issue written acceptance and retain units.
If units fail to meet specifications, Buyer shall provide detailed rejection notice,
and Company shall replace within [15] days at Company's cost.

3.3 Installation and Support
Company shall provide:
- [X] hours of technical installation support
- Training for Buyer's team ([X] hours)
- Documentation and user manuals
- [X] days of technical support post-delivery

Additional support available at $[XXX] per hour.

---

4. DELIVERY MILESTONES AND CLAWBACK PROVISIONS

4.1 Delivery Milestones
Delivery milestones ("Milestones") are critical to this Agreement:

Milestone 1 (by [DATE]): [X] units delivered
- If not met: Company shall pay Buyer $[X] liquidated damages within [15] days
- Adjusted new deadline: [X] days later

Milestone 2 (by [DATE]): [X] units delivered
- If not met: Company shall pay Buyer $[X] liquidated damages within [15] days

Milestone 3 (by [DATE]): All [X] units delivered
- If not met by [extended date]: Buyer may clawback [X]% of prepayment
- Details: See Section 4.3 below

4.2 Force Majeure Exceptions
Milestones excused if non-delivery caused by:
- Major supplier failure (documented)
- Natural disaster or pandemic
- Government action preventing manufacture
- Transportation disruption (not within Company's control)

In Force Majeure event, Milestones shall be delayed by documented delay period (not to exceed [90] days).

4.3 Non-Delivery Clawback
If Company fails to deliver [50%] of units by [DATE] + [90 days], Buyer may require
Company to refund [_X_%] of advance payment within [20] days.

Clawback calculation:
- For every [30] days late beyond grace period: [X]% of total payment due back
- Maximum clawback: [X]% of total payment

Example: If $400K prepaid, 50% due by Month 12, and Company delivers Month 15:
- [90] days late = [X]% clawback = $[X] refunded to Buyer

4.4 Remedies for Non-Delivery
If Company fails to deliver clawed-back units, Buyer's remedies:
- Right to purchase equivalent product from third party at market price
- Company reimburses Buyer for incremental cost (market price - prepaid price)
- Company pays 1.5% monthly interest on refund amount from clawback date until payment

---

5. PRODUCT WARRANTY

5.1 Manufacturer's Warranty
Company warrants that all units shall:
- Be free from defects in materials and workmanship for [24] months post-delivery
- Perform in accordance with specifications (Exhibit A) for [24] months
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

5.2 Warranty Coverage
During [24] month warranty period, Company shall:
- Repair or replace defective units at no cost to Buyer
- Provide replacement units within [10] business days of receiving defective unit
- Cover shipping costs for both directions

5.3 Warranty Exclusions
Warranty does NOT cover:
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from misuse, neglect, or accident
- Modifications not approved by Company
- Use outside specified environmental conditions
- Third-party components (covered under manufacturer warranties)

5.4 Software and Updates
For [24] months post-delivery, Company shall provide:
- Quarterly software updates and patches
- Bug fixes and security updates
- Telephone and email support

---

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND RIGHTS

6.1 IP Ownership
Company retains all intellectual property rights in:
- Hardware design and manufacturing specifications
- Firmware and embedded software
- Documentation and training materials

6.2 License Grant
Company grants Buyer limited license to:
- Use units solely for Buyer's internal business operations
- Reproduce documentation for Buyer's internal use only

Buyer may NOT:
- Sublicense to third parties
- Reverse engineer or disassemble units
- Create derivative works
- Resell or lease units to third parties

6.3 Buyer Ownership
Buyer owns the physical hardware units once delivered and accepted.
Buyer may:
- Use units as needed
- Transfer units between Buyer's own locations
- Integrate with Buyer's own systems (if compatible)

---

7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

7.1 Company Representations
Company represents and warrants:
- Company has authority to sell product
- Product design and manufacturing does not infringe third-party IP
- Company shall comply with all applicable laws
- Delivery timeline is realistic based on Company's current capacity

7.2 Buyer Representations
Buyer represents and warrants:
- Buyer has financial capability to pay prepayment obligations
- Buyer is purchasing for legitimate business use
- Buyer shall comply with all applicable laws (including export controls, if applicable)

---

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties shall maintain confidentiality of:
- Product specifications and roadmap
- Unit pricing and discount structure
- Delivery timeline and milestones
- Any technical specifications or use cases

---

9. TERMINATION

9.1 Termination by Company
Company may terminate only for:
- Buyer's material breach (failure to pay when due)
- Bankruptcy or insolvency of Buyer
- Legal prohibition preventing delivery

9.2 Termination by Buyer
Buyer may NOT terminate for convenience. However:

9.3 Effect of Termination

- All delivery obligations cease
- Undelivered units are not owed
- Buyer's clawback rights apply per Section 4.3

- Parties shall meet to attempt resolution
- If unresolved after [30] days, independent third-party inspector shall assess conformity
- Inspector's determination is binding
```

- Multi-year grant possible

## Key Negotiation Points Across All Templates

### What Family Offices Usually Want:

1. **Clear Revenue/Impact Definition** - Explicitly defined metrics so no disputes later
2. **Information Rights** - Regular reporting to monitor investment
3. **Governance Access** - Board seat or observer status (especially for large checks)
4. **Protective Provisions** - Control over major decisions (sale, merger, etc.)
5. **Clawback Provisions** - Way to get capital back if things go wrong
6. **Reasonable Caps** - Don't want unlimited obligation (especially revenue share)

### What Founders Usually Want:

1. **Speed** - Close in 8-12 weeks, not 6 months
2. **Flexibility** - Adjust terms if business situation changes
3. **Clarity** - Know exactly what obligations are
4. **No Dilution** - Keep 100% equity
5. **Minimal Governance** - Don't want family office in the weeds
6. **Long Runway** - Multi-year term so predictable

### Common Negotiation Compromises:

| Family Office Ask | Founder Counter | Typical Middle Ground |
|---|---|---|
| 5% revenue share, unlimited term | 2% revenue share, 3 years | 3% revenue share, 5 years with cap |
| Board seat with voting rights | No board seat | Board observer, non-voting |
| $1M cap on payouts | No cap | $750K cap with extension option |
| 90-day notice on any change | 180-day notice | 120-day notice with good-faith discussion |
| Monthly reporting | Quarterly reporting | Monthly financials, quarterly calls |
| Unrestricted ROFR on equity rounds | No ROFR | ROFR if raise >$[X] |
| All customers as revenue base | Select customers for revenue base | Clearly defined customer segments |

## Important Legal Warnings

1. **State Variations**: Securities laws vary by state. Revenue share may be considered a security. Consult lawyer in your jurisdiction.

2. **Tax Implications**: Different structures have different tax treatments for both company and family office. Consult tax advisor.

3. **ERISA Considerations**: If family office is a retirement plan/ERISA account, additional rules may apply.

4. **Accredited Investor**: Some structures may require investor to be "accredited." Confirm with family office.

5. **Material Adverse Change**: Include MACs (material adverse change) clauses for company protection.

6. **Amendment Procedures**: All templates should require written amendment. Don't modify verbally.
