### Strategy for Accessing Single Family Offices

**Direct Contact Methods**:
- Chief Investment Officer (CIO) - primary decision-maker for investments
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO) - controls cash flow and operations
- Head of Investments - day-to-day investment decisions
- Chief Operating Officer (COO) - operational efficiency improvements

**Identification Sources**:
- **Family Office Directories**:
  - https://www.soa.org (Society of Professional Asset Managers)
  - https://familyofficeprofessionals.com (Family Office Professionals Association)

  - https://www.uhnwdatabase.com (Ultra-High Net Worth Database)

### Single Family Office Engagement Timeline

**Phase 1: Research & Identification (4-6 weeks)**
- T-6 weeks: Identify target single family offices (10-15 key targets)
- T-5 weeks: Research CIO and investment team background
- T-4 weeks: Identify mutual connections through LinkedIn or advisors
- T-3 weeks: Gather contact information and verify decision-makers

**Phase 2: Warm Introduction Period (3-4 weeks)**
- T-3 weeks: Request warm introduction from mutual contact
- T-2 weeks: Wait for connection to facilitate introduction
- T-1 week: Receive introduction and confirm conversation timing
- T-0: Initial conversation with CIO or investment officer

**Phase 3: Pitch & Presentation (3-4 weeks)**
- Week 1: Initial 20-minute exploratory call
- Week 2: Send comprehensive pitch deck + one-page summary
- Week 3: Request 45-minute detailed presentation meeting
- Week 4: Conduct presentation + Q&A

**Phase 4: Due Diligence & Negotiation (6-10 weeks)**
- Weeks 1-2: Provide background materials and references
- Weeks 3-5: Family office conducts internal due diligence
- Weeks 6-8: Management team meetings with family office investment team
- Weeks 9-10: Term sheet negotiation and documentation

**Total Timeline for Single FO Engagement**: 16-24 weeks from initial identification

- Family offices complete Q4 analysis
- Set annual investment themes and allocation targets
- Plan fund raises and new initiatives
- Budget for new commitments

**Action Items**: Pitch new opportunities, request initial meetings, present annual themes

**Timeline**: T-0 to T-2 weeks (initial contact to first meeting)

---

**March-April: Due Diligence Acceleration Phase**
- Fund selection and initial screening complete
- Formal due diligence processes begin
- Management team meetings scheduled
- Reference checks initiated

**Action Items**: Prepare comprehensive materials, schedule management meetings, provide references

**Timeline**: T-3 to T-8 weeks (presentations to due diligence decision)

---

**May-June: Commitment & Closing Phase**
- Final investment decisions made
- Documents prepared and executed
- Funds received
- Onboarding process begins

**Action Items**: Finalize legal terms, prepare closing documents, arrange capital calls

**Timeline**: T-8 to T-12 weeks (due diligence to fund close)

---

**July-August: Summer Slowdown & Execution Phase**
- Many family office executives on vacation
- Existing investments monitored
- Portfolio rebalancing occurs
- Pipeline review for H2

**Action Items**: Maintain relationships, provide quarterly updates, plan H2 strategy

**Timeline**: Limited new business activity; relationship maintenance focus

---

**September-October: Q4 Planning & Acceleration**
- Back-to-school effect; executives return
- Q4 planning begins
- Year-end tax planning starts
- Target allocation reviews

**Action Items**: Q3 updates, pitch new opportunities aligned with year-end planning, request meetings

---

**November-December: Year-End Close & Planning**

**Action Items**: Year-end performance updates, tax-efficient structure proposals, Q1 planning presentations

## Part 6: Strategic Outreach Timing by Investor Type

### Outreach to Chief Investment Officers (CIOs)

**Optimal Contact Months**: January-March, August-September
**Best Contact Method**: Warm introduction via mutual advisor
**Initial Meeting Duration**: 20-30 minutes (exploratory)
**Follow-up Meeting**: 45-60 minutes (strategy discussion)

**Email Timing Strategy**:
- Send outreach emails: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM (CIOs check email early)
- Avoid Mondays (catch-up on weekend emails)
- Avoid Fridays (end of week distraction)
- Avoid lunch hours (11:30 AM - 1:00 PM)

**Phone Outreach Timing**:
- Best times: 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM
- Avoid before 9 AM (morning reviews)
- Avoid 12-2 PM (lunch/calendar reviews)
- Avoid 4:30-5:30 PM (end of day wrap-up)

---

### Outreach to Chief Financial Officers (CFOs)

**Optimal Contact Months**: January-February (budget planning), August-September (fiscal prep)
**Best Contact Method**: Direct outreach or advisor referral
**Focus on**: Operational efficiency, cash flow management, liability management

**Timing Advantage**:
- Early January: Budget planning season
- Mid-August: Fiscal year preparation
- These are 2 peak planning periods when CFOs are most open to new strategies

---

### Outreach to Family Office Operations Managers

**Best Contact Method**: Conference networking, advisor recommendations
**Focus on**: Process efficiency, technology integration, compliance

**Advantage**: More stable availability throughout year

- **May-June**: First wave of capital deployment post-planning

---

### Market Conditions Affecting Timing

**Rising Rate Environment Timing**:
- Better reception for fixed income structures
- Appeal to floating rate investors
- Emphasis on liability management strategies
- Optimal pitch angle: "Portfolio diversification with attractive yield"

**Falling Rate Environment Timing**:
- Better reception for longer-duration structures
- Emphasis on locked-in yields
- Appeal to income-seeking families
- Optimal pitch angle: "Locked-in returns before rates fall further"

**Credit Spread Timing**:
- Wide spreads (>400 bps): Attractive for credit investors
- Tight spreads (<250 bps): Emphasis on capital preservation
- Credit deterioration: Flight-to-quality messaging
- Credit recovery: Opportunity angle

## Part 9: Document Preparation Timeline

### Pitch Materials Timeline (8-Week Preparation Cycle)

**Week 1: Strategy Definition**
- Define target family offices
- Identify decision-makers
- List required materials
- Create project plan

**Week 2-3: Executive Summary Creation**
- Write compelling pitch narrative (2-3 pages max)
- Include investment thesis and opportunity
- Define target investor profile
- Create one-page summary

**Week 4-5: Presentation Deck Development**
- Create 15-20 slide presentation deck
- Include team bios, track record, risk disclosure
- Add comparison to alternatives
- Include sample term sheets or structures

**Week 6: Materials Compilation**
- Annual reports and financial statements (3 years)
- Reference lists (3-5 existing investors)
- Team CVs and credentials
- Third-party certifications/audits

## Part 15: Critical Timing Mistakes to Avoid

### Timing Error #1: Pitching During Summer Vacations (June-August)
**Problem**: 60% of family office decision-makers on vacation
**Impact**: 90% non-response rate, relationships appear ignored
**Solution**:
- Plan vacation outreach in May (before vacations)
- Resume heavy outreach September 1
- Use summer for relationship maintenance, not new business

---

### Timing Error #2: Missing Conference Registration Deadlines
**Problem**:
- Early bird deadlines create better pricing and networking slots
- Late registration → limited meeting availability
**Solution**:
- Set calendar alerts 8 weeks before each conference
- Budget for early bird rates (often 30-40% savings)
- Register immediately upon deadline announcement

---

### Timing Error #3: Insufficient Lead Time for Due Diligence
**Problem**:
- Family offices require 6-10 weeks minimum for due diligence
- Rushed process signals lack of seriousness
**Solution**:
- Provide comprehensive materials within 1 week of pitch
- Proactively answer expected questions
- Allow 8+ weeks between pitch and expected close

---

### Timing Error #4: Poor Follow-Up Timing
**Problem**:
- Following up too quickly (within 24 hours) appears desperate
- Following up too slowly (2+ weeks) loses momentum
- Wrong day/time for follow-up emails
**Solution**:
- Initial follow-up: 48-72 hours after meeting
- Subsequent follow-ups: Every 5-7 business days
- Send on Tuesday-Thursday, 8-11 AM or 2-4 PM

---

### Timing Error #5: Not Accounting for Family Office Board Meetings
**Problem**:
- Major decisions require board approval
- Board meetings are scheduled quarterly (often in specific months)
- Missing the meeting = 3-month delay
**Solution**:
- Ask during initial meeting: "When are your board meeting dates?"
- Time pitches to arrive 4-6 weeks before board meetings
- Plan closing to occur within 2 weeks after board approval

---

### Timing Error #6: Neglecting Year-End Tax Timing
**Problem**:
- November-December decisions driven by year-end tax planning
- Tax-efficient structures close much faster Q4
**Solution**:
- Launch tax optimization campaigns September 15
- Emphasize tax benefits in Q4 pitches
- Be prepared for accelerated timelines (2-4 week closings possible)

---

### Timing Error #7: Not Following Conference-Cycle Momentum
**Problem**:
- Conference season (Feb-May, Sept-Nov) requires intensive coordination
- Off-season (June-Aug) requires different approach
- Missing the rhythm = lost opportunities
**Solution**:
- Plan heavy conference schedule Feb-May and Sept-Nov
- Use June-August for relationship maintenance and due diligence advancement
- Build 2-3 month pipelines in advance of conference season
