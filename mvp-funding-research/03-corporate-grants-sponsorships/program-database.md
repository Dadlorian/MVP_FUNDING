## How to Use This Database

### 1. Filter by Your Profile
- **Stage**: Look at funding amounts aligned with your stage
- **Focus Area**: Find programs in your industry/sector
- **Timeline**: Note application deadlines
- **Effort**: Some programs require detailed applications; others are quick

### 2. Priority Scoring System
**Quick Fit Assessment:**
- Amount matches your target? (High priority if yes)
- Strategic alignment? (High priority if direct)
- Easy application process? (Medium priority)
- No equity required? (Confirm for all)
- Good fit with your mission/business? (Essential)

### 3. Research Strategy
**Week 1: Tier 1 Programs**
- Pick 5 programs with highest fit scores
- Deep research: Read all guidelines, recent winners
- Contact program manager with questions
- Assess likelihood of success: 60%+?

**Week 2-3: Tier 2 Programs**
- Pick 10 additional programs
- Standard research and application
- Likelihood of success: 40-60%

**Week 4+: Tier 3 Programs**
- Applications to programs with moderate fit
- Long-shot opportunities that could pay off
- Likelihood of success: 20-40%

### 4. Application Timeline
- Start 3-4 weeks before deadline for complex applications
- Start 1 week before for simple form-based applications
- Leave time for reviews by advisors/mentors
- Submit early (avoid last-minute stress)

### 5. Track Progress
Use the tracking worksheets (tracking-worksheets.md) to:
- Log each program researched
- Track application status
- Monitor decision timelines
- Record outcomes for future applications
