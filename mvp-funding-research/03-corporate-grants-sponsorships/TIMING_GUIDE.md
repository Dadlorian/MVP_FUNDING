# Corporate Grants & Sponsorships - Timing Guide

## Overview
This comprehensive timing guide provides specific deadlines, application cycles, and key dates for corporate grants, sponsorships, and partnership opportunities. Most corporate funding operates on distinct calendar cycles—understanding these timelines is critical for success.

## Quick Reference: Application Processing Times

| Program Category | Typical Processing Time | Priority Level |
|---|---|---|
| **In-Kind Credits** (AWS, Microsoft, Google) | 24 hours - 3 weeks | HIGH - Apply immediately |
| **Rolling Programs** (Oracle, IBM, GitHub) | 1-4 weeks continuous | HIGH - No deadline pressure |
| **Cohort-Based Programs** (Start Path, Accelerators) | 2-4 weeks (fixed cohorts) | CRITICAL - Fixed deadlines |
| **Corporate Foundation Grants** | 4-16 weeks | MEDIUM - Quarterly review cycles |
| **Sponsorships/Pilots** | 4-12 weeks | MEDIUM - Budget-dependent |
| **Contracts/Procurement** | 4-8 weeks legal review | MEDIUM - Lengthy process |

## Budget Cycle Planning Calendar

### Understanding Corporate Budget Cycles

Most corporations operate on **annual budget cycles** (Jan-Dec) or **quarterly cycles** (Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4). Understanding these cycles is critical for timing sponsorship asks.

| Quarter | Corporate Activity | Optimal Sponsorship Ask Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** (Jan-Mar) | Budget locked from prior year; execution phase | Poor timing - budgets fixed |
| **Q2** (Apr-Jun) | Mid-year budget reviews; some flexibility available | Good timing - mid-year budget requests |
| **Q3** (Jul-Sep) | **BEST**: Planning season for next year's budget | **BEST**: Pitch for 2027 sponsorships |
| **Q4** (Oct-Dec) | Final decisions made for following year; "use it or lose it" | Good timing for leftover budget requests |

### 2026 Corporate Budget Timeline

**July-September 2026**: BEST SEASON FOR 2027 SPONSORSHIP PITCHES
- Corporate marketing/CSR teams planning 2027 budgets
- Sponsorship budgets being allocated
- Decision-makers actively reviewing opportunities
- **Action**: Execute all major sponsorship outreach July-September 2026

**October-December 2026**: Final 2027 Approvals
- Budgets being finalized
- Late approvals still possible
- "Use it or lose it" budget opportunities
- **Action**: Follow up on pending sponsorships; pitch remaining opportunities

**January-March 2027**: Budget Execution
- Budgets locked in for year
- Limited flexibility for new sponsorships
- **Action**: Execute on approved partnerships; begin 2028 planning

**April-June 2027**: Mid-Year Adjustments
- Some budget flexibility from program underutilization
- **Action**: Pitch for mid-year sponsorship adjustments; target underbudgeted initiatives

## Critical Timing Dos & Don'ts

### DO:
- ✅ Apply to in-kind programs on Day 1 (continuous rolling admissions)
- ✅ Pitch for annual sponsorships July-September (budget planning season)
- ✅ Target major conferences 4-6 months in advance (lead time required)
- ✅ Submit corporate grant proposals in Q3/Q4 (before year-end reviews)
- ✅ Plan for corporate legal/procurement (4-8 weeks minimum)
- ✅ Follow up 1-2 weeks after proposal submission
- ✅ Build relationships 6-12 months before asking for money
- ✅ Time your asks around corporate announcements (earnings calls, CSR reports)
- ✅ Renew corporate partnerships 4-6 months before contract expiration

### DON'T:
- ❌ Expect quick corporate decisions (6-18 month typical sales cycle)
- ❌ Ask for 2024 sponsorship in December 2023 (budgets already locked)
- ❌ Miss fixed application deadlines (cohort-based programs fill quickly)
- ❌ Ignore corporate procurement requirements (security reviews, insurance, vendor onboarding)
- ❌ Assume in-kind credits are "free money" (they have specific restrictions/expiration dates)
- ❌ Submit generic proposals (custom proposals have 30%+ vs. 5% success rates)
- ❌ Underestimate legal review time (corporate legal takes 4-8 weeks minimum)
- ❌ Contact only one person at corporation (relationship-dependent, staff turns over)
- ❌ Forget to report on deliverables (kills renewal chances)
- ❌ Assume first "no" is final (reapproach same corporation annually)
