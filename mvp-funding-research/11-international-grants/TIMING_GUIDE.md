## Strategic Timing Tips

### Best Practices for International Applications

1. **Plan 6-9 months ahead** - Most international programs require detailed proposals
   - Horizon Europe: 9+ months recommended
   - NSERC: 6-8 months minimum
   - Innovate UK: 8 weeks from open to deadline

2. **Align funding cycles** - Coordinate with partner countries' deadlines
   - EU-Canada calls typically align May-June
   - EUREKA clusters offer multiple calls per year
   - Bilateral agreements often coordinate deadlines

3. **Track rolling deadlines** - Many programs accept applications year-round
   - EIC Accelerator (Horizon Europe): Rolling with 2x/year deadlines
   - IRAP (Canada): Continuous intake through regional offices
   - DFG Individual Grants (Germany): Year-round

4. **Leverage consortium requirements** - International programs often require partnerships
   - Minimum 3 countries for Horizon Europe
   - Partner selection 4-6 months before deadline
   - Establish MOUs early (2-3 months pre-deadline)

5. **Consider time zones** - EU and Canada deadlines often conflict
   - Most EU deadlines: 17:00 Brussels time
   - Canadian deadlines: 17:00 EST/CST
   - Plan for documentation preparation spanning multiple time zones
