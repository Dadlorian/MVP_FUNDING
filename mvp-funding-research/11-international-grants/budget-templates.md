# Budget Templates for International Grant Programs

## Overview
Excel-ready budget templates aligned with EU, UK, and Canadian grant program requirements. These templates meet compliance standards for different funding bodies.

---

## 1. EU Horizon Europe Budget Template

### Structure & Requirements
- **Total Project Duration**: Maximum 60 months
- **Funding Model**: Lump sum, Flat rate, or Unit costs available
- **Cost Categories**: Personnel, Subcontracting, Equipment, Indirect costs

### Budget Narrative Format

```
PROJECT BUDGET SUMMARY
Project Title: [Your Project]
Funding Scheme: Cluster [X] / Pillar [X]
Total Eligible Costs: €[XXX,XXX]
EU Contribution (85%): €[XXX,XXX]
Partners' Contribution (15%): €[XXX,XXX]

PARTNER BUDGET BREAKDOWN

Partner 1: [Organization Name]
Role: [Coordinator/Beneficiary]
Country: [Country Code]

| Cost Category | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 | Year 5 | TOTAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personnel (10% admin) | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX |
| Subcontracting (max 30%) | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX |
| Purchase of Equipment | €XXX | - | - | - | - | €XXX |
| Travel & Subsistence | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX |
| Other Direct Costs | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX |
| Indirect Costs (25% max) | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX |
| PARTNER TOTAL | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX | €XXX |
```

### Cost Category Guidelines

**Personnel Costs**
- Include salaries for project staff
- Monthly rate = (Annual Salary × 1.2) / 12
- Include employer contributions, social security
- Cap administrative staff at 10% of personnel budget

**Subcontracting Costs**
- Maximum 30% of total eligible costs
- Requires competitive procurement (3+ quotes)
- Must be cost-effective (no mark-ups >25%)
- Examples: Consultancies, specialized testing labs

**Equipment Costs**
- Depreciation method: Cost / useful life (years)
- Only eligible if equipment <€200,000
- Must have direct link to project
- Justified in technical section

**Travel Costs**
- Staff mobility: €200-400 per day (depending on destination)
- Subsistence rates: €0-100 per night
- Per diem: €0-50 per day
- Use official rate cards from EC website

**Other Direct Costs**
- Open-access publication fees
- Subcontracted research
- IT costs (licenses, cloud services)
- Consumables (lab supplies, materials)

**Indirect Costs**
- Flat rate: 25% of direct eligible costs
- Alternative: Accept EC audit rates
- Covers office, utilities, administration

- **Facility Support Charges**: Use institutional TRAC rates

### Tri-Council Specific Rules

**NSERC (Natural Sciences & Engineering)**
- Personnel: Highly flexible (graduate students, RAs, technicians)
- Equipment: Depreciation allowed on equipment <$50k
- No overhead recovery on grants
- Travel: Up to $200/day for domestic, $300 for international

**SSHRC (Social Sciences & Humanities)**
- Personnel: Research assistants (undergraduate/graduate)
- Equipment: Limited to $5,000 total per year
- Requires Canadian content focus
- Travel: Research trips must be project-necessary

**CIHR (Health Research)**
- Personnel: Salary restrictions for PhD/MD personnel
- Biostats/Informatics support: Separately budgeted
- Equipment: Standard depreciation rules
- Travel: Restricted to research-essential activities

## 7. Excel Budget Template Tips

### Creating Dynamic Templates
1. **Use Named Ranges**: Simplifies formula references across sheets
2. **Build Validation**: Dropdown lists for cost categories, countries
3. **Auto-Calculations**: Annual totals, percentage of total, FTE calculations
4. **Currency Conversion**: Link to real-time rates for multi-currency projects
5. **Scenario Planning**: What-if analysis for different funding scenarios

### Key Formulas
```excel
=SUM(B2:B10)                    # Annual subtotals
=ROUND(B11*0.85,2)             # 85% EU co-funding
=B2/12                         # Monthly breakdown
=IF(B2>5000,"Itemize","")      # Conditional rules
=VLOOKUP(B2,rates,3)           # Salary rate lookups
```

### Compliance Checklist for All Programs
- [ ] Personnel costs justified with FTE/hourly rates
- [ ] Equipment costs itemized with depreciation calculations
- [ ] Subcontracting fees detailed with competitive procurement evidence
- [ ] Travel budgets broken down by trip purpose
- [ ] Indirect costs calculated per funder rules
- [ ] Co-funding sources confirmed and validated
- [ ] Total project costs align across all sections
- [ ] Currency consistency (no mixed currency mistakes)
- [ ] Justifications reference technical work packages
- [ ] Annual breakdowns align with project timeline

## 8. Common Budget Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-budgeting indirect costs**: Exceeds funder maximum percentages
2. **Missing cost justifications**: Insufficient narrative for large line items
3. **Inconsistent currency conversion**: Using old exchange rates
4. **Unsubstantiated salary rates**: Not aligned with institutional scales
5. **Subcontracting without competition**: Lack of competitive procurement evidence
6. **Equipment without depreciation**: Not properly calculated over useful life
7. **Travel inflated beyond realistic**: Exceeds program per diem rates
8. **Co-funding not secured**: Unconfirmed partner contributions

## 10. Best Practices Summary

✓ **Be Detailed**: More justification reduces rejection risk
✓ **Be Realistic**: Budgets that match technical plan are more credible
✓ **Be Compliant**: Follow each funder's specific template and rules
✓ **Be Consistent**: Ensure budget aligns with timeline and deliverables
✓ **Be Transparent**: Document all assumptions and cost sources
✓ **Be Competitive**: Compare with similar projects' budgets in field
