## Enterprise Sales & Decision Timelines

### Typical Enterprise Partnership Decision Cycle: 6-12 Months

Understanding enterprise buying cycles is critical for pipeline management.

### Enterprise Decision Timeline (For Target: 6-Month Deal Closure)

| Month | Stage | Corporate Activities | Your Partnership Actions |
|-------|-------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| **Month -6** | Discovery | Target identifies strategic gaps; explores market | Research target company; identify decision-maker; warm intro |
| **Month -5** | Awareness | Company issues RFP/RFI or informal inquiry | Respond to RFP; provide partnership proposal; demonstrate use cases |
| **Month -4** | Evaluation | Internal stakeholders (IT, Finance, Ops, Legal) review options | Present to steering committee; prepare demo; provide references |
| **Month -3** | Negotiation | Legal + Finance teams engage; terms discussed | Negotiate terms; address compliance concerns; provide case studies |
| **Month -2** | Procurement | IT security assessment; compliance review; final approvals | Security audit; compliance documentation; executive alignment |
| **Month -1** | Budgeting | Finance approves allocation; contracts prepared for signature | Prepare statement of work; align on commercial terms |
| **Month 0** | Execution | Contracts signed; implementation begins; press release issued | Launch pilot; onboard teams; begin joint GTM |

**Key Insight:** Typical enterprise cycles are 6-12 months. To close a deal in June 2026, you must **start outreach by December 2025 or earlier**. YOU ARE NOW IN THE OPTIMAL OUTREACH WINDOW.

### What Kills Enterprise Partnerships (Timing Mistakes)

| Mistake | Impact | Prevention |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Waiting for annual conference to pitch | 3-6 month delay | Start conversations now, use conference to accelerate |
| Assuming summer hiatus = no decisions | Missed deals | Build relationships June-Aug; close in Sept-Dec |
| Not aligning with budget cycles | Budget depletion; delayed deal | Pitch Feb-Apr (budget planning); close Sept-Dec (urgency) |
| Unclear ROI timelines | Deal stalls in negotiation | Show 30-60 day implementation; Q1 revenue recognition |
| Waiting for perfect product | Missed market window | Partner with MVP; iterate during pilot phase |

### BANT Framework for Enterprise Partnerships

Use BANT to qualify opportunities and accelerate decision cycles:

| BANT Element | Questions to Ask | Why It Matters for Partnerships | Timeline Signal |
|--------------|------------------|------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Budget** | "What's the allocated partnership budget?" | Confirms financial commitment exists | Budget confirmed = 3-4 month cycle; No budget = 6-12 months |
| **Authority** | "Who approves partnerships?" | Ensures you talk to decision-maker | Direct sponsor = 6 months; Committee approval = 12+ months |
| **Need** | "What strategic gap does partnership solve?" | Confirms urgent problem | Urgent gap = fast-track; Exploratory = slow-track |
| **Timing** | "When does partnership need to launch?" | Determines decision pressure | Q1 2026 launch = close by Dec 2025; Q3 2026 = close by June 2026 |
