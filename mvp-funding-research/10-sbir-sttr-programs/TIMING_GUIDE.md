## PHASE I, II, III TIMELINE ARCHITECTURE {#phase-timelines}

### Standard SBIR/STTR Phase Progression

```
PHASE I (Feasibility)
├─ Duration: 6-12 months (varies by agency)
├─ Funding: $50K-$250K (SBIR), $50K-$100K (STTR)
├─ Deliverable: Feasibility report + Phase II proposal outline
├─ Outcome: 15-25% advance to Phase II
└─ Key Questions:
   • Is the concept technically feasible?
   • Is there market demand?
   • Can a prototype be developed?

    ↓ 6-12 month gap (typical)

PHASE II (Development & Validation)
├─ Duration: 24 months
├─ Funding: $750K-$2.0M (SBIR), $400K-$750K (STTR)
├─ Deliverable: Prototype/MVP + detailed commercialization plan
├─ Outcome: 70%+ transition to Phase III
└─ Key Questions:
   • Can we build and validate a working prototype?
   • What are the commercialization pathways?
   • What is the go-to-market strategy?

    ↓ Variable (3-24 months typical)

PHASE III (Commercialization)
├─ Duration: Varies (no limit)
├─ Funding: Production contracts or commercial revenue
├─ Source: Government contracts, private customers, or venture funding
├─ Outcome: Revenue-generating commercial product
└─ Key Questions:
   • Can we scale to production?
   • What are customer acquisition costs?
   • What is the path to profitability?
```

### Agency-Specific Phase Timelines

#### DoD Timeline
```
PHASE I:  6-12 months, $250K
├─ Submission: ~1 month preparation
├─ Review: 1-2 months
├─ Notification: 30-60 days after close
├─ Award-to-Start: 60-90 days
└─ Total: ~7-15 months from award to Phase I completion

### Phase Execution Timeline (Typical 7-Year Journey)

```
Year 1:      Phase I Submission → Phase I Execution (6-12 mo)
Year 2:      Phase II Proposal & Award → Phase II Execution begins
Years 2-4:   Phase II Execution (24 months)
Years 3-5:   Phase III Preparation & Market Validation
Years 4-7:   Phase III Execution (Production, Sales, Scale)
```

### Critical Timing Constraints

**Pre-Phase II Requirements**
- Complete Phase I research and documented results
- Develop detailed Phase II technical approach (typically 4-8 weeks work)
- Conduct customer/market validation (60-90 days recommended)
- Secure letters of support from potential customers/partners
- Establish commercialization team

**Pre-Phase III Requirements**
- Completed Phase II prototype/MVP with performance validation
- Detailed go-to-market strategy and business plan
- Identified customer(s) or market segments
- Letter(s) of intent or LOI from government/commercial customers
- Funding strategy for Phase III (government contracts, venture, revenue)

## FAST-TRACK OPPORTUNITIES {#fast-track-opportunities}

### Overview
Fast-Track programs allow combining Phase I and Phase II into a single proposal submission, accelerating the award-to-commercialization timeline.

### NSF SBIR/STTR Fast-Track Pilot

**Program Structure**
- **Single Submission:** Phase I + Phase II combined proposal
- **Single Review:** One technical and commercial evaluation
- **Faster Timeline:** Potential 6-12 month acceleration vs. traditional pathway

**Funding & Duration**
- **Phase I Component:** Up to $400,000 (6-12 months)
- **Phase II Component:** Up to $1,155,000 (18-24 months)
- **Total Maximum:** $1,555,000 over 24-36 months
- **Awards per cycle:** Competitive (specific numbers TBD for 2026+)

**Eligibility Requirements**
1. Demonstrated NSF research funding lineage (prior NSF grants helpful)
2. Completion of formal customer discovery training (NSF I-Corps or equivalent)
3. Complete management team in place at proposal submission
4. Strong commercialization narrative and go-to-market strategy
5. Evidence of customer validation/market need

**Key Dates (Historical)**
- **Solicitation Release:** ~March (subject to program status)
- **Submission Deadline:** ~May/June (varies by year)
- **Proposal Preparation Window:** ~60-90 days typical

**Fast-Track vs. Traditional Timeline**
```
TRADITIONAL PATHWAY:
Q1 Phase I Submit → Q3-Q4 Phase I Award → Q4-Q1 Phase II Submit → Q2-Q3 Phase II Award → Q3-Q4 Phase II starts
Timeline: 12-18 months to Phase II start

FAST-TRACK PATHWAY:
Q1 Submit (FT) → Q2-Q3 Award → Q2-Q3 Phase I starts, Phase II planning simultaneously
Timeline: 6-9 months to parallel Phase I/II execution
ACCELERATION: 6-12 months faster
```

```
DoD (Fastest):
├─ Close: Month 0
├─ Notification: Month 0.5-2
├─ Award: Month 1-2
├─ Kick-off: Month 1.5-3
└─ First Funds: Month 2-3 (60-90 days)

NSF (Slower):
├─ Close: Month 0
├─ Review: Months 1-2

- **Government Processing:** 30-60 days (contract setup, compliance checks)

- **First Funds Release:** Day 90-120 typical (30-90 days after kick-off)
- **Account Setup:** ~30 days from first funds release

- **Budget for 6+ month cashflow:** Assume 6 months minimum from submission to receiving first funds
- **Runway Required:** 9+ months ideal (submit, wait 6 months, bootstrap 3+ months while Phase I setup completes)
- **Contractor Costs:** Budget contractors/consultants starting 2-3 months after Phase I award

## PRE-APPLICATION PREPARATION TIMELINE {#pre-application-preparation}

### Recommended 6-Month Pre-Submission Timeline

#### Months 0-1: Research & Agency Selection
**Week 1-2: Topic Research**
- Review all agency SBIR/STTR topic areas
- Identify 3-5 matching agencies and topics
- Check agency-specific eligibility requirements
- Document alignment between your tech and topic descriptions

**Week 3-4: Program Structure Learning**
- Review agency solicitation documents
- Understand Phase I/II funding amounts
- Check historical success rates (if available)
- Attend agency webinars/office hours

**Timeline:**
- Select agency: Week 1-2
- Confirm topic fit: Week 2
- Research deadline: Week 3-4

#### Month 1-2: Team & Resource Planning
**Team Assembly**
- Identify Phase I technical lead (subject matter expert)
- Recruit Phase I project manager
- Confirm company commitment and resources
- Assign commercialization lead (VP Sales/Marketing)

**Capability Documentation**
- Compile team resumes/bios
- Document relevant prior experience
- Identify advisory board members/collaborators
- Collect letters of support from advisors/potential customers

**Technical Preparation**
- Outline current technical status/proof of concept
- Document preliminary data/results
- List required equipment/resources
- Identify technical risks and mitigation strategies

**Timeline:**
- Team finalized: Week 1
- Documentation assembled: Week 2-3
- Technical outline ready: Week 4

#### Month 2-3: Customer Discovery & Validation
**Market Research**
- Conduct 20-30 customer interviews
- Document pain points and need
- Assess market size and opportunity
- Identify potential Phase II customers/partners

**Customer Engagement**
- Identify potential Phase II sponsors/customers
- Request letters of support/interest
- Document customer value proposition
- Establish advisory relationships

**Competitive Analysis**
- Research competing solutions
- Document differentiation strategy
- Identify market gaps
- Assess commercialization barriers

**Timeline:**
- Customer interviews: Week 1-2
- Letters of support requests: Week 2-3
- Competitive analysis: Week 4

#### Month 3-4: Proposal Framework Development
**Technical Approach**
- Draft Phase I research plan (10-15 pages)
- Outline specific aims/objectives
- Document methodology
- Establish success metrics
- Identify risks and mitigation

**Commercialization Strategy**
- Draft commercialization plan (5-10 pages)
- Document go-to-market strategy
- Identify revenue model
- Assess market barriers/opportunities
- Develop 3-year revenue projections

**Budget Development**
- Identify required resources
- Document personnel needs
- List equipment/materials costs
- Prepare detailed budget justification
- Compile vendor quotes

**Timeline:**
- Technical approach draft: Week 1-2
- Commercialization plan draft: Week 2-3
- Budget framework: Week 3-4

#### Month 4-5: Proposal Writing & Refinement
**Full Proposal Development**
- Write Phase I project narrative
- Integrate technical and commercial content
- Add figures, diagrams, timelines
- Prepare detailed budget narrative
- Compile required forms and certifications

**Internal Review**
- Technical review (scientific accuracy, feasibility)
- Commercial review (market viability, scalability)
- Compliance review (eligibility, forms, requirements)
- Editing and formatting review

**External Feedback**
- Share draft with technical advisors
- Share draft with potential customers
- Refine based on external feedback
- Strengthen weak sections

**Timeline:**
- Complete draft: Week 1-2
- Internal review: Week 2-3
- External feedback: Week 3-4
- Final revision: Week 4

#### Month 5-6: Submission Preparation
**Final Review & Submission**
- Final editing and formatting
- Compliance check (all required documents)
- PDF finalization (proper naming, formatting)
- System registration (SAM.gov, Grants.gov, etc.)
- Dry-run submission test

**Submission Day Preparation**
- Assign submission team (2-3 people)
- Plan submission schedule (early submit, not deadline)
- Verify system access and functionality
- Prepare backup submission plans

**Post-Submission**
- Verify receipt confirmation
- Document submission details
- Archive all proposal materials
- Plan communication strategy

**Timeline:**
- Final edits: Week 1
- Compliance check: Week 1-2
- System testing: Week 2-3
- Early submission: Week 3-4 (before deadline)
- Buffer week for issues: Week 4

### Quick Submission Timeline (3 Months Aggressive)

If you discover a late-opening solicitation:

**Month 1: Rapid Ramp-Up**
- **Week 1-2:** Confirm topic fit, gather team, begin writing
- **Week 3:** Complete first draft of proposal narrative
- **Week 4:** Internal review and revision

**Month 2: Content Development**
- **Week 1:** Finalize technical approach
- **Week 2:** Develop budget and commercialization plan
- **Week 3:** External review and feedback
- **Week 4:** Comprehensive revision

**Month 3: Submission**
- **Week 1-2:** Final edits, compliance check
- **Week 3:** System testing, submission preparation
- **Week 4:** Submit (early, not deadline day)

### Registration & Compliance Timeline (Separate)

These processes run in parallel and should start 8+ weeks before deadline:

**SAM.gov Registration**
- **Timeline:** 4-8 weeks
- **Required for:** All federal grants
- **Action:** https://www.sam.gov/
- **Update:** Entity status must be current (renew annually)

**Grants.gov Registration**
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks after SAM.gov active
- **Required for:** Most agency submissions
- **Action:** https://www.grants.gov/
- **Update:** Must maintain current registration

**DUNS Number (if needed)**
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks
- **Required for:** Some legacy systems (decreasing)
- **Action:** Register at https://www.dnb.com/
- **Status:** Being phased out in favor of UEI (Entity ID)

**Agency-Specific Registrations**
- **DoD:** WAWF (Wiser), eSTRA, CMMC compliance documentation
- **NIH/USDA:** eRA Commons, SciENcv
- **DOE:** PAMS system registration
- **NSF:** FastLane or system specific portal

**Total Compliance Timeline:** 8-12 weeks minimum
