## SECTION 1: MONTHLY CALENDAR & KEY EVENTS

### JANUARY
#### Key Dates & Cycles
- **Jan 1-31**: New Fiscal Year Planning Month - Many foundations review prior year results
- **Early January**: Gates Foundation Deadline periods often open (check website for 2025)
- **Mid-January**: Arnold Ventures rolling applications active
- **Late January**: Start planning for Spring deadlines

#### Events & Conferences
- **Nonprofit Ready** webinars begin (https://www.nonprofitready.org) - Often feature foundation funding sessions
- **Candid Learning** winter courses launch (https://learning.candid.org)
- Foundation annual reports published (review Dec/Jan for prior year trends)

#### Action Items
- Review 2024 foundation grant outcomes from grantees
- Start 2-month timeline for Q1 deadline preparations
- Register for spring webinars
- Research foundations' 2024 annual reports for 2025 priorities

- **Feb 1**: New Profit early application window (rolling, but increasing volume this month)
- **Feb 14**: Potential deadline for some community foundations' spring grants
- **Late Feb**: Many foundations issue Q1 reporting requirements from 2024 grantees

#### Events & Conferences

#### Application Cycles Opening

#### Action Items

## SECTION 2: MAJOR FOUNDATION DEADLINE CALENDARS (2024-2025)

### A. EARLY-STAGE FOCUSED FOUNDATIONS

#### **Echoing Green Fellowship**
- **Website**: https://www.echoinggreen.org
- **Focus**: Early-stage social entrepreneurs
- **Eligibility**: Individual leaders, new organizations (0-4 years), any sector
- **Grant**: $90,000 over 2 years + fellowship coaching/network
- **Application Timeline**:
  - Opens: October 1 (or Oct 15 soft open)
  - Deadline: Mid-April (typically April 15-30)
  - Notification: May (announces cohort)
  - Program starts: August
  - Total process: ~9-10 months
- **Application Type**: Competitive online application
- **URL**: https://www.echoinggreen.org/fellowships
- **Contact**: applications@echoinggreen.org
- **Success Rate**: ~2% (highly competitive)
- **2024-2025 Key Dates**:
  - Oct 15, 2024: Opens
  - Apr 30, 2025: Deadline
  - May 2025: Cohort announced
  - Aug 2025: Class 22 begins

---

#### **Draper Richards Kaplan (DRK) Foundation**
- **Website**: https://www.drkfoundation.org
- **Focus**: Early-stage social enterprises with revenue models
- **Eligibility**: 1-5 years old, generating revenue, scalable model
- **Grant**: $300,000 over 3 years ($100K annually), often in tranches
- **Application Timeline**:
  - Opens: September 1 (typically)
  - Deadline: Late October/Early November (6-8 week window)
  - Notification: March/April
  - Grant starts: Awarded in tranches, Q2/Q3
  - Total process: ~6-7 months
- **Application Type**: Competitive online submission + video
- **2024-2025 Key Dates**:
  - Sep 1, 2024: Opens
  - Nov 5, 2024: Closed
  - Mar 2025: Notifications
  - Jun 2025: Funding begins
- **Success Rate**: ~1-2% (highly selective)
- **Pro Tips**:
  - Apply early in window (Sept/early Oct), not at deadline
  - Video element is critical - production quality matters
  - Demonstrate clear revenue model (not just grant-dependent)
  - Have external advisors review application

---

#### **Fast Forward (formerly Idealware)**
- **Website**: https://www.ffwd.org
- **Focus**: Tech-enabled nonprofits (any cause area)
- **Eligibility**: Early traction (500+ users or $100K+ revenue), tech-focused
- **Grant/Services**: $80K cash + $300K+ in services over 6 months
- **Application Timeline**:
  - 2 Cohorts per year (Spring & Fall)
  - Spring cohort: Applications close Jan 31
  - Fall cohort: Applications close ~July 31
  - Notification: 2 weeks after deadline
  - Program: 6-month intensive
  - Total process: 3 months application, 6 months program
- **Application Type**: Online application + 10-min video pitch
- **2024-2025 Key Dates**:
  - Jan 31, 2025: Spring applications close
  - Feb 15, 2025: Spring notifications
  - Jul 31, 2025: Fall applications close
  - Aug 15, 2025: Fall notifications
- **Application URL**: https://www.ffwd.org/application
- **Success Rate**: ~20% (easier than DRK/Echoing Green)
- **Special Notes**:
  - Rollover applications accepted if initially rejected
  - Services (dev, design, coaching) often worth more than $80K cash

---

#### **Kapor Center**
- **Website**: https://www.kaporcenter.org
- **Focus**: Tech for social impact, closing opportunity gaps
- **Eligibility**: Tech-enabled startups, diverse founders preferred
- **Grant**: $50K-$500K (typically $100K-$200K)
- **Application Timeline**:
  - Rolling applications (no specific deadlines)
  - Review: 4-8 weeks
  - Decision: Varies, typically 3-6 months from application
- **Application Type**: Online application
- **2024-2025 Key Dates**:
  - Ongoing rolling - submit anytime
  - Expected review time: 4-8 weeks
- **Application URL**: https://www.kaporcenter.org/apply
- **Success Rate**: ~15-20% (moderate, less competitive than DRK)

## SECTION 10: RED FLAGS & DEADLINE MISTAKES TO AVOID

### **Common Timing Mistakes**

1. **Waiting Until Deadline to Submit**
   - Mistake: Submitting application on deadline day
   - Consequence: Technical issues, rushed quality, lost opportunity
   - Fix: Submit 1 week before deadline minimum

2. **Missing Internal Deadlines**
   - Mistake: Not accounting for internal review time
   - Consequence: Rushed application, quality issues, missed deadline
   - Fix: Work backward 2 weeks from deadline for internal QA

3. **Applying to Wrong Deadline**
   - Mistake: Submitting to foundation with no 2024 deadline info
   - Consequence: Missed deadline, or applying to next cycle (1 year delay)
   - Fix: Call foundation to confirm 2025 deadline before applying

4. **Not Building a Pipeline**
   - Mistake: Waiting for results on one application before starting next
   - Consequence: Gaps in funding, crisis-mode fundraising
   - Fix: Always have 10-15 applications in various stages

5. **Applying Before Ready**
   - Mistake: Applying before you have impact data to demonstrate
   - Consequence: Weak application, rejection
   - Fix: Build track record, get initial results, then apply

6. **Missing Soft Deadlines**
   - Mistake: Treating soft/early deadlines same as hard deadline
   - Consequence: Getting lost in large volume at deadline
   - Fix: Prioritize applying by soft deadlines (1-2 weeks before hard deadline)

7. **Not Respecting Review Timelines**
   - Mistake: Assuming quick response (many take 3-6 months)
   - Consequence: Unrealistic expectations, missed budget planning
   - Fix: Build 6-month timeline into funding strategy

8. **Overlooking Rolling Applications**
   - Mistake: Thinking rolling deadlines are continuous
   - Consequence: Slowed response when submitting near quarter-end
   - Fix: Submit rolling applications early in quarters for faster decisions

## FINAL NOTES & STRATEGIC TIMING INSIGHTS

### **The Golden Rules of Foundation Grant Timing**

1. **Always Be Submitting**: Average 3-6 month review times means you need pipeline with 10-15 applications at various stages to ensure steady funding flow.

2. **Strategic Timing Matters**: Submit to high-priority foundations early in deadline window, not at last minute. Early submissions get less crowded review periods.

3. **Build Relationships First**: Timing matters less if you have relationships. A strong relationship can create custom deadlines or rolling windows.

4. **Prepare 6 Months Ahead**: For major foundations (Gates, Ford, Skoll), plan 6-12 months in advance. Start conversations now for 2026 funding.

5. **Use Slow Seasons**: July/August are slow review months - perfect for deep proposal development without time pressure.

6. **Seasonal Patterns**:
   - Jan-Mar: Strong Q1 season (post-holiday giving planning)
   - Apr-Jun: Mid-year funding (education, youth programs)
   - Jul-Aug: Slow season (vacations)
   - Sep-Nov: Major deadline rush (fiscal year alignment)
   - Dec: Year-end giving surge

7. **Event-Based Strategy**: Attend SOCAP (Oct), Skoll World Forum (April), regional convening meetings. Relationships made at events often lead to grants 6-12 months later.

8. **Fiscal Year Alignment**: Many foundations operate on calendar year (Jan-Dec) or fiscal year (varies). Understanding their cycles helps predict when they're reviewing and what funding is available.
