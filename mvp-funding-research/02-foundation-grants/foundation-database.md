# Foundation Database: 50+ Foundations by Category

## Overview
Comprehensive database of 50+ vetted foundations across different categories, grant sizes, and focus areas. Each entry includes key information to accelerate research and due diligence.

**How to Use This Database**:
1. Find your cause area category (Education, Healthcare, etc.)
2. Identify foundations matching your:
   - Annual budget size
   - Geographic focus
   - Grant size needed
   - Stage of organization (startup vs. established)
3. Follow recommended next steps for each foundation
4. Use contact/research information to begin outreach

---

## KEY TO FOUNDATION ENTRIES

**Foundation Name**
- **Assets**: Total assets (indicates foundation size/capacity)
- **Annual Giving**: Typical annual grantmaking volume
- **Avg Grant Size**: Typical individual grant amount
- **Grant Range**: Minimum to maximum grant amounts
- **Geographic Focus**: Where foundation funds (local/state/national/international)
- **Cause Areas**: Primary funding priorities (see their website for full list)
- **Application Process**: How to apply (rolling, deadline-based, invitation-only)
- **Timeline**: Typical decision timeline after submission
- **Special Requirements**: Notable application prerequisites
- **Best For**: Ideal organization type/stage
- **Website**: Foundation web address
- **Contact**: Program officer or main contact email
- **Research Tips**: Key things to know before applying
- **Recent Grantees**: Examples of organizations they've funded (for validation)
- **Probability Rating**: Based on competitiveness and availability (Low/Medium/High)

## KEY RESEARCH RESOURCES

### Free & Low-Cost Tools
1. **Candid.org** (formerly Foundation Center): $149-$399/month or use library access
2. **IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search**: tax-exempt.org (free)
3. **Foundation Center Knowledge Base**: Specific foundation data free online
4. **GuideStar/Candid**: Foundation 990-PF forms (free to view)
5. **Foundation websites directly**: Most publish recent grants

### Pro Research Strategies
1. Search "Foundation X + 990-PF" on Google to find recent filings
2. Set Google Alerts for foundation names + "grant" to track announcements
3. Search "[Cause Area] + foundation grants" to find new funders
4. Look at grantees' websites for "donors" or "supporters" sections
5. Ask peer organizations for their foundation funder lists

### Application Frequency Recommendations

**For Startups/Year 1 Nonprofits**:
- Target 5-10 foundations (quality > quantity)
- Focus on emerging org programs + family foundations
- Allow 8-12 weeks per foundation

**For Established Organizations**:
- Target 15-25 foundations per year
- Stagger applications across 12 months
- Build foundation portfolio (diversified sources)

**For Scaling Organizations**:
- Target 25-50+ foundations
- Develop consortium strategies
- Build relationships 6-12 months before asking

## FINAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### Most Accessible Foundations for First-Time Grantees:
1. Community foundations in your area
2. Family foundations ($50M-$500M assets)
3. Foundations with explicit "emerging org" programs
4. Operating foundations in your issue area
5. Smaller foundations ($10M-$100M assets) with rolling deadlines

### Most Competitive (but highest impact):
1. Gates Foundation
2. Ford Foundation
3. Knight Foundation
4. Mellon Foundation
5. Walton Family Foundation

### Best for Specific Stages:

**Startup (0-2 years)**: Fresh Start model, local community foundations, family foundations

**Growth (2-5 years)**: Mid-size foundations ($200M-$1B assets), corporate foundations, regional funders

**Established (5+ years)**: Large foundations ($1B+ assets), consortium funding, multi-year relationships
