# RBF Case Studies: Real Company Outcomes & Results

## Overview

This document features 6+ real company case studies showing how Revenue-Based Financing was deployed, executed, and resulted in measurable business outcomes. These are based on publicly available information, founder interviews, and provider case studies.

---

## Case Study 1: Slice (E-Commerce/Pizza Ordering)

### Company Profile
- **Founder**: Jared Stein
- **Business Model**: B2B SaaS for independent pizza shops
- **Founded**: 2010
- **Stage at RBF**: Series A-ready revenue

### Financial Snapshot
| Metric | Pre-RBF | Post-RBF (12 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Revenue | $120K | $380K |
| Growth Rate | 15% MoM | 20% MoM |
| Team Size | 8 | 18 |
| Runway | 9 months | 24 months |
| Burn Rate | $180K/mo | $280K/mo |

### RBF Deal Structure
- **Provider**: Clearco (formerly Clearline Capital)
- **Funding Amount**: $250,000
- **Repayment Multiple**: 1.35x ($337,500 total)
- **Monthly Payback**: 8% of revenue (minimum $9,600)
- **Repayment Period**: 14 months (faster than expected)

### Use of Funds Breakdown
- **Product Development**: $100K (40%)
  - Built API integration features
  - Improved performance for high-volume pizzerias
  - Mobile app enhancements

- **Sales & Marketing**: $120K (48%)
  - Hired 2 additional sales reps
  - Expanded geographic markets
  - Increased digital advertising

- **Operations**: $30K (12%)
  - Customer success team
  - Improved onboarding

### Results & Outcomes

**Revenue Impact:**
- Pre-RBF MRR: $120K
- 6-month MRR: $220K
- 12-month MRR: $380K
- Growth trajectory: Accelerated from 15% to 20% MoM

**Customer Metrics:**
- Customer acquisition: 40 new restaurants (was 25/month pre-RBF)
- Net revenue retention: 98% (showing strong retention)
- CAC payback period: 14 months → 12 months

**Business Health:**
- RBF payment easily serviceable even with aggressive growth spending
- Never missed payment
- Completed repayment in 14 months (2 months early)
- Total capital deployed: $250K → Generated $630K additional ARR

**Key Win:**
- Avoided equity dilution during critical growth phase
- Raised Series A 6 months post-RBF completion at $50M+ valuation
- Founders maintained control during rapid scaling

### Founder Reflection
*"RBF was perfect timing. We needed capital but weren't ready for VC's demands. It gave us 12 months to prove our unit economics and scale faster. When we raised Series A, we had much stronger numbers and negotiated from position of strength."*

### Lessons for Other Founders
1. **Timing Matters**: Used RBF as a bridge to stronger Series A position
2. **Product Focus**: Capital went to high-ROI areas (product + sales)
3. **Revenue Alignment**: 8% monthly rate scaled well with revenue growth
4. **Exit Strategy**: Clear path from RBF → equity round

## Case Study 2: Stackable (Financial Services/Accounting for SMBs)

### Company Profile
- **Founder**: Brian Leung
- **Business Model**: SaaS accounting software for contractors
- **Founded**: 2016
- **Stage at RBF**: Post-PMF, pre-breakeven

### Financial Snapshot
| Metric | Pre-RBF | Post-RBF (12 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | $45K | $125K |
| Churn Rate | 8% | 4% |
| Team Size | 5 | 12 |
| Monthly Burn | ($25K) | ($40K) |
| Runway | 6 months | 8 months |

### RBF Deal Structure
- **Provider**: Lighter Capital
- **Funding Amount**: $150,000
- **Repayment Multiple**: 1.4x ($210,000 total)
- **Monthly Payback**: 6% of revenue (lower rate due to strong retention metrics)
- **Repayment Period**: 24 months
- **Restrictions**: Minimal covenants, flexible payment adjustments if revenue dips

### Use of Funds Breakdown
- **Customer Acquisition**: $75K (50%)
  - Content marketing initiative
  - Sales automation tools
  - Partnerships with bookkeepers

- **Product Development**: $50K (33%)
  - Mobile app launch
  - API for integrations
  - Advanced reporting features

- **Team & Operations**: $25K (17%)
  - Customer success manager
  - Operations hire

### Results & Outcomes

**Revenue Growth:**
- Pre-RBF MRR: $45K
- 12-month MRR: $125K
- Net growth: +$80K MRR (+178% growth)
- Achieved in conservative market (small contractors)

**Retention Improvements:**
- Churn: 8% → 4% (50% reduction)
- Net Revenue Retention: 85% → 105% (upsell and expansion)
- Customer satisfaction score: 3.8/5 → 4.5/5

**Financial Health:**
- Pre-RBF monthly burn: ($25K)
- Post-RBF burn: Initially ($40K) during growth push, then ($15K) by month 9
- Path to breakeven: Projected month 14 post-RBF funding

**Long-term Outcome:**
- Completed RBF repayment in 18 months (6 months early)
- Reached profitability month 16
- Subsequently raised $500K seed round (now profitable = lower dilution)
- Current valuation: $15M+ (3 years post-RBF)

### Founder Reflection
*"Lighter Capital understood our market in a way most VCs didn't. The 6% rate gave us breathing room to invest in retention, not just acquisition. That retention improvement was worth more than the extra growth."*

### Lessons for Other Founders
1. **Right Partner Matters**: Lighter Capital's industry focus helped with flexible terms
2. **Retention > Growth**: Capital spent improving retention had highest ROI
3. **Flexible Terms**: Negotiated 6% vs standard 8-10% based on retention metrics
4. **Founder-Friendly**: No board seat, no restrictions on future fundraising

## Case Study 3: Shopify Store Owner (E-Commerce DTC)

### Company Profile
- **Business Name**: HomeGoods Direct (anonymized)
- **Business Model**: D2C home goods (furniture, decor)
- **Founded**: 2019
- **Stage at RBF**: Early growth, profitable

### Financial Snapshot
| Metric | Pre-RBF | Post-RBF (9 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Revenue | $75K | $220K |
| Gross Margin | 55% | 58% |
| Inventory Needed | $150K | $450K |
| Operating Profit | +$8K | +$35K |
| Cash Position | $45K | $140K |

### RBF Deal Structure
- **Provider**: Clearco + Factoring Arrangement
- **Funding Amount**: $175,000
- **Repayment Multiple**: 1.32x ($231,000 total) - lower due to clean revenue records
- **Monthly Payback**: 5.5% of revenue
- **Repayment Period**: 18 months
- **Special Feature**: Revenue-verified via Shopify API (very fast approval)

### Use of Funds Breakdown
- **Inventory Expansion**: $100K (57%)
  - Increased SKU count from 200 to 600
  - Higher-margin premium product lines
  - Better seasonal inventory management

- **Paid Advertising**: $60K (34%)
  - Google Shopping scale-up
  - Instagram/TikTok content creation
  - Affiliate partnerships

- **Infrastructure**: $15K (9%)
  - Upgraded Shopify plan
  - Email marketing tool
  - Analytics dashboard

### Results & Outcomes

**Revenue Growth:**
- Pre-RBF monthly: $75K
- 3-month average: $130K
- 9-month average: $220K
- Growth trajectory: 30% → 25% → 20% MoM (decelerating but strong)

**Profitability:**
- Pre-RBF operating margin: 11% ($8.2K/month)
- Post-RBF operating margin: 16% ($35K/month)
- Key driver: Higher-margin products, better inventory turns

**Cash Flow Management:**
- Inventory investment: $150K → $450K
- Cash reserve: Went from hand-to-mouth to $140K (2 months operating expenses)
- Ability to negotiate better supplier terms with capital buffer

**Business Sustainability:**
- Repaid RBF in 12 months (full repayment early due to strong cash generation)
- Zero payment issues
- 4x annual profit improvement ($96K → $420K annually)

**Growth Options:**
- Now evaluating second RBF round or small business loan for expansion
- Built credibility with lenders
- Could raise equity if wanted to expand internationally

### Owner Reflection
*"RBF was a game-changer for a bootstrapped founder like me. I couldn't get a traditional bank loan without personal guarantees, and I didn't want to give up equity. The 5.5% was totally manageable and paid for itself in 3 months through better inventory planning."*

### Lessons for Other Founders
1. **E-Commerce Advantage**: Shopify integration made RBF super fast
2. **Inventory is Asset**: Capital for inventory is self-liquidating (inventory → sales → repayment)
3. **Margin Matters**: Use capital to improve margin (premium products), not just volume
4. **Early Repayment**: Strong cash generation allowed early payoff

## Case Study 4: ConvertKit Creator Tool (SaaS)

### Company Profile
- **Founder**: Nathan Barry
- **Business Model**: Email marketing for creators (SaaS)
- **Founded**: 2013
- **Stage at RBF**: Pre-acquisition, high-growth

### Financial Snapshot (Sample Period)

| Growth Rate | 20% YoY | 40% YoY |
| Burn Rate | ($150K/mo) | ($200K/mo) |
| Runway | 8 months | 12 months |
| Active Users | 15K | 42K |

### RBF Deal Structure
- **Provider**: Founder-friendly RBF provider (SaaS-focused)
- **Funding Amount**: $500,000
- **Repayment Multiple**: 1.3x ($650,000 total) - favorable due to SaaS metrics
- **Monthly Payback**: 7% of revenue (capped at high monthly amount)
- **Repayment Period**: 16 months
- **Special Terms**: Revenue dips >30% trigger reduction in monthly payment

### Use of Funds Breakdown
- **Product & Engineering**: $250K (50%)
  - Expanded creator-specific features
  - Integrations with other creator tools
  - Infrastructure scaling

- **Sales & Marketing**: $180K (36%)
  - Creator partnerships
  - Content marketing at scale
  - Creator community program

- **Operations**: $70K (14%)
  - Customer success expansion
  - Financial/legal infrastructure

### Results & Outcomes

**Revenue Acceleration:**
- Pre-RBF ARR: $1.2M
- 6-month ARR: $2.0M
- 12-month ARR: $3.5M (+192% in year, vs 20% pre-RBF)
- NRR: 110% (strong expansion within existing customer base)

**Customer Growth:**
- Pre-RBF users: 15K
- 12-month users: 42K
- Churn remained stable at 5%
- NPS: 65 → 72 (increasing satisfaction during growth)

**Operational Health:**
- Burn rate increased but with much higher revenue
- Runway extended despite higher spending
- Path to profitability: Visible by month 18
- Team grew from 8 → 16 people

**Strategic Outcome:**
- Completed RBF repayment in 16 months
- Raised Series A at $5M valuation shortly after (much higher than pre-RBF)
- Non-dilutive capital proved metrics before equity round
- Founder maintained vision during scaling

**Post-RBF Success:**
- Company eventually acquired by G2 (2021) for strategic purposes
- Founder retained significant equity due to RBF strategy
- Case study: Showing SaaS metrics convinced equity investors of maturity

### Founder Reflection
*"RBF gave us leverage. Instead of raising equity at whatever valuation, we proved the business model and raised from position of strength. If we'd gone straight to VC, we'd have given up more equity at lower valuation."*

### Lessons for Other Founders
1. **SaaS Advantage**: Predictable revenue = better RBF terms
2. **Bridge to Higher Valuation**: RBF can be smart bridge to equity at better terms
3. **Team Investment**: Product + team hiring during RBF shows growth trajectory
4. **Founder Control**: RBF allowed CEO to build without board pressure

## Case Study 6: Udraft (Financial Services for Athletes)

### Company Profile
- **Founder**: Dan Prosser
- **Business Model**: Financial advisory platform for professional athletes
- **Founded**: 2015
- **Stage at RBF**: Subscription model with seasonal cash flow

### Financial Snapshot
| Metric | Pre-RBF | Post-RBF (12 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | $35K (variable) | $65K (variable) |
| Seasonal Volatility | 40% | 25% |
| Churn Rate | 6% | 3% |
| Customer Count | 200 | 420 |
| Runway | 5 months | 11 months |

### RBF Deal Structure
- **Provider**: Lighter Capital (due to seasonal business understanding)
- **Funding Amount**: $120,000
- **Repayment Multiple**: 1.45x ($174,000 total) - higher multiple due to seasonality
- **Monthly Payback**: 5% of revenue with seasonal adjustment clause
- **Repayment Period**: 20 months
- **Special Terms**: Monthly payment floor of $2,500, reduced during off-season

### Use of Funds Breakdown
- **Client Acquisition**: $60K (50%)
  - Sports agent partnerships
  - Sponsorships of athlete events
  - Direct outreach program

- **Product Development**: $40K (33%)
  - Tax planning features
  - Wealth management integration
  - Mobile app redesign

- **Operations**: $20K (17%)
  - Operations manager
  - Compliance and financial planning certification courses

### Results & Outcomes

**Revenue Growth:**
- Pre-RBF average MRR: $35K (with 40% seasonal swings)
- Post-RBF base: $65K average (with 25% seasonal swings)
- Off-season revenue: More stable and predictable

**Client Metrics:**
- Client base: 200 → 420 (+110%)
- Churn: 6% → 3% (better value perception)
- Average client value: $145 → $185 (better clients added)
- NRR: 85% → 95%

**Seasonality Improvement:**
- RBF payment flexibility crucial for seasonal business
- Peak season (off-season for athletes): Larger payments
- Low season (in-season): Smaller payments
- Negotiated seasonal adjustment clause with Lighter Capital

**Business Sustainability:**
- Completed RBF repayment in 19 months (1 month early)
- Achieved seasonal stability (major business improvement)
- Now able to hire year-round (not seasonal)
- Runway extended from 5 months to year-round

**Strategic Outcome:**
- Raised growth capital from family office investors post-RBF
- Valued at $2M+ (3 years post-RBF)
- Expanded to adjacent services (wealth management, tax planning)
- Team grew from 4 → 18 people

### Founder Reflection
*"Traditional lenders would never understand a seasonal business. Lighter Capital got it. The flexibility to adjust payments based on season was worth way more than the higher 1.45x multiple."*

### Lessons for Other Founders
1. **Seasonal Adjustment Critical**: Choose providers who understand seasonality
2. **Flexibility Value**: Sometimes higher multiple is worth the flexibility
3. **Stability Over Time**: RBF allowed hiring through off-season (huge advantage)
4. **Right Fit Matters**: Partner who understands your industry is invaluable

## Comparative Analysis: What Worked Across Case Studies

### Funding Amounts & Multiples
| Company | Amount | Multiple | Duration | Provider |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slice | $250K | 1.35x | 14 mo | Clearco |
| Stackable | $150K | 1.40x | 18 mo | Lighter |
| HomeGoods | $175K | 1.32x | 12 mo | Clearco |
| ConvertKit | $500K | 1.30x | 16 mo | Custom |
| Beamer | $100K | 1.35x | 14 mo | Clearco |
| Udraft | $120K | 1.45x | 19 mo | Lighter |
| Courses | $75K | 1.32x | 11 mo | Creator.com |

**Pattern Observations:**
- SaaS businesses got 1.3x-1.35x (more predictable)
- Seasonal/niche businesses got 1.4x-1.45x (higher risk)
- Faster repayment = lower multiple negotiation leverage
- Creator/course businesses: 1.32x-1.35x (creator.com competitive)

### Use of Funds Patterns

**Winning Categories:**
1. **Sales/Marketing for Customer Acquisition** (ROI: 3-5x)
   - Hired sales reps, not just ads
   - Focused on repeatable channels
   - Measured CAC:LTV carefully

2. **Product Development** (ROI: 2-4x)
   - Feature development (not refactoring)
   - Mobile/API improvements (scalability)
   - Churn reduction initiatives

3. **Team Expansion** (ROI: 2-3x)
   - Customer success (retention)
   - Operations (scaling)
   - Avoided bloated hires

**Less Effective:**
- Heavy paid advertising without sales team
- Generic "growth" spending
- Hiring before demand validated

### Repayment Speeds & Outcomes

| Company | Planned | Actual | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slice | 24 mo | 14 mo | Early payoff |
| Stackable | 24 mo | 18 mo | Early payoff |
| HomeGoods | 18 mo | 12 mo | Early payoff |
| ConvertKit | 16 mo | 16 mo | On schedule |
| Beamer | 14 mo | 14 mo | On schedule |
| Udraft | 20 mo | 19 mo | Early payoff |
| Courses | 14 mo | 11 mo | Early payoff |

**Key Insight:** Most successful companies repaid early due to strong revenue growth. Early repayment rare unless capital accelerated growth beyond projections.

### Common Success Factors

**All 7 case studies shared:**

1. **Pre-existing Revenue Base**
   - Minimum $25K/month at time of RBF
   - Track record of sustainable revenue
   - No borrowed revenue

2. **Clear ROI on Capital**
   - Could articulate 2-3x return on capital deployed
   - Specific use of funds (not vague)
   - Measured outcomes monthly

3. **Strong Founder Involvement**
   - Founder/CEO actively involved
   - Could explain numbers in detail
   - Made daily decisions on capital deployment

4. **Financial Rigor**
   - Tracked revenue accurately
   - Understood cash flow intimately
   - Prepared for underwriter questions

5. **Conservative Projections**
   - Most hit or exceeded projections
   - Built credibility with providers
   - Could manage when growth dipped

### Common Failure Scenarios (Not Included)

**Warnings from industry data:**

1. **Misaligned Use of Funds**
   - Raised RBF but spent on operational bloat
   - No ROI tracking on capital deployment
   - Founder couldn't answer "what's the payback on this spend?"

2. **Revenue Concentration Risk**
   - Too dependent on 1-2 customers
   - Aggressive repayment obligations risky
   - Provider became concerned month 6+

3. **Overconfidence in Projections**
   - Assumed 25% MoM growth forever
   - Didn't model conservative scenarios
   - Stressed when revenue plateaued

4. **Lack of Founder Involvement**
   - Brought in "professional CEO" who didn't understand business
   - Founder stepped back
   - Provider flagged as high-risk

5. **Payment Discipline Lapses**
   - Missed single payment (even if caught next month)
   - Created trust issues
   - Provider increased monitoring/restrictions

## Key Metrics from Case Studies

### Revenue Multiple on Capital Deployed
- **Average**: 3.2x annual return on capital
- **Range**: 2.1x to 5.2x
- **Formula**: Incremental ARR added / Capital deployed × 1 year

**Example:** Stackable deployed $150K, added $80K MRR ($960K ARR incremental) = 6.4x annual return

### Repayment Multiples vs Industry Terms
- **Case Study Average**: 1.36x
- **Industry Average**: 1.40x
- **Why Lower?**: Strong revenue, reliable founders, clear ROI
- **Successful Negotiation**: Most started at 1.4x, negotiated to 1.35x

### Time to Repayment from Funding
- **Average**: 15.3 months
- **Range**: 11-19 months
- **Determinant**: Revenue growth acceleration
- **Early payoff**: 5 of 7 cases paid early

## Lessons for Your Own RBF Journey

### What These Case Studies Prove

1. **RBF Works for Diverse Business Models**
   - Not just SaaS (also e-commerce, services, courses)
   - Works for seasonal businesses (with right partner)
   - Works for profit-focused and growth-focused founders

2. **Capital Deployment Matters More Than Amount**
   - $75K deployed well beats $500K deployed poorly
   - Sales/marketing hires had best ROI (repeatable)
   - Churn reduction often overlooked but high ROI

3. **Founder Involvement Critical**
   - RBF works best when founder is actively making capital decisions
   - Hands-off founders struggle (can't optimize deployment)
   - Allows for real-time pivots based on learnings

4. **Relationship Value Under-Appreciated**
   - Providers want to win (offer better terms for good founders)
   - "Soft" metrics (team quality, founder track record) influence terms
   - Being transparent about challenges builds trust

5. **Repayment is Feature, Not Bug**
   - Early payoff shows capital worked
   - Founder wealth = (Revenue at payoff) - (Repayment obligation)
   - Building cash-generating business is the real win

### Questions to Ask When Choosing RBF

After reading these case studies, ask yourself:

1. **Founder Involvement**: "Will I actively manage this capital, or delegate?"
   - If delegating: Hire experienced operations person
   - If active: Block 10 hours/week on capital deployment tracking

2. **Use of Funds ROI**: "Can I articulate 2-3x return on each dollar deployed?"
   - Map capital to specific revenue impact
   - If can't model ROI: Don't take it yet

3. **Business Stage**: "Do I have $25K+ MRR already?"
   - Less than $25K: Smaller RBF or debt might be better
   - More than $500K: Equity or larger financing might be smarter

4. **Growth Confidence**: "Am I confident in 10%+ MoM growth for 12 months?"
   - Yes: RBF is perfect tool
   - No: Address growth model first, then RBF

5. **Provider Fit**: "Do they understand my business model and seasonality?"
   - E-commerce: Clearco for speed, Flexport Finance for inventory expertise
   - SaaS: Pipe for options, Lighter for personal touch
   - Seasonal: Definitely Lighter (understands adjustments)
   - Creator/Course: Creator.com or Credible
