## Strategic Application Timing: Revenue Cycles & Seasonal Considerations

### Optimal Timing for Application Windows

#### January - March (Q1)
**Advantages:**
- New year funding cycles begin
- Tax information finalized from prior year
- Time to use capital before peak season
- Aligned with business planning season
- Lighter Capital popular during this period

**Best For:** Tech/SaaS companies, B2B services, businesses planning Q2+ growth

#### April - June (Q2)
**Advantages:**
- Q1 performance data available (proven traction)
- Inventory planning for Q3/Q4
- Summer campaign funding
- Mid-year review cycle

**Best For:** Service-based businesses, seasonal Q3/Q4 planners

#### July - September (Q3)
**Advantages:**
- Q2 financial data strong (if business is growing)
- Back-to-school and holiday inventory prep
- Last window for major seasonal campaigns
- Year-end planning begins

**Best For:** E-commerce, retail, holiday-dependent businesses

#### October - December (Q4)
**Advantages:**
- Post-holiday replenishment
- Year-end tax planning considerations
- Post-holiday revenue available for repayment
- Black Friday/Cyber Monday funding (usually too late, better to fund pre-August)

**Challenges:** Holiday season distractions, slower processing

### Seasonal Business Optimization Strategy

**Critical Principle:** Apply **before** your peak revenue season, not during or after.

**Timeline Framework:**
1. **12 months prior:** Gather baseline revenue data
2. **6 months before peak season:** Begin lender research
3. **3 months before peak season:** Submit applications to 3-5 lenders
4. **2 months before peak season:** Receive funding
5. **Peak season:** Receive capital with flexible payback tied to revenue

**Examples:**

| Business Type | Peak Season | Apply Window | Optimal Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-commerce/DTC | October-December (holiday) | June-August | June, July, August |
| Seasonal retail | Summer (June-August) | February-April | February, March, April |
| Tax services | January-April | August-October | August, September, October |
| Tourism/hospitality | Summer/holidays | April-May & August | April, May, August |
| Back-to-school | July-September | April-May | April, May |
| Gift items | December | July-August | July, August |

**Key Insight:** Seasonal businesses using RBF adjust repayment percentages based on expected revenue patterns (higher % during peak months, lower % during slow months).

## Quarterly Review Periods & Financial Cycles

### Standard RBF Review Schedules

**Monthly Financial Reviews (Most Common)**
- Lenders pull repayment percentage monthly (e.g., 5-10% of monthly revenue)
- Payment dates typically set at contract start
- Recommended: Review actual vs. projected revenue every 30 days
- ACH debit takes 1-2 business days to process
- **Action:** Set calendar reminder 5 days before ACH pull date

**Quarterly Performance Reviews**
- Major lenders often conduct quarterly reviews of business metrics
- Timing: End of Q1 (March 31), Q2 (June 30), Q3 (Sept 30), Q4 (Dec 31)
- Review items: Revenue trend, growth forecast updates, business changes, competitive landscape
- If forecast misses >10-15%, notify lender immediately

**Semi-Annual Contract Reviews**
- Some providers: 6-month and 12-month check-ins
- Opportunity to renegotiate terms if business trajectory changes
- Clearco and Lighter Capital offer mid-term adjustments

**Year-End Planning**
- December 31: Complete annual financial reconciliation
- January: Plan next year's funding strategy
- Optimal time to apply for new capital if previous RBF is paid off

### Recommended Financial Calendar

**Monthly (All RBF Borrowers):**
- Day 1: Record monthly revenue from all sources (Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Square, bank deposits)
- Day 5: Reconcile with accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero, Wave)
- Day 25: Notification from lender of scheduled ACH pull
- Day 30: Reconcile RBF payment

**Quarterly (Every 90 Days):**
- Day 1 of next quarter: Prepare quarterly financial summary
- Calculate: Revenue trend, growth %, cash position, projection accuracy
- Update financial forecast (forward 12 months)
- Review lender contract terms for any changes
- Communicate with lender if variance >10%

**Semi-Annually (Every 6 Months):**
- 30-day check-in: Assessment of business against plan
- Renegotiation opportunity window
- Review repayment cap progress toward payoff
- Assess need for additional capital

**Annually (Year-End & Anniversary):**
- Day 1: Compile complete financial year summary
- Tax preparation: RBF payments, interest, fees
- Payment timeline review: On track to pay off by agreed date?
- Plan next year's capital needs
- Generate auditable RBF payment documentation

## Application Deadlines & Multiple Lender Strategy

### Simultaneous Multi-Lender Application Timeline (Recommended Strategy)

**Week 1: Preparation**
- Day 1-2: Choose 3-5 lenders based on your business type
- Day 3-5: Gather documents (financial statements, tax returns, revenue verification)
- Day 5-7: Create shared folder with all materials (Google Drive/Notion)

**Week 2: Initial Contact**
- Day 8-10: Complete pre-qualification calls with 3-5 lenders
- Ask: "What's your typical approval timeline? Any upcoming deadlines?"
- Document responses in spreadsheet
- Day 11-14: Submit applications to all lenders on same day

**Week 3: Active Underwriting**
- **Set calendar alerts: 48 hours after submission** - If no contact, follow up
- Track application reference numbers for each lender
- Day 15-21: Expect initial feedback/requests for additional info
- Respond within 24 hours to all inquiries

**Week 4: Offers & Comparison**
- Day 22-28: Receive term sheets from multiple lenders
- Compare: Repayment %, cap, timeline, flexibility, payment schedule
- Negotiate if needed (many lenders will match competitor offers)
- Accept best offer; inform other lenders you've selected different partner

**Week 5-6: Documentation & Funding**
- Day 29-35: Complete final documentation
- Day 36-42: Receive funds (varies by lender)

## Critical Timing Considerations for RBF

### Data Quality & Recency Requirements

**Minimum Historical Data Needed:**
- Clearco: 3+ months of transaction history (fastest lenders)
- Lighter Capital: 6+ months preferred, 3+ months minimum
- Pipe: 3-6 months of clean financial data
- Capchase: 12+ months of contract/ARR data (optimal)
- General rule: 12 months of operational history = best terms

**Data Freshness Rules:**
- Most lenders prefer data updated within last 30 days
- Monthly reconciliation required during repayment period
- Year-end data takes longer to process (January slowdown)

### Avoid These Timing Mistakes

1. **Applying during fiscal year-end (December):** Processing slower, data reconciliation complex
2. **Applying during personal/business crises:** Underwriters detect instability
3. **Applying with outdated financial data:** Instant rejection or severe term penalty
4. **Failing to meet 48-hour follow-up deadline:** Lender moves on to next applicants
5. **Inconsistent data sources:** QuickBooks ≠ Stripe ≠ bank deposits = red flag
6. **Applying AFTER peak season:** You miss the capital you needed most
7. **Sole reliance on one lender:** Set 3-5 applications simultaneously for leverage

### Acceleration Tactics for Faster Funding

**To get funded fastest (Clearco benchmark: 2-7 days):**

1. **Pre-application preparation:**
   - Verify all financial data is accessible (Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks, etc.)
   - Clean up accounting records 1 month before applying
   - Ensure business account access is ready

2. **During application:**
   - Complete application during business hours (lenders prioritize during office hours)
   - Have all documents ready to upload immediately
   - Set phone appointment within 24 hours of submission

3. **After submission:**
   - Respond to ALL requests within 4 hours (not 24 hours)
   - Have backup financial statements ready (bank statements, accountant records)
   - Keep phone nearby for underwriter calls

4. **Best-case scenario timing:**
   - Tuesday-Thursday applications (avoid Monday/Friday backlogs)
   - Morning submissions (9-11 AM EST) get processed same day
   - Businesses with <12 months history: Add 2-5 days to processing time
   - Seasonal businesses: May need additional documentation (adds 3-5 days)

## Payment Schedule & Repayment Timeline Planning

### Monthly Repayment Cycles

**Payment Calculation:**
- Date set at contract: Monthly revenue % pulled automatically
- ACH debit timing: 1-2 business days to clear
- Example: 5% monthly repayment on $100K revenue month = $5,000 payment

**Set Up Payment Readiness:**
- Day 25 of each month: Lender notifies of pending ACH pull
- Day 28: Ensure sufficient funds in checking account
- Day 30 (or contract date): ACH debit processes
- Day 31: Verify payment in lender dashboard and accounting system

**Calendar Reminders to Set:**
- Month 1: Set recurring "RBF Payment Due" alert 5 days before pull date
- Track repayment percentage progress toward cap (1.32x-1.5x)
- Monitor: Days remaining until payoff at current revenue trajectory

### Repayment Cap Milestones

**Track Progress Toward Repayment Cap:**

Example: $100,000 advance with 1.35x cap = $135,000 total repayment required

| Month | Revenue | 5% Payment | Cumulative Paid | % Toward Cap | Remaining |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | $100K | $5,000 | $5,000 | 3.7% | $130,000 |
| Month 3 | $120K | $6,000 | $18,000 | 13.3% | $117,000 |
| Month 6 | $150K | $7,500 | $42,500 | 31.5% | $92,500 |
| Month 12 | $200K | $10,000 | $115,000 | 85.2% | $20,000 |
| Month 14 | $250K | $12,500 | $140,000 | 103.7% | **PAID OFF** |

**Key Planning Point:** Most RBF borrowers pay off in 9-18 months if revenue is consistent.
