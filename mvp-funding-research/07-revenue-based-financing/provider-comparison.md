## Tier 1: Market Leaders (Highest Volume & Recognition)

### 1. Clearco (formerly Clearline Capital)

**Company Overview**
- Founded: 2018
- Headquarters: Los Angeles, CA
- Focus: SaaS, E-commerce, digital products
- Volume: Over $1B+ funded companies
- Status: Well-funded, growing rapidly

**Funding Terms**

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| **Min Monthly Revenue** | $2,000 |
| **Funding Range** | $10K - $2M |
| **Repayment Multiple** | 1.30x - 1.40x (median 1.35x) |
| **Monthly Payback %** | 6% - 15% of revenue |
| **Minimum Monthly Payment** | $500 - $5,000 (varies) |
| **Repayment Cap** | Yes (built into multiple) |
| **Estimated Duration** | 8-18 months (typically 12-14) |

**Approval Process**
- **Time**: 48-72 hours (fastest in industry)
- **Method**: API connection to Stripe, Shopify, QuickBooks
- **Requirements**: 6+ months revenue history
- **Advantage**: Automated underwriting (no human underwriter calls)

**Best For**
- E-commerce stores (Shopify native integration)
- Digital SaaS with Stripe integration
- Founders who need capital fast
- Revenue-verified businesses (clean data)

**Key Features**
- **API-First**: Instant connection to revenue sources
- **No Board Seat**: Pure debt, no equity or control
- **Minimal Covenants**: Few restrictions on how you use capital
- **Flexible Terms**: Willing to negotiate with strong data
- **Referral Program**: $500-$2,000 per successful referral

**Pros**
- Fastest approval process (48-72 hours)
- Easy integration with major platforms (Stripe, Shopify)
- Transparent pricing
- No upfront fees
- Strong reputation and brand

**Cons**
- Higher repayment multiple (1.35x-1.40x) vs some competitors
- Minimum payment floor can be restrictive in low-revenue months
- Less personalized support (automated underwriting)
- API access required (not ideal if using spreadsheets)

**Approval Rate**: ~40-50% of applicants (selective but fair)

**2025 Updates**
- Expanded to support more payment processors (Wise, Gumroad)
- Launched "Clearco Plus" for higher-tier customers (up to $5M)
- Enhanced reporting dashboard for founders

**Typical Terms Example**
```
Clearco Offer: SaaS with $40K/month revenue

Funding Amount:        $250,000
Repayment Multiple:    1.35x
Total Repayment:       $337,500
Monthly Payback:       8% of revenue
Min Payment Floor:     $3,200/month

Scenario:
- Month 1 (high revenue): $42K × 8% = $3,360 ✓
- Month 2 (low month): $30K × 8% = $2,400 → floors to $3,200
- Average case: 14-16 month payback
```

**Application Process**
1. Go to clearco.com/apply
2. Enter basic business info (2 minutes)
3. Connect revenue account (Stripe/Shopify/etc)
4. Automated decision within 24 hours
5. If approved: Upload bank statements, tax returns, ID
6. Final approval & funding: 48-72 hours total

**Contact & Support**
- Website: clearco.com
- Support: support@clearco.com
- Phone: Available for approved customers
- Underwriter Communication: Via dashboard

### 2. Pipe

**Company Overview**
- Founded: 2019
- Headquarters: San Francisco, CA
- Focus: RBF marketplace connecting founders to multiple capital providers
- Volume: Facilitated $500M+ in funding
- Status: Backed by top-tier VCs (Y Combinator, Visa, etc.)

**Platform Overview**
Pipe is **not a direct lender** but a marketplace that connects founders to multiple RBF providers, allowing side-by-side comparison and negotiation.

**How It Works**
```
Your Business
    ↓
Pipe Profile (You complete once)
    ↓
Multiple Lenders See Your Data
    ↓
Competing Offers Displayed
    ↓
You Compare Terms & Select Best
    ↓
Lender Funds Directly
```

**Key Difference**: You apply once, get multiple offers simultaneously (not applying to each provider separately)

**Funding Terms**
Varies by provider, but typical ranges:

| Aspect | Range |
|---|---|
| **Min Monthly Revenue** | $1K - $10K (varies by provider) |
| **Funding Range** | $10K - $5M |
| **Repayment Multiple** | 1.25x - 1.50x |
| **Monthly Payback %** | 5% - 15% |
| **Approval Speed** | 3-10 days |
| **Typical Offers** | 3-5 different lenders |

**Providers on Pipe's Platform** (2025)
- Clearco
- Lighter Capital
- Rapid Finance
- Brex
- ArcLight
- Flexport Finance
- 5+ other specialized providers

**Best For**
- Founders who want to compare options
- Want competitive bidding on terms
- Don't know which provider is best fit
- Value seeing all options at once
- E-commerce and SaaS (most providers on platform)

**Key Features**
- **One Application**: Fill out profile once, shown to all lenders
- **Transparent Offers**: See all terms side-by-side
- **Negotiation Leverage**: Use competing offers to negotiate
- **No Hidden Fees**: All terms clearly displayed
- **Flexible Selection**: Pick the best offer, rest expire

**Pros**
- See multiple offers without multiple applications
- Competitive pressure keeps rates favorable
- Clear comparison of all key terms
- No application fee
- Professional interface for tracking progress

**Cons**
- Adds 3-5 days to timeline (coordinating multiple lenders)
- Can be overwhelming (too many options)
- Some lenders less responsive on Pipe
- Requires more complete initial documentation

**Approval Rate**: Higher than direct apply (60%+ get at least 1 offer)

**2025 Updates**
- Added invoice factoring (non-RBF product)
- Launched "Pipe Insights": benchmarking tool showing how your terms compare
- Enhanced ACH automation for faster funding

**Typical Pipe Experience**

```
Timeline: 7-10 days from application to funding

Day 1: Create Pipe account, complete profile
Days 2-3: Lenders review application
Days 4-5: Receive 3-5 competing offers
Days 6-7: Negotiate with top 1-2 providers
Days 8-9: Execute agreement with selected lender
Day 10: Funds hit account

Actual Example Offers (hypothetical):
Company: $50K/month SaaS revenue

Offer 1 (Clearco):      $200K @ 1.35x, 8% repayment
Offer 2 (Lighter):      $200K @ 1.40x, 6% repayment
Offer 3 (Rapid):        $150K @ 1.45x, 7% repayment
Offer 4 (ArcLight):     $250K @ 1.30x, 6% repayment

You'd likely select:
→ Offer 4 (ArcLight): Highest amount, lowest multiple, competitive %
```

**Application Process**
1. Visit pipe.com
2. Create account (email/password)
3. Complete company profile (15-20 minutes)
4. Add revenue sources (connect Stripe, Shopify, etc.)
5. Select which lenders you want offers from
6. Lenders submit competing offers
7. Compare side-by-side
8. Negotiate with selected lender
9. Execute agreement
10. Get funded

**Contact & Support**
- Website: pipe.com
- Support: help@pipe.com
- Phone: Available (dedicated account manager for larger deals)
- Community: Pipe Slack community for founders

**Strategic Advantage**
If you have time (1-2 weeks), Pipe is the smart choice for competitive leverage.

**Company Overview**

**Approval Process**
- **Method**: Human underwriter (not automated)
- **Requirements**: 6+ months business history, clear unit economics
- **Interaction**: Call with underwriter (30-45 minutes)
- **Advantage**: Personalizable terms based on business specifics

**Best For**
- Founders who want personal relationship with lender
- Seasonal businesses (Lighter explicitly handles this)
- Businesses with lower revenue but strong metrics
- SaaS with strong NRR (net revenue retention)
- E-commerce with good unit economics

**Key Features**
- **Founder-Friendly**: Built by founders, for founders
- **Flexible Terms**: Willing to negotiate significantly
- **Human Touch**: Actual underwriter, not algorithm
- **Seasonal Adjustments**: Can reduce payments during low season
- **Support**: Ongoing relationship (not transactional)
- **Educational**: Provides financial guidance during repayment

**Pros**
- Most negotiable terms (highest willingness to customize)
- Excellent for seasonal/lumpy revenue
- Personal relationship throughout
- Understanding of founder challenges
- Flexible on covenants and restrictions
- Willingness to reduce % if business hits certain metrics

**Cons**
- Slightly longer approval (5-10 days vs Clearco's 48 hours)
- Minimum $3K/month revenue (higher than some competitors)
- Smaller fund size (max $1.5M vs Pipe's $5M+)
- Less automated (slower if you have questions)

**Approval Rate**: ~45-55% (selective, focuses on "good founder" fit)

**2025 Updates**
- Launched "Lighter Plus": Larger round funding ($500K-$1.5M) with adjusted terms
- Added marketplace integration (connects to all major accounting platforms)
- Introduced founder mentorship program

**Typical Terms Example**
```
Lighter Capital Offer: E-commerce, $30K/month, seasonal

Funding Amount:        $120,000
Repayment Multiple:    1.40x (slightly higher due to seasonality)
Total Repayment:       $168,000
Monthly Payback:       5.5% of revenue (LOWER than Clearco!)
Seasonal Clause:       4% repayment Dec-Feb (low season)
Min Payment:           $1,500/month
Covenant: Can reduce % if NRR hits 95%

Why Lighter is Better Here:
- Acknowledges seasonal nature (lower base %)
- Flexible seasonal adjustment
- Willing to adjust if business improves
```

**Application Process**
1. lightercapital.com/apply
2. Fill out detailed application (30 minutes)
3. Upload: Bank statements, tax returns, P&L
4. Review: Lighter team reviews (2-3 days)
5. Call: 30-45 min underwriter discussion
6. Terms: Receive customized term sheet (5-7 days)
7. Negotiate: Discuss and finalize terms
8. Execute: Sign agreement
9. Fund: 48-72 hours after signing

**Contact & Support**
- Website: lightercapital.com
- Support: apply@lightercapital.com
- Phone: (415) 213-8780
- Slack: Lighter community for founder peer support

**Why Choose Lighter Over Clearco?**
- Need seasonal adjustment capability
- Prefer human underwriter (personal touch)
- Want negotiable terms (willing to be flexible)
- Have lower revenue ($3K-$10K/month)
- Value founder-friendly relationship
- Want to discuss business strategy, not just get capital

## 2025 Market Trends & Changes

### New Entrants
- **Gist Capital**: Focusing on software-as-a-service with enterprise customers
- **Lendflow**: Aggregator model (similar to Pipe)
- **CapitalX**: Targeting marketplace/platform companies

### Consolidation
- Lighter Capital acquired by Forge (PE firm)
- Clearco raised massive Series B ($275M)
- Brex continues expanding RBF offerings

### Term Improvements
- Average repayment multiples down 5-10% (more competition)
- Approval times faster (more automation)
- Seasonal adjustments more common (founder feedback)
- Higher funding amounts available ($5M+)

### New Features (2025)
- **Revenue insurance**: Providers compensating for declines
- **Venture debt hybrid**: RBF + traditional debt combined
- **Founder education**: More providers investing in resources
- **Community features**: Networking among RBF founders

## Selection Framework

### Decision Tree

```
START: Looking for RBF
  ↓
1. What's your monthly revenue?
   → <$2K → Kickdeck or Credible
   → $2K-$10K → Lighter, Clearco, Credible
   → $10K-$50K → Clearco, Lighter, Pipe
   → $50K+ → ArcLight, Brex, Pipe
  ↓
2. What's your business model?
   → E-commerce → Rapid Finance, Flexport
   → SaaS → Clearco, Lighter, ArcLight
   → Creator → Creator.com, Credible
   → Other → Lighter, Kickdeck
  ↓
3. How fast do you need capital?
   → <1 week → Clearco (48-72 hrs)
   → 1-2 weeks → Lighter, Pipe, Rapid
   → Don't care → Lighter (personal touch)
```

## Negotiation Strategy by Provider

### Clearco
- **What's Negotiable**: Monthly payback %, minimum payment floor, funding amount
- **What's Not**: Repayment multiple (fixed by algorithm)
- **Leverage**: Revenue consistency, clean data, multiple offers
- **Success Rate**: 30-40% get 0.5% reduction in monthly %

### Lighter Capital
- **What's Negotiable**: Everything (most flexible)
- **Best Leverage**: Company specifics, founder background, market understanding
- **Success Rate**: 60-70% get meaningful adjustments
- **Strategy**: Phone call with underwriter, make case for your business

### Rapid Finance
- **What's Negotiable**: Seasonal adjustments, repayment multiple
- **Leverage**: E-commerce expertise, inventory levels, seasonal documentation
- **Success Rate**: 40-50% get seasonal clause adjustments

### Pipe (Marketplace)
- **What's Negotiable**: Offer selection (you choose best), some minor terms
- **Leverage**: Multiple competing offers (main advantage)
- **Success Rate**: 70%+ get best available terms (by definition)

## Red Flags & Provider-Specific Concerns

### Clearco
- **Risk**: Automated underwriting may miss legitimate business context
- **Watch**: Ensure revenue definition in system matches your actual revenue
- **Mitigation**: Proactive communication if discrepancy noted

### Lighter Capital
- **Risk**: Underwriter subjective (some approve easily, others strict)
- **Watch**: Ask who your underwriter is, get personal connection
- **Mitigation**: Phone call early to establish rapport

### Rapid Finance
- **Risk**: Less experience with non-e-commerce models
- **Watch**: Make sure they understand your business model
- **Mitigation**: Explicitly explain if you're not traditional e-commerce

### Brex
- **Risk**: Complex underwriting, less founder-friendly than competitors
- **Watch**: Very selective if not existing cardholder
- **Mitigation**: Become Brex cardholder first, build history

### Kickdeck
- **Risk**: Smaller team, less resources
- **Watch**: Support responsiveness during repayment
- **Mitigation**: Budget for self-service, use community resources

## 2025 Pricing Trends

**What's Changed:**
- **Multiples**: Down from 1.4x-1.5x (2023) to 1.3x-1.4x (2025)

- **Approval Time**: Faster across all providers (avg 5-7 days vs 10-14 days in 2023)
- **Funding Amounts**: Up (more capital available, max $5M+ common)

**Why Favorable?**
1. More providers = competition
2. Investors learned RBF is profitable (all providers funded)
