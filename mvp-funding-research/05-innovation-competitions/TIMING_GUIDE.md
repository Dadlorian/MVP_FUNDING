## RED FLAGS & TIMING ISSUES

### Common Deadline Mistakes
- **Missing deadlines by hours**: Apply 48+ hours early, never at deadline
- **Not checking regional variations**: Same competition has different deadlines by region
- **Time zone confusion**: Verify PT vs CT vs ET deadlines
- **Forgetting video requirements**: Some competitions have 3-4 week video turnaround needed

### Seasonal Trends
- **Slowest Period**: December 25 - January 5 (holidays)
- **Busiest Application Period**: August-October (TechCrunch, Slush, awards)
- **Busiest Event Period**: October-November (TechCrunch Disrupt, Slush, SWC Grand Finale)
- **Best for Acceptance**: January-March (less competition, still strong judging panels)

### Submission Timeline Planning
- **Video competitions**: Start 3-4 weeks before deadline
- **Written applications**: 2 weeks minimum for quality writing
- **Live pitch events**: Register 2-3 months early for logistics
- **Demo-required**: Ensure demo is production-ready 1 month before event
