# Communications: Responses and Rebuttals Templates

## Overview
This document contains 35+ professional response templates for managing crisis communications, competitor claims, customer issues, regulatory matters, investor concerns, and social media crises. Each template is provided in three versions:

- **Diplomatic**: Collaborative, relationship-focused tone
- **Assertive**: Strong, confident, professionally firm tone
- **Legal Review**: Conservative wording suitable for legal scrutiny

---

## Section 1: Negative Press Response Templates (6 templates)

### 1.1 General Negative Coverage Response

#### Diplomatic Version

3. Prior art [reference documents] demonstrate that [key elements] predate [competitor]'s patent filing.

[Company] will continue its operations as currently conducted and reserves all rights and remedies. [Company] has documented its non-infringing status and welcomes technical review by neutral experts or USPTO proceedings.

## Template Usage Guidelines

### When to Use Each Version

**Diplomatic Version**:
- Best for: Ongoing relationships, customer retention, partnership preservation
- Use when: Stakeholder relationship preservation is priority
- Tone: Collaborative, understanding, solutions-focused

**Assertive Version**:
- Best for: Defending reputation, setting record straight, establishing credibility
- Use when: Facts are clearly on your side, stakeholder relationship is secondary
- Tone: Confident, direct, factual, professionally firm

**Legal Review Version**:
- Best for: High-stakes matters, regulatory issues, potential litigation
- Use when: Legal risk is present or matter may escalate legally
- Tone: Careful, precise, legally protective, formal

### Best Practices

1. **Personalize**: Always customize templates with specific facts, names, dates, and context
2. **Verify Facts**: Ensure all statements are factually accurate before sending
3. **Speed**: Respond to issues promptly to prevent escalation
4. **Consistency**: Coordinate messaging across all channels
5. **Documentation**: Keep records of all communications sent
6. **Escalation**: Have legal review high-stakes or sensitive responses
7. **Follow-up**: Provide timeline for next steps or additional information
8. **Tone Matching**: Ensure selected version matches your strategic objective
