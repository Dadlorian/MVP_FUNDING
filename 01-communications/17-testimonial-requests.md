## USAGE NOTES & TIPS

### Customization Strategy

- **Personalization**: Always customize templates with specific metrics, challenges, or achievements relevant to the recipient
- **Timing**: Send testimonial requests within 30 days of project completion or achievement milestone
- **Incentives**: Consider non-monetary rewards (co-marketing, feature highlights, exclusive access) alongside or instead of cash
- **Ease**: Make participation as easy as possible—offer multiple formats (written, video, phone call)

### What Makes Effective Testimonial Requests

1. **Specificity**: Reference specific results, challenges, or features they used
2. **Authenticity**: Let people know you genuinely value their feedback
3. **Brevity**: Keep requests short—people respond better to concise asks
4. **Options**: Offer multiple formats or time commitments
5. **Timing**: Follow up after clear value has been delivered
6. **Respect**: Never pressure; always provide an easy "no" path

### Follow-Up Schedule

- **Day 0**: Initial request
- **Day 5-7**: First gentle follow-up
- **Day 14-21**: Second follow-up (offer to make it easier)
- **Day 28**: Final follow-up with deadline or alternative ask
- **Stop**: After 4 touches, accept non-response as a "no"

### Best Practices

- **Video testimonials** generate 5x more conversions than written
- **Customer names and titles** are critical—anonymous testimonials have low credibility
- **Specific metrics** (40% efficiency gain, $500K saved) beat vague claims
- **Challenges overcome** are more compelling than easy wins
- **Third-party platforms** (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot) add credibility
- **Reciprocity** works—be willing to write recommendations/testimonials first

### Response Rate Optimization

- Video requests: 25-30% response rate
- Simple written requests: 40-50% response rate
- Phone call approach: 60-70% response rate
- Provide incentives: +15-20% increase in response rate
- Multiple follow-ups: +25% additional responses
