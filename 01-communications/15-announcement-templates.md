# Announcement Templates

## Executive Overview

This document contains **40+ ready-to-use announcement templates** for internal and external communications across multiple announcement categories. Each template includes placeholder variables (marked with [BRACKETS]) that should be customized for your specific use case.

### Template Variables Reference

- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Your company/product name
- `[FOUNDER_NAME]` - Founder or CEO name
- `[INVESTMENT_AMOUNT]` - Funding round amount (e.g., $2M)
- `[INVESTOR_NAME]` - Lead investor or investor names
- `[INVESTOR_LIST]` - List of multiple investors
- `[FUNDING_USE]` - How the funding will be used
- `[PRODUCT_NAME]` - New product or feature name
- `[PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION]` - Product feature description
- `[PRODUCT_BENEFITS]` - Key benefits of the product
- `[LAUNCH_DATE]` - Product launch date
- `[TEAM_MEMBER_NAME]` - Employee name
- `[TEAM_MEMBER_ROLE]` - Job title
- `[TEAM_MEMBER_BIO]` - Professional background/bio
- `[TEAM_MEMBER_QUOTE]` - Quote from team member
- `[PREVIOUS_COMPANY]` - Previous company worked at
- `[LOCATION]` - Office city/location
- `[OFFICE_ADDRESS]` - Full office address
- `[OFFICE_LAUNCH_DATE]` - Date office opens
- `[CURRENT_NAME]` - Current/old company name
- `[NEW_NAME]` - New company name
- `[REBRANDING_REASON]` - Why the rebrand is happening
- `[REBRANDING_DATE]` - Date rebranding takes effect
- `[ACQUISITION_COMPANY]` - Company being acquired
- `[ACQUISITION_AMOUNT]` - Acquisition price
- `[ACQUISITION_TERMS]` - Deal terms/details
- `[ACQUISITION_DATE]` - Acquisition completion date
- `[AWARD_NAME]` - Award or recognition name
- `[AWARD_CATEGORY]` - Category or type of award
- `[AWARDING_ORGANIZATION]` - Organization giving award
- `[EVENT_NAME]` - Event name
- `[EVENT_DATE]` - Event date
- `[EVENT_LOCATION]` - Event location
- `[EVENT_DESCRIPTION]` - Event details
- `[PIVOT_EXPLANATION]` - How and why the pivot is happening
- `[PREVIOUS_FOCUS]` - Previous business focus
- `[NEW_FOCUS]` - New business focus
- `[POLICY_NAME]` - Name of policy change
- `[POLICY_DETAILS]` - Policy details
- `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]` - When policy takes effect
- `[METRIC]` - Quantifiable achievement
- `[IMPACT]` - Business impact
- `[QUOTE]` - Direct quote
- `[HASHTAGS]` - Relevant hashtags

### 1. SEED FUNDING ROUND - EXTERNAL

**Channel:** Press release, email, social media
**Audience:** Customers, partners, media, general public

```
[COMPANY_DESCRIPTION]

```

## USAGE GUIDELINES

### When to Use These Templates

- **Executive announcements** (funding, acquisitions, major pivots): Use press release format, customize with specific details
- **Team announcements** (new hires, departures): Tailor depth and tone based on seniority level
- **Policy changes** (remote work, benefits): Be clear, direct, and provide timeline for implementation
- **Internal vs. External**: Internal versions can be more casual and transparent; external should be polished and strategic
- **Multi-channel approach**: Major announcements often need versions for: press, social media, customer email, employee email

### Best Practices

1. **Customize thoroughly** - Replace all [BRACKETS] with real information
2. **Tone matching** - Ensure tone matches your company culture
3. **Timing** - Coordinate announcement timing across channels
4. **Internal first** - Always communicate internally before external announcements
5. **Clear action items** - Include what recipients should do next
6. **FAQ preparation** - Anticipate questions for major announcements
7. **Stakeholder alignment** - Get approvals from relevant leaders before sending
8. **Archive copies** - Keep records of all announcements for consistency

### Distribution Strategy

| Announcement Type | Internal First | Days Before External | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Funding | Yes | 1-2 days | All-hands, email, social, press |
| Product Launch | Yes | 1 day | Email, blog, social, press |
| Team Changes | Yes | Immediate | Team email, all-hands, Slack |
| Office Changes | Yes | 1-2 weeks | Email, all-hands, HR |
| Pivots | Yes | 1-2 weeks | All-hands, email, press, investor update |
| Acquisitions | Yes | Coordinated announcement | Press, email, all-hands |
| Policy Changes | Yes | Immediate | Email, all-hands, HR |
| Awards | No | Immediate | Social, press, email |
| Events | No | 1-2 weeks prior | Email, social, website |
| Partnerships | No | Coordinated | Press, blog, social |
