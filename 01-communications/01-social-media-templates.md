# Social Media Communications Templates

## Executive Overview

This document contains **60+ ready-to-use templates** for social media communications across Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. Each template includes placeholder variables (marked with [BRACKETS]) that should be customized for your specific use case.

### Template Variables Reference

- `[COMPANY_NAME]` - Your company/product name
- `[FOUNDER_NAME]` - Founder or CEO name
- `[INVESTMENT_AMOUNT]` - Funding round amount (e.g., $2M)
- `[INVESTOR_NAME]` - Lead investor or investor names
- `[PARTNER_NAME]` - Partnership company name
- `[MILESTONE]` - Key achievement or milestone
- `[METRIC]` - Quantifiable achievement (e.g., 10K users)
- `[TIMEFRAME]` - Time period (e.g., in 6 months, last quarter)
- `[FEATURE_NAME]` - New product/feature name
- `[PROBLEM_STATEMENT]` - Problem being solved
- `[SOLUTION_DESCRIPTION]` - How it's being solved
- `[TEAM_MEMBER_NAME]` - Employee name
- `[TEAM_MEMBER_ROLE]` - Job title
- `[QUOTE]` - Direct quote or testimonial
- `[STATISTIC]` - Data point or research finding
- `[HASHTAGS]` - Relevant hashtags (platform-specific)
- `[CALL_TO_ACTION]` - Action you want audience to take
- `[IMPACT_METRIC]` - Business impact (users, revenue, engagement)

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC BEST PRACTICES

### Twitter/X Guidelines
- Keep it under 280 characters (but use the full space when needed)
- Use threads for longer content
- Engage within the first hour of posting
- Respond to comments and quote-tweets
- Use relevant, trending hashtags (1-3 maximum)
- Timing: Best posts at 8am-10am or 5pm-7pm on weekdays

### LinkedIn Guidelines
- Write like you're talking to professionals
- Use line breaks for readability
- Include 3-5 relevant hashtags
- Share personal stories that connect to business
- Timing: Best posts Tuesday-Thursday, 7am-9am or 12pm-1pm
- Document learnings and insights from experiences

### Facebook Guidelines
- Use conversational, warm tone
- Encourage comments with questions
- Share longer-form stories
- Use 5-10 relevant hashtags
- Include emojis for visual interest
- Timing: Best posts Tuesday-Sunday, 1pm-3pm

### Instagram Guidelines
- Prioritize high-quality visuals
- Use 15-30 hashtags (but naturally integrated)
- Write captions that tell stories
- Use Alt Text for accessibility
- Include Call-To-Actions (CTAs)
- Timing: Best posts Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-12pm or 7pm-9pm
- Reels are prioritized over static posts

### TikTok Guidelines
- Use trending sounds and hashtags
- Keep videos under 60 seconds initially
- Post consistently (3-5x per week minimum)
- Engage with comments in first 24 hours
- Use text overlays for clarity
- Trends matter more than polish
- Gen Z and Millennial language
- Show personality and authenticity

## CONTENT CALENDAR STRATEGY

### Weekly Balance (Suggested Ratio)
- **40%**: Product/Feature updates and company news
- **25%**: Thought leadership and industry insights
- **20%**: Team/culture and behind-the-scenes
- **10%**: Engagement and community
- **5%**: Crisis/Urgent communications

### Monthly Themes
- **Week 1**: Product announcements and launches
- **Week 2**: Thought leadership and insights
- **Week 3**: Team highlights and culture
- **Week 4**: Community building and engagement

### Seasonal Opportunities
- **Q1**: New year goals, partnership announcements
- **Q2**: Product launches, fundraising announcements
- **Q3**: Growth celebrations, team expansion
- **Q4**: Year-end reviews, looking ahead posts

## GENERAL TEMPLATE CUSTOMIZATION TIPS

1. **Adjust Tone by Platform**
   - Twitter/X: Fast, witty, conversational
   - LinkedIn: Professional, thoughtful, strategic
   - Facebook: Warm, community-focused, accessible
   - Instagram: Visual, lifestyle, personality-driven
   - TikTok: Playful, trendy, authentic

2. **Timing & Frequency**
   - Twitter/X: 1-3 posts per day
   - LinkedIn: 2-4 posts per week
   - Facebook: 1-2 posts per week
   - Instagram: 3-5 posts per week
   - TikTok: 3-5 videos per week

3. **Before You Post**
   - [ ] Check for typos and grammar
   - [ ] Verify all [VARIABLES] are filled in
   - [ ] Confirm links work
   - [ ] Test emojis display correctly
   - [ ] Schedule or queue if appropriate
   - [ ] Plan responses to likely comments

4. **Measuring Success**
   - Track engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
   - Monitor conversation sentiment
   - Measure click-through rates on links
   - Track follower growth
   - Analyze which templates resonate most
   - A/B test different messaging
