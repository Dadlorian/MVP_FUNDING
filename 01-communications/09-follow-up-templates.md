## Post-Meeting Follow-Ups

**Timing:** Send within 24 hours of meeting

### Template 1: Brief Executive Post-Meeting

**Send within:** 4-24 hours
**Best for:** Investors, partners, key stakeholders

Subject: Great meeting you today - next steps

Hi [Name],

Thank you so much for taking the time to meet with me today. I really appreciated your insights on [specific topic discussed], particularly your perspective on [specific comment they made].

As we discussed, our next steps are:
- [Action item 1] - I'll handle by [date]
- [Action item 2] - You'll review by [date]
- [Action item 3] - We'll connect on [date]

I'm attaching [relevant materials] for your reference. Please let me know if you have any questions or need any clarifications.

Looking forward to continuing our conversation on [date/topic].

Best,
[Your Name]

## No-Response Follow-Ups

**Timing:** Vary based on initial contact date

### Template 6: First Follow-Up (3-5 Days After Initial Outreach)

**Send after:** 3-5 days of no response
**Subject line style:** Casual, value-focused

Subject: Wanted to share something relevant to [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I realize my email last week might have gotten buried. No pressure at all - inboxes are crazy.

I came across [article/news/data] about [relevant topic] and thought of our conversation. Felt worth sharing: [brief context].

If you're open to a quick conversation, I'm flexible. If not, totally understand.

[Your Name]

---

### Template 7: Second Follow-Up (7-10 Days After Initial)

**Send after:** No response to first follow-up
**Goal:** Pivot angle, add value

Subject: [Name], quick question for you

Hi [Name],

I might be approaching this wrong - let me ask you directly: is now a good time to explore [topic/opportunity], or are you heads-down on something else?

[Alternative angle/value prop]: Many [peer type] are focused on [trend/solution]. Is that on your radar at all?

No worries if the timing isn't right. Happy to reconnect in [timeframe].

[Your Name]

---

### Template 8: Third Follow-Up (14 Days After Initial)

**Send after:** Two prior no-responses
**Approach:** Provide clear exit option

Subject: Last attempt to connect - [Company Name]

Hi [Name],

I'm going to take another approach here. I've been trying to get time on your calendar to discuss [topic], but I understand you might not be interested or it's just not the right timing.

Rather than keep pinging you, I'll do this: if [specific condition/update/milestone] happens, would it make sense to reconnect? If so, I'll follow up then.

If you'd prefer I don't reach out again, just let me know.

Either way, wishing you great success with [Company/Initiative].

Best,
[Your Name]

## Multi-Touch Sequences

**Strategic approach:** Planned sequences with specific timing and messaging angles

### 3-Touch Sequence: The Quick Exploration

**Timeline:** Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 10
**Best for:** Early-stage conversations, initial interest testing

#### Touch 1: Initial Outreach (Day 1)

Subject: Quick question about [their area of expertise]

Hi [Name],

I'm [brief intro] and we're exploring [topic/approach]. Given your background in [their expertise], I'd love to get your perspective on something.

[Specific question related to their expertise]

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to pick your brain? Happy to return the favor on anything in my wheelhouse.

Best,
[Your Name]

**No response → move to Touch 2 after 3 days**

---

#### Touch 2: New Angle (Day 4)

Subject: [Name], one more thought on [previous topic]

Hi [Name],

I didn't hear back - no worries, I know you're busy. But I came across [relevant article/news/insight] that directly relates to what I was asking.

[2-3 sentence insight from the article]

Thought you should see this either way. If you want to chat about it, I'm around.

[Your Name]

**No response → move to Touch 3 after 6 days**

---

#### Touch 3: Graceful Exit (Day 10)

Subject: [Name] - let's stay connected

Hi [Name],

I've been trying to get some time with you to pick your brain, but I know timing isn't always right.

Here's what I'll do: I'll stop pinging you, but I'm going to stay on your radar. If [specific trigger/milestone] happens in the next [timeframe], I'll reach back out.

In the meantime, if you think of anything I could help with, I'm a message away.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending any follow-up, ensure you:

- [ ] Use recipient's correct name and title
- [ ] Reference a specific detail from your previous interaction
- [ ] Address their specific concern, interest, or goal
- [ ] Include at least one personalized insight or observation
- [ ] Reference a specific company/project they're working on
- [ ] Make it clear why you're reaching out to *them* specifically
- [ ] Provide a clear, easy next step
- [ ] Keep the ask reasonable for the stage
- [ ] Proofread for spelling and grammar
- [ ] Test links and attachments before sending

---

## Timing Best Practices

### First Follow-Up Window
- **Best time:** 3-5 days after initial contact
- **Avoid:** Immediate re-contact, excessive frequency
- **Goal:** New angle or value add

### Second Follow-Up Window
- **Best time:** 7-10 days after first follow-up
- **Approach:** Direct question or pivot strategy
- **Goal:** Establish clearer picture

### Third Follow-Up Window
- **Best time:** 14 days after second follow-up
- **Approach:** Provide graceful exit option
- **Goal:** Maintain relationship without being pushy

### Re-engagement Window
- **Best time:** 30+ days later with trigger event
- **Trigger:** News, traction update, market change
- **Goal:** Restart conversation with fresh context

### Day/Time Optimization
- **Best days:** Tuesday-Thursday
- **Avoid:** Monday (email overload), Friday (low open rates)
- **Best times:** 9-11 AM, 3-4 PM (your recipient's timezone)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Sending too many follow-ups too quickly** - respect recipient's time and inbox
2. **Generic follow-ups** - always reference something specific to the conversation
3. **Overselling in follow-ups** - focus on value, not your needs
4. **Ignoring signals** - if someone says no, respect that
5. **Missing key details** - typos, wrong company names, incorrect links erode trust
6. **Abandoning too early** - 3-5 touches across weeks is standard, not aggressive
7. **Unclear asks** - always include a specific, easy next step
8. **Forgetting context** - always remind them who you are and how you met
9. **Hard-selling** - position as exploration, not obligation
10. **Not tracking sequences** - use a CRM or spreadsheet to avoid duplicates/missed followups

## Metrics to Track

For each follow-up sequence, measure:

- **Open rate** - are your subject lines compelling?
- **Response rate** - are you getting replies?
- **Meeting conversion** - how many turn into conversations?
- **Stage advancement** - how many move to next stage?
- **Time to response** - how quickly are people engaging?
- **Sequence completion** - which touch points drive engagement?
