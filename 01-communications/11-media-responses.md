### Template 1: Accepting Interview Request - Executive Interview

Dear [JOURNALIST_NAME],

[COMPANY_REPRESENTATIVE_NAME], our [TITLE], would be delighted to participate in an interview. [REPRESENTATIVE_NAME] brings [RELEVANT_EXPERTISE] and can speak knowledgeably about [TOPIC_AREAS].

**Regarding logistics:**
- **Preferred format:** [PHONE/VIDEO/IN-PERSON]
- **Best times:** [SPECIFIC_DAYS_AND_TIMES]
- **Duration:** [ESTIMATED_LENGTH]
- **Key talking points:** [BRIEF_DESCRIPTION]

We're committed to providing accurate, insightful commentary that adds value to your piece. Please share:
1. Publication timeline and deadline
2. Target audience
3. Key questions or angles you're exploring
4. Any specific metrics or data you'd like us to address

Best regards,
[YOUR_NAME]
[YOUR_TITLE]
[COMPANY_NAME]
[CONTACT_INFORMATION]
