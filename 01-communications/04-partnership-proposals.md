# Partnership Proposal Templates

A comprehensive collection of 25+ partnership proposal templates for MVP funding. Each template includes executive summary, value proposition, terms outline, and next steps.

---

## Part 1: Strategic Partnerships

### Template 1.1: Competitor Co-Development Partnership

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

This strategic partnership proposal seeks to establish a collaborative framework between [Your Company] and [Partner Company] to co-develop complementary solutions in [Industry/Market]. By combining our respective strengths, we aim to create a market-leading offering that neither organization could achieve independently, while sharing development costs and market access.

**VALUE PROPOSITION**

For [Your Company]:
- Access to [Partner's] established customer base of [X] enterprises
- Shared R&D costs reducing development expense by [X]%
- Enhanced product capability through integration of [Partner's] technology
- Accelerated time-to-market by [X] months
- Combined market credibility increasing win probability by [X]%

For [Partner Company]:
- Revenue stream from [X] new customer segments
- Reduced go-to-market costs through joint marketing
- Technology innovation that enhances [Partner's] platform
- Risk mitigation through shared development investment

**TERMS OUTLINE**

- **Partnership Duration**: 3-year initial term with automatic 1-year renewals
- **Development Commitment**: [X] FTE from each party dedicated to co-development
- **Revenue Sharing**: [X]% to [Your Company], [X]% to [Partner Company]
- **IP Ownership**: Joint ownership of co-created IP with licensed access to pre-existing IP
- **Exclusivity**: Non-exclusive arrangement with [specific geographic or vertical restrictions]
- **Quarterly Review**: Performance metrics reviewed quarterly with adjustment rights
- **Governance**: Joint steering committee with monthly meetings
- **Minimum Revenue Guarantee**: [$X] annual minimum or either party may exit

**NEXT STEPS**

1. Schedule 30-minute call with [Partner's] VP Product to discuss technical feasibility (Week 1)
2. Conduct joint market analysis to validate addressable opportunity (Week 2-3)
3. Develop detailed integration roadmap and resource plan (Week 3-4)
4. Legal review and partnership agreement drafting (Week 4-5)
5. Executive sign-off and partnership launch (Week 6)

These 25+ partnership proposal templates provide a comprehensive framework for developing strategic partnerships across seven key categories. By customizing these templates with specific market data, financial projections, and partner-specific benefits, you can develop compelling proposals that drive mutual success.
