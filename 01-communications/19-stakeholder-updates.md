# Stakeholder Update Templates

A comprehensive collection of stakeholder update templates for MVP funding. Includes investor updates (monthly/quarterly), board updates, advisor updates, partner updates, team all-hands, and annual shareholder letters. All templates include KPI reporting, narrative updates, and clear asks. Variables are marked with brackets [LIKE_THIS] for easy customization.

## General Best Practices for Stakeholder Updates

### Format & Distribution:
- **Frequency:** Monthly for investors, Quarterly for board and advisors, Ad-hoc for partners, Monthly for team
- **Format:** Email for lean startups, Slide deck + narrative for growth stage, Full report for public/near-public
- **Distribution:** Secure email, shared document link, or password-protected portal
- **Archive:** Maintain historical updates for board records

### Content Best Practices:
- **Metrics:** Lead with most important metrics; provide YoY/QoQ comparison
- **Narrative:** Balance data with storytelling that explains causality
- **Honesty:** Bad news early; show you're aware and have a plan
- **Asks:** Be specific about what you need; make it easy to say yes

### Common Variables:
- [COMPANY_NAME] = Your company name
- [INVESTOR_NAME] = Individual investor/stakeholder name
- [METRIC_VALUE] = Specific number for key metric
- [PERCENTAGE] = Growth or change percentage
- [ACHIEVEMENT] = Specific company win or milestone
- [TIMELINE] = Specific date or timeframe
- [ASK] = Specific request from stakeholder
- [CHALLENGE] = Current business challenge being navigated

### Customization Tips:

**For Investor Updates:**
1. Know their focus areas and reference them
2. Quantify everything possible
3. Show how you're spending their money
4. Include traction metrics they care about

**For Board Updates:**
1. Include governance/legal items
2. Present options when needing decisions
3. Be thorough but concise
4. Flag material risks/opportunities

**For Advisor Updates:**
1. Ask specific questions
2. Make it easy to help
3. Show you're taking their advice
4. Keep them feeling valued

**For Partner Updates:**
1. Highlight mutual wins
2. Show business impact of partnership
3. Have clear asks for next period
4. Create accountability for results

**For Team All-Hands:**
1. Be transparent and authentic
2. Connect to company mission
3. Celebrate people, not just numbers
4. Acknowledge challenges honestly

**For Shareholder Letters:**
1. Balance business update with personal reflection
2. Acknowledge major challenges/learnings
3. Paint compelling vision for future
4. Show gratitude to various stakeholders
