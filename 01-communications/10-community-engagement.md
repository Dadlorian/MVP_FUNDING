## Community Guidelines

### 28. Community Code of Conduct

**Subject:** [Product] Community Code of Conduct

Our community is built on respect, collaboration, and constructive dialogue. To maintain a welcoming environment for everyone, we ask that all community members follow these guidelines:

**Our Values:**
1. **Respect**: Treat all community members with courtesy and consideration
2. **Inclusivity**: Welcome members of all backgrounds and perspectives
3. **Constructiveness**: Contribute meaningfully and offer solutions, not just criticism
4. **Professionalism**: Keep conversations work-appropriate and focused
5. **Safety**: No discrimination, harassment, or illegal content

**Specific Guidelines:**
- No hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks
- No spam, promotion of competing products, or excessive self-promotion
- Keep discussions on-topic and relevant
- No sharing of confidential or proprietary information
- Respect others' intellectual property
- Help others while being honest about limitations

**If You Witness a Violation:**
Please report it to [moderators/support team]: [email] or [report form]

**Consequences:**
- First violation: Warning
- Second violation: Temporary suspension
- Repeated violations: Permanent removal

**Questions?** See our FAQ or email [email].

### 29. Community Moderation Policy

**Subject:** How We Moderate [Community Platform]

We want [product name] community to be a safe and productive space. Here's how we keep it that way:

**Our Moderation Approach:**
- We aim for transparency and fairness
- We give warnings before taking action when possible
- We involve community members in creating community standards
- We review decisions when appealed

**What Gets Moderated:**
- Spam and self-promotion
- Off-topic discussions (redirected, not removed)
- Harassment and personal attacks
- Discrimination or hate speech
- Confidential information sharing
- Illegal content

**What Doesn't Get Moderated:**
- Different opinions on [product] features
- Constructive criticism
- Disagreement about best practices
- Recommendations of tools or services relevant to discussions

**Appeal Process:**
If you believe content was unfairly moderated, email [moderation@company.com] with:
- Your name and username
- The post ID or content
- Why you believe it should be reinstated

**Our Team:**
Meet the moderators: [bios and contact info]

---

### 30. Anti-Spam Policy & Expectations

**Subject:** Keeping Our Community Clean: Anti-Spam Guidelines

We work hard to keep spam out of our community spaces. Here's what we consider spam and how to avoid it:

**What We Consider Spam:**
- Repeated identical or near-identical posts
- Posts with excessive links or promotional content
- Paid promotion disguised as genuine discussion
- Unrelated commercial offers
- Bot-generated content

**What's Allowed:**
- ✓ Sharing your side project or creation (once, genuinely)
- ✓ Linking to relevant resources that help solve a problem
- ✓ Mentioning a service you use and recommend
- ✓ Announcing events or news relevant to the community
- ✓ Self-promotion in designated [#self-promotion] section

**Consequences:**
- First offense: Post removal and warning
- Second offense: [Post removal and account restriction]
- Third offense: Temporary suspension
- Patterns: Permanent ban

**How to Report Spam:**
Use the [report] button on any post or email [moderation@company.com]

**Questions?** See our moderation FAQs or ask in [#meta-discussion].

- Include relevant links to your platform, forms, or resources
- Adapt formality level based on your community culture

**Best Practices:**
- Keep messages concise and scannable with clear headers
- Include a call-to-action in most communications
- Use positive, inclusive language
- Respond to community feedback and adapt templates accordingly
- Monitor engagement metrics and refine based on what resonates
- Always proofread before posting
- Consider timing and frequency to avoid overwhelming your community

**Measurement & Iteration:**
- Track which types of posts get highest engagement
- Gather feedback on communication preferences
- Test different formats and lengths
- Adjust templates based on community response
- Document what works for future reference
