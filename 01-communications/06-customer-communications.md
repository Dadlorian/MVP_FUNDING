# Customer Communication Templates

A comprehensive collection of 40+ email and message templates for customer lifecycle management and engagement. These templates can be customized for your specific product/service, brand voice, and audience.

## Tips for Using These Templates

### Customization Best Practices:
1. **Replace All Placeholders**: Personalize [First Name], [Product Name], [Amount], dates, and metrics
2. **Match Your Brand Voice**: Adjust tone, language, and terminology to reflect your brand personality
3. **Include Clear CTAs**: Every email should have 1-2 primary calls-to-action
4. **Add Relevant Links**: Ensure all links are tested and up-to-date
5. **Consider Segmentation**: Different customer segments may need different messaging
6. **Test Before Sending**: Always test personalization, links, and formatting

### Timing Guidelines:
- **Welcome Series**: Email 1 (day 1), Email 2 (day 2), Email 3 (day 3), Email 4 (day 5), Email 5 (day 7)
- **Renewal Reminders**: 30 days, 14 days, 7 days, and 1 day before
- **Disruption Notices**: Initial report within 15 min, updates every 30 min, resolution email within 1 hour of fix
- **Reactivation Series**: 1-2 weeks after cancellation, then every 2-4 weeks for 2-3 months
- **Upsell Campaigns**: After 2-4 weeks of usage (once they see value), then quarterly

### Metrics to Track:
- **Open Rate**: Target 25-40% for transactional emails, 15-25% for marketing
- **Click Rate**: Target 3-8% for emails with primary CTAs
- **Conversion Rate**: Varies by template type; track your specific goals
- **Unsubscribe Rate**: Keep below 0.5% by sending relevant, valuable content
- **Bounce Rate**: Keep below 2% by maintaining clean email lists

### Legal/Compliance:
- Include unsubscribe option in marketing emails
- Honor all GDPR, CCPA, and CAN-SPAM requirements
- Get explicit consent before adding customers to email lists
- Include company address in footer
- Honor preference centers and frequency caps

## Additional Resources

**Email Design Best Practices**:
- Use single-column layouts for mobile optimization
- Keep emails under 600px width
- Include alt text for images
- Use a clear hierarchy with headers
- Test across email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, etc.)

**Content Best Practices**:
- Write compelling subject lines (50 characters or less)
- Front-load important information
- Use short paragraphs and bullets for scanability
- Include specific numbers/metrics when possible
- Always focus on customer benefit, not features
- End with clear next steps

**Frequency Guidelines**:
- Onboarding: Daily for first week, then taper
- Newsletters: Weekly or bi-weekly (let customers choose)
- Product Updates: Varies by pace of releases
- Promotional: 2-4 per month max
- Transactional: As needed (renewal, billing, etc.)
