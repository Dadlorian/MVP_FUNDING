## Cold Outreach Emails

### Template 1: Classic Cold Outreach

**Subject:** [COMPANY_NAME] - Solving [PROBLEM] for [TARGET_MARKET]

Dear [INVESTOR_NAME],

I hope this message finds you well. I'm reaching out because your track record of investing in [INDUSTRY] companies particularly resonates with what we're building at [COMPANY_NAME].

We've developed a solution to [PROBLEM_DESCRIPTION] that is currently enabling [TARGET_MARKET] to [DESIRED_OUTCOME]. Early traction shows [KEY_METRIC]: [METRIC_VALUE].

I'd love to share more about our vision and hear your thoughts. Would you be open to a brief 20-minute call next week?

Best regards,
[YOUR_NAME]
[YOUR_TITLE]
[COMPANY_NAME]
[CONTACT_INFORMATION]

**CTA:** Would you have 20 minutes for a call next [DAY] or [DAY]?

## Email Best Practices

### General Tips:
- **Subject lines:** Keep under 50 characters when possible; use action words and specificity
- **Personalization:** Always reference something specific about the investor or their portfolio
- **Brevity:** Respect investor time - aim for 3-5 paragraph maximum
- **Mobile:** Ensure emails render well on mobile devices
- **Call-to-action:** Make next steps crystal clear

### Variable Key:
- [COMPANY_NAME] = Your company name
- [INVESTOR_NAME] = Name of investor you're reaching out to
- [YOUR_NAME] = Your full name
- [FUNDING_AMOUNT] = Dollar amount you're raising
- [TARGET_MARKET] = Your customer segment
- [METRIC_VALUE] = Relevant traction metric
- [PROBLEM] = The core problem you solve
- [SOLUTION_TYPE] = Type of solution you offer
- [INDUSTRY] = Industry vertical
- [TIMEFRAME] = Specific dates or quarters
- [MILESTONE] = Important company achievement
- [INVESTMENT_FOCUS] = Investor's focus area
- [FOUNDER_BACKGROUND] = Your relevant experience
- [UNIQUE_DIFFERENTIATOR] = What makes you different
- [COMPETITIVE_ADVANTAGE] = Why you win vs. competitors

### Tips for Customization:
1. **Research deeply:** Mention specific companies in their portfolio, recent news, or public statements
2. **Show relevance:** Explain why THIS investor is right for YOUR company
3. **Be authentic:** Use your voice - investors can tell when emails are templated
4. **Quantify everything:** Use specific numbers instead of vague claims
5. **Tell a story:** Help investors understand your passion and vision
6. **Follow up:** 70% of deals require multiple touches before getting a meeting

### Timing Considerations:
- **Cold emails:** Tuesday-Thursday are best; avoid Mondays and Fridays
- **Follow-ups:** 3-7 days after first contact; then 10-14 days for second follow-up
- **Best time of day:** 6-9 AM or 5-7 PM
- **Quarter-end:** Avoid month-ends and quarter-ends when investors are busy with reviews
